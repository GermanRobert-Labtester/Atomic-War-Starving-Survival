## 0. Prologue — The Harrow

> *"Something is still working up there. It is not aiming. It does not have to."*

There is no enemy in the sky. There is a **process** — an inventory of spent hardware in decaying
orbits, still obeying schedules written before the schedules' authors stopped existing. The
Harrow is what a war looks like forty years after anybody fought it: thermodynamics with a
timetable.

The roof is the shelter's answer to arithmetic. Concrete, steel, sandbags, a canopy bought on
terms. Every column has a condition and every condition has a day. The countdown board is not a
warning system. It is a *ledger of what has already been decided elsewhere*, arriving on a delay.

**Tone & register.** Clinical, meteorological, unsentimental. The vocabulary is the engineering
office: *cell, thickness, degradation rate, lead time, absorbed, breached, penetrated*. Never
anthropomorphise the sky and never dramatise an impact. The most frightening line in this plan is
a two-day-old number on a board.

**Mystery & texture.** Olympus remains far-horizon context (E13) and is never a schedule source
(DEC-SK-04). That boundary is the plan's central silence: there is a *bigger* thing recorded in
the instrument telemetry, spanning 5,110 days and 84,000 MJ, and it is not coming for you.
The twelve debris events are merely what is left. §12 keeps the difference permanent.

## 1. Goal & Outcome

> *Design intent: a roof you can see the wear on. Every repair is a bet against a date you have
> been shown and cannot change.*

- **Goal:** (a) a **Roof** the player can install and repair from the authored catalogue; (b) **storm load** that wears ceiling columns by the catalogue's own degradation rates, carved out of the existing resilience hit; (c) **breach** states with consequences through existing owners; (d) an authored, profile-aware **Countdown** of the 12 existing orbital events, one pending pass at a time, ending in a terminal pass; (e) **Impact Day** answers (paid Brace, clear the top, intercept) and an **aftermath** (salvage, revealed site, radio line).
- **Outcome (observable):** on a fixed seed, a shelter with an installed concrete column and a scheduled Phase-II pass sees: a warning with the catalogue's lead time; storm wear reduce that column while resilience drops by the *remainder*; a paid Brace halve the impact; a breached neighbouring column expose the rooms beneath through air/stress/power owners; a false alarm fizzle with no salvage; save/load mid-countdown is exact.
- **Non-Goals:** no rework of Sky Defense; no Olympus compression into the campaign; no new damage number; no injuries by default; no new save section; no new routed panel; no change to `weather_hardening` upgrades; no Unity.
- **"Done":** §6 acceptance passes via `bin/run-scoped-tests`; ship-dark parity holds; handoff lists untouched shared paths.

## 1b. Texture, Mystery & Voice

**Confidence is a mood, not a number.**

DEC-SK-07 grades a track *Unconfirmed → Probable → Confirmed*. Make that grade read like weather
reporting from a station that is not sure it is still receiving. The grade is presentation only —
but it is the whole emotional payload of the countdown board. A track that stays *Unconfirmed* for
eight days and then resolves as a false alarm should feel like a held breath released, not a
failure state.

**The catalogue disagrees with the evaluator (E8b).**

Do not quietly fix this. The four disagreeing rows — scrap overlay 12 vs 2 MJ, steel hull 45 vs 18,
canopy 8 vs 1.2, sandbag 5 vs 4 — are exactly the kind of discrepancy a shelter would discover
about its own paperwork. DEC-SK-03 makes the printed value the installed value. Let the *panel*
show the disagreement as a note. A shelter whose catalogue lies to it is a shelter that has been
alive too long.

**What the player is never told.**

- Why `is_false_positive` exists in the catalogue at all. Two of twelve events are designed to
  waste your attention. Nobody wrote down why there are two.
- Whether the terminal pass is the last one. "Ending in a terminal pass" describes a schedule,
  not a promise.
- What `penetration_power_mj` measures against that the shelter does not have.
- Whether the revealed excavation sites are the *same* kind of thing as the debris.

**Voice — sample fragments (content candidates for `sky_countdown_texts.json`).**

> "Track 4 is Unconfirmed and has been for six days. Unconfirmed is not safe. Unconfirmed is only
> unsaid."

> "Storm wear: column C-7 to Worn. Resilience takes the remainder. The remainder is the part nobody
> budgets for."

> "Impact Day. Brace paid. Absorbed. The top of the shelter is now a different shape than it was
> at breakfast."

> "False alarm. No salvage. We stood in the corridor for nothing, which is the best outcome on the
> board."

**Design texture beats.**

- **One pending pass at a time is the entire tension.** Do not add queueing. A single slot means a
  single worry, and a single worry is one the player can carry for ten days.
- **The carve-out must be invisible in totals (DEC-SK-02).** Roof wear and resilience share one
  budget. If the player can detect the split in the arithmetic, the fiction breaks.
- **Breach exposes rooms beneath.** Air, positive-only stress, a repair job with a due day. Three
  small facts, no drama. The due day is what hurts.
- **A false alarm yields nothing (DEC-SK-06).** Give it the plainest line in the game. Relief
  should feel like being let go, not like being cheated.

### SK-P1 — Roof ledger (Core + data)
- `RoofLedger` per column: `patchedUntilDay`, `lastLoadDay`, `breached` flag, small wear log; nested additive fields; four states from condition (Sound/Worn/Failing/Breached) via thresholds in data; one additive `ApplyLoad(gridX, wear)` on the armour owner.
- **Accept:** round-trip; old saves load; ship dark with no columns; `ApplyLoad` never raises condition.

### SK-P2 — Install and repair commands (host)
- Read the catalogue through the existing loader; `composition` / `repair_cost` as atomic inventory transactions; install stores an optional per-cell blast-resistance override (DEC-SK-03); the Emergency Blast Canopy is the stopgap.
- **Accept:** conservation; a failed cost leaves no cell; cells without override keep today's arithmetic exactly.

### SK-P3 — Storm load carve-out (host, `INT`)
- In the cascade host's shelter-damage route, split the magnitude by a data share: `magnitude × share × degradation_rate` to `ApplyLoad`, the *remainder* to `AdjustResilience`; no roof plan → today's behaviour.
- **Accept:** total pressure identical with and without a plan; parity test on both roof-relevant rows.

### SK-P4 — Breach effects (host adapters)
- Failing → air-purity input + damp signal; Breached → rooms beneath exposed (air, positive-only stress, repair job due day); impact → existing power surge unchanged.
- **Accept:** each effect calls exactly one public owner method; no health write; no stock write.

### SK-P5 — Countdown (Core + data + host daily hook)
- `SkyCountdown` reads rows per profile/branch; on the warning day it refuses while `HasPendingImpact`, else calls `ScheduleEventDef` (lead time from `lead_time_days`); target column from a `CampaignStreamIds` fork keyed `(day, eventId)`; false alarms as fizzles.
- **Accept:** determinism; never two pending; a profile change changes days not rules; ship dark with no rows.

### SK-P6 — Impact Day (host)
- Paid Brace wrapper (atomic consume then `Brace`); clear-the-top affordance (advisory, no forced moves); intercept via the existing battery; outcome grades Absorbed / Breached / Penetrated with authored lines.
- **Accept:** Brace cost is atomic and once per pass; no injury path.

### SK-P7 — Aftermath (host + data)
- Salvage claim (existing), revealed excavation site (existing), `radio_hook_text` and `sky_countdown_texts.json` through the existing radio/journal surfaces.
- **Accept:** each unread catalogue field has exactly one reader; a false alarm yields no salvage.

## 6. Acceptance criteria
1. Core authority + host owner + event path + persistence + observable outcome agree (CLAUDE.md "integrated").
2. Ship-dark parity: no roof columns installed and no countdown rows → armour, telemetry, cascade, resilience, power and Sky Defense outputs identical on a saved corpus.
3. Conservation: install, repair and brace costs balance; a failed cost creates no cell and no brace.
4. Carve-out parity: total storm pressure (resilience + roof wear share) equals today's resilience hit.
5. Determinism: identical target columns, pass days and outcomes on replay (`CampaignStreamIds` fork; never `System.Random`).
6. Save round-trip mid-countdown and mid-repair; old saves load.
7. One pending pass at a time; false alarms fizzle; no injuries by default.
8. The catalogue-vs-evaluator conflict resolved by decision, not by silent edit.
