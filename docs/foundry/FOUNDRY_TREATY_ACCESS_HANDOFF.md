# Foundry Treaty Access Handoff

The consequence schema has no access flag or route mutator. Access remains
owned by existing stance, expedition, and facility systems. `standing_delta`
may change the Foundry trade stance after a live assessment, but a policy row
does not silently open or seal a route.

## Authored intent

| Treaty outcome | Intended institutional consequence | Live data effect |
|---|---|---|
| Saltworks met | measured pipe-walk priority retained | standing + market relief |
| Saltworks violated | priority review and inspection | standing loss + market pressure |
| Coal missed | next safe haul slot lost | standing loss + coal/fuel pressure |
| Crisis violated | emergency cost-recovery review | standing loss + water/fuel pressure |
| Incident Book met | renewal record accepted | standing gain only |

These are narrative handoff terms, not unsupported access flags. A future
typed access policy may consume the stable treaty/outcome record. No critical
progression path is soft-locked by this data-only pass.
