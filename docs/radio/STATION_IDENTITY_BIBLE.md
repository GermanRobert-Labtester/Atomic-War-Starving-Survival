# Station Identity Bible

> **Document Status:** Authoritative Station Canonical Guide
> **Authority:** Plan 24 (Task 24B)
> **Number of Canonical Stations:** 6 Primary Broadcasters

---

## 1. Station 1: Central Civil Defense & Public Service (`station_civil_defense`)

- **Canonical Name:** Central Civil Defense Radio (Emergency Service 88.5)
- **Primary Frequency:** `88.50 MHz` (VHF Band II)
- **Owner / Source:** Remnant Municipal Emergency Directorate & Civil Meteorological Bureau
- **Persona / Voice:** Formal, measured, clinical mid-century public broadcaster. Unflappable cadence under crisis; chimes mark half-hours.
- **Reliability:** `Official` (High factual accuracy on weather and sanitation protocols; sanitized regarding military losses).
- **Typical Programming:**
  - Morning Meteorological Briefings (06:00)
  - Water Treatment & Sanitation Directives
  - Iodine Dosage Schedules & Shelter Filtration Maintenance
  - Lost-and-Found Person Inquiries & Missing Civilian Lists
  - Public Health Quarantine Bulletins
- **Political Agenda:** Civil order preservation, prevention of panic, centralized compliance.
- **Campaign State Behavior:**
  - *Early Game (Days 1–30):* Continuous public service announcements and routine bulletins.
  - *Mid Game (Days 31–180):* Deteriorating signal quality, reduced schedule, emergency loops.
  - *Late Game / Verdict (Days 181+):* Channel hijacked or intermittently overridden by the Office of Censuses.

## 2. Station 2: Iron Garrison Command / Overlord Actual (`station_garrison_overlord`)

- **Canonical Name:** Iron Garrison Military Net (Overlord Actual / 9th Battalion)
- **Primary Frequency:** `88.40 MHz` (adjacent to Civil Defense, slight receiver bleed)
- **Owner / Source:** Central Garrison Continuity Office / Military Remnants
- **Persona / Voice:** Barked military protocol, clipped phonetic triads, background diesel generator rattle and tactical squelch.
- **Reliability:** `Partisan` (Exaggerates military readiness; downplays supply shortages; reports logistics decrees as absolute law).
- **Typical Programming:**
  - Martial Decrees & Curfew Schedules
  - Armory Serial Seizure Notices & Contraband Confiscation
  - Convoy Escort Timetables & Checkpoint Road Closures
  - Warlord & Raider Deterrence Warnings
  - Ceasefire & Parley Terms
- **Political Agenda:** Garrison hegemony, forced resource requisitioning, military legitimacy.
- **State Transition / Silence:**
  - If Garrison logistics collapse or HQ falls: transitions to continuous automated Dead Hand repeater (`station_null_carrier`) or static silence.

## 3. Station 3: Voice of the Vitrified Crater (`station_vitrified_crater`)

- **Canonical Name:** Voice of the Vitrified Crater / The Ash Pulpit
- **Primary Frequency:** `104.20 MHz`
- **Owner / Source:** Cult of the Ash Sign & Crater Zealots
- **Persona / Voice:** Deep, echoing, liturgical chant; resonant pauses; no background mechanical noise (recorded in deep sealed vaults).
- **Reliability:** `Partisan / Ideological` (Metaphorical and ominous; remarkably accurate regarding radioactive fallout movement, highly unreliable on human intent).
- **Typical Programming:**
  - The Liturgy of the Black Water
  - Sermon on the Virtue of Waiting
  - Prophecies of the Deep Thaw
  - Denunciations of Bunker Machine Cults
- **Political Agenda:** Spiritual acceptance of nuclear catastrophe as divine cleansing; rejection of old-world technology.
- **State Transition:** Becomes louder and more desperate during severe radioactive fallout storms.

## 4. Station 4: The Open Airwaves / Civilian Free Radio (`station_open_classroom`)

- **Canonical Name:** The Open Airwaves (The Open Classroom & Lineman's Loop)
- **Primary Frequency:** `91.30 MHz` (Education) & `142.50 MHz` (Lineman Infrastructure)
- **Owner / Source:** Independent survivor teachers, scavengers, and infrastructure repairers (e.g. Ottilie Frayne, Lineman Pavel).
- **Persona / Voice:** Warm, human, tired, pragmatic. Chalk on blackboard taps, tool rattles, wind whistling in antenna masts.
- **Reliability:** `Anonymous / Civilian` (Honest human eyewitness reports; limited scope; occasionally misinformed on regional military moves).
- **Typical Programming:**
  - Lessons on Air (Water cycle, radiation fractions, seed preservation)
  - Lineman Grid Status (Copper theft reports, tower collapses, wire barter)
  - Regional Caravan Arrivals & Market Prices
  - Community Barter Appeals (Boiler parts, glycol antifreeze, seed potatoes)
- **Political Agenda:** Mutual aid, survival education, communal autonomy.
- **State Transition:** Silenced if local community hubs are destroyed or raided.

## 5. Station 5: Unidentified Transmitters & Numbers Stations (`station_numbers_sigint`)

- **Canonical Name:** Unidentified Shortwave Transmitters (The Dead Buzzer / Rhapsody Orphan / Poacher Echo)
- **Primary Frequencies:** `7.325 MHz`, `14.487 MHz`, `19.800 MHz`, `4.625 MHz`
- **Owner / Source:** Pre-war automated military intelligence arrays, clandestine dead drops, unmonitored RTG repeaters.
- **Persona / Voice:** Synthesized female voice, metallic music box chimes, corrupted tape flutter, monotone electronic buzzers.
- **Reliability:** `Automated / Exact` (Carries raw encrypted mathematical data; zero editorial commentary).
- **Typical Programming:**
  - Five-digit phonetic number groups
  - Mechanical clockwork chime intervals
  - Coded coordinate carrier bursts (Plan 11B Cipher Quests)
  - Seismic array fault telemetry
- **Downstream Consequence:** Directly feeds `CipherQuestChainEngine.cs` and `SignalIntelligenceCatalog.cs`.
- **State Transition:** Goes completely silent once the corresponding bunker or cache is breached and resolved.

## 6. Station 6: Meteorological & Emergency Beacon Array (`station_automated_relay`)

- **Canonical Name:** Automated Emergency Beacon Array (Maritime 142.85 & Span 96.10)
- **Primary Frequencies:** `142.850 MHz` & `96.100 MHz`
- **Owner / Source:** Pre-war automated navigational repeaters, icebreaker beacons, bridge-mining defense computers.
- **Persona / Voice:** Synthetic robotic speech, carrier tone pulses, continuous teletype chatter.
- **Reliability:** `Automated` (100% objective sensor readings; unyielding mechanical warnings).
- **Typical Programming:**
  - Icebreaker *Aurora Borealis* evacuation corridor count
  - Radon-222 infiltration alerts & permafrost thaw warnings
  - Detachment 9 bridge demolition countdown loops
  - Atmospheric EMP sniffer readouts
- **Political Agenda:** None (programmed machinery running until component failure).
- **State Transition:** Disarmed or terminated upon player interaction at terminal nodes.
