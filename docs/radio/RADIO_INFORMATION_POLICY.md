# Radio Information Policy

> **Document Status:** Authoritative Information Classification
> **Authority:** Plan 24 (Task 24AR)

---

## 1. Information Tiers & Scope

To maintain worldbuilding plausibility and prevent accidental meta-knowledge leaks, all broadcast content adheres to strict source knowledge constraints:

| Information Tier | Allowed Broadcasters | Prohibited Content | Plausibility Rules |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Public Waste News** | Civil Defense, Open Classroom, Works | Secret faction caches, private shelter inventory | Broadcasters observe surface conditions, weather, and open market trading. |
| **Faction Partisan Claims** | Garrison, Cult, Hydro-Barons | Internal enemy casualties, accurate enemy troop counts | Factions claim absolute control, exaggerate enemy losses, and conceal their own supply deficits. |
| **Tactical Intercepts** | Sentry radios, patrol squads | High-level political treaties | Tactical squads speak in urgent, localized terms (coordinates, ammo counts, perimeter breaches). |
| **Signal Intelligence** | Clandestine numbers stations | Human names / editorial opinions | Raw cipher strings, mechanical chimes, automated fault telemetry. |
| **Private Shelter State** | **NONE** | Shelter food count, survivor names (unless announced by player) | External radio cannot know what happens behind sealed bunker blast doors. |
