## F — QC notes against CONSISTENCY_ANCHORS A01

The imported set holds the locked anchor well: flat opaque black field, centered three-quarter framing, top-left key, dry-gouache material blocks, one wear story per object. Specific findings:

- **Readable-text violation (A01 'Text'):** unidentified #7 has gibberish lettering (`AOCHIL`) on a can label. Must be painted out.
- **Drop shadow (A01 'Background: no drop shadow'):** a contact shadow is visible on `epi_pen`, `thermometer`, `hand_crank_radio`, `car_battery`, `improvised_water_boiler` and `improvised_herb_garden`. Minor, but it breaks the flat-black rule and should be removed in paintover.
- **Colour outside the locked palette:** `screwdriver` has a saturated red handle, `wire_cutters` red grips, `pliers` blue grips, `epi_pen`/`thermometer` yellow bodies. Defensible as functional colour-coding, but they read hotter than the 'restrained' palette — worth a desaturation pass.
