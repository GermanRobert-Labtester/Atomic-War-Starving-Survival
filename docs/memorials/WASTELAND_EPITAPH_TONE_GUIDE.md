# Wasteland Grave Epitaphs — Tone Guide

**Pillar:** Environmental Storytelling & Memorial Atmosphere
**Setting:** Ruined post-nuclear wasteland, bitter scarcity, collective trauma, physical labor.

---

## 1. Core Writing Philosophy

A grave marker in ASHFALL is not a eulogy, an obituary, or a moral treatise. It is a hasty, physical mark left behind by exhausted survivors who had limited time, crude tools (a nail, a knife, scrap tin, paint, charcoal, chisel), and heavy grief.

The inscription should convey **one specific, grounded circumstance** about how the person died or what remained of them at the end.

---

## 2. Mandatory Tone Attributes

- **Grounded & Physical:** Anchor lines in real artifacts and physical conditions (dosimeter films, rusted culverts, damp wool, pump casings, notched wooden beams, cold pipework, gravel pits, brass buttons).
- **Concise & Restrained:** Exactly one clean sentence. No purple prose, no ornate vocabulary, no melodramatic hyperbole.
- **Unsentimental:** Let the bare facts carry the emotional weight. Avoid telling the reader how to feel.
- **Survivor-Carved Perspective:** Words written by someone who had to dig the hole, carry the body, or clean the gear.

---

## 3. Explicit Prohibitions

| Pattern to Avoid | Rationale | Approved Alternative |
|---|---|---|
| "Died a hero" / "Saved us all" | Romanticizing martyrdom | "Three people made it through the culvert gate because she stayed behind to draw the fire." |
| "The cold found them" / "The road took her" | Repetitive sentimental clichés | "The wind tore her tarp away while she was searching for the road marker in the dark." |
| "They were finally free" / "Chose peace" | Endorsement/romanticization of suicide | "We carved her name where she used to sit because no one found words for the rest." |
| Graphic violence / gory medical detail | Shock-value melodrama | "The winch cable snapped during the generator hoist and left no time for anyone to yell." |
| Chemical recipes / poison procedures | Actionable hazardous instructions | "Drank from the condensate drip behind the transformer vault before the test strip turned bright purple." |
| Real-world countries/alliances | Invariant / World lore rules | Fictionalized wasteland terminology, local geography. |

---

## 4. Length & Punctuation Guardrails

- **Sentence Count:** Exactly 1 sentence per new entry.
- **Word Budget:** 5 to 20 words.
- **Punctuation:** A single terminal period. No semicolon chains masquerading as single sentences.
