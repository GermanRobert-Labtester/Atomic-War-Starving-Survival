# Standing Record Depth Audit (Expansion 03)

## 1. Subsystem Coverage

The Standing Record represents the archaeological, uncertain, and memory-driven investigation of historical sites.

### Key Metrics
- **Memories:** 52 strata memories across 14 sites (pre, after, now strata).
- **Quests:** 22 quests (10 core, 6 forensic reconstructions, 4 layout recoveries, 2 memorials).
- **Layouts:** 14 site layouts with 74 fully inspectable and navigable rooms.
- **Systems:** `LocationLayoutSystem`, `LocationMemorySystem`, `SiteEncounterSystem`, `MemorialSystem`.

## 2. Deepened Mechanics
- **Forensic Archaeology:** Blasted vault mutiny timelines, derailed subway train signal sabotage, collapsed coal mine survivor tokens, and charred archive directives.
- **Layout Discovery:** Hidden utility annexes, HVAC crawlway bypasses, flooded brine cellars, and gas/power schematic alignment.
- **Memorialization:** Granite chisel inscriptions on Railway Span 44 and mountain cairn beacons at Summit Relay 9.
