## 1. Expansion thesis

The Scientific Emergency Beacon is a four-day automated transmission from an observatory whose data integrity is compromised. It reports lost telescope alignment, corrupted atmospheric readings, a failing generator, and pre-war astronomical, atmospheric, and geological surveys stored on magnetic media. The final message says the archive will degrade within ninety days of power loss near the summit entrance. This bank treats the archive as a relationship between fragile material and future readers, without turning retrieval into a route or research quest.

## 2. Story question

When an observatory asks strangers to save data before its generator fails, what does an archive owe the people who cannot preserve it?
