## 1. Expansion thesis

The Match That Counts Down expands the optional birthday ritual about a recorded birthday, one wooden match, darkness, communal silence, a meager flame, blistered fingers, and memory of the sun. The plan deepens celebration and discomfort without adding birthday records, fire handling, injury mechanics, morale systems, or ritual enforcement.

## 2. Story question

When one wooden match is permitted in a pitch-black dormitory and its flame burns toward a celebrant’s fingers, how can a brief light remember the sun without becoming a promise?
