**02 — material attention.** The strongest details in this bank are physical: a threshold, a surface, a line of sight, a document, a marker, a schedule, or a room that still has its old shape. Keep those details ordinary enough to be believed and specific enough to distinguish The Bright Hooks from another location.

**03 — voice separation.** The three roles in this plan do different work. One notices the immediate object, one protects the record’s limits, and one carries the emotional consequence. Do not let all three speak in the same polished register or give one person the authority of the whole The Bright Hooks record.

**04 — player space.** The player is allowed to observe before deciding what the observation means. A pause, refusal, question, or incomplete answer can be the passage’s consequence. **12 — emotional movement.** The movement through The Bright Hooks should be felt as a change in attention. A selected passage may mention room_fixture_clinic_curtain_wire/the_partition_we_have by its display name, by a local nickname already present in the source, or by an ordinary physical detail. Do not add a second identifier merely to make the prose sound larger.

Keep proposed objects portable in meaning but bounded in authority. A page can be carried, a marker can be seen, and a sentence can be repeated without creating an item registry, a universal rule, or a new save section.

Let silence have more than one cause in the prose: respect, fatigue, uncertainty, refusal, distance, or the simple fact that the current record ends. Never use silence as permission to invent a secret.

Where a source names a hazard, write the people’s relationship to that hazard rather than a method for defeating it. Fear, caution, grief, and disagreement are content; operational guidance belongs to an authorized system owner, not this bank.

The document’s length does not widen the canon. It gives the writer room to choose among small moments at The Bright Hooks while keeping the source boundary visible in every form. A longer bank is useful only when its alternatives remain honest and distinct.

A final reading should sound like a person who has lived with the place, not like a catalog that has learned to emote. Keep the nouns concrete, keep the claims attributed, and let the last sentence leave a little air around the player’s choice.

The last editorial pass should ask whether the passage could belong to someone standing near The Bright Hooks with limited time, imperfect memory, and no need to impress a reviewer. If the answer is yes, the bank has done its work: it has made a place available for play without pretending the place belongs to the plan.
