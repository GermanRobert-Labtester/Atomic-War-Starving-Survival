**01 — source fidelity.** The anchor remains wiretap_courier_guild_route_collapse_briefing (The Seventeen Sealed Letters). **02 — material attention.** The strongest details in this bank are physical: a boundary, a surface, a line of sight, a document, a marker, a schedule, or a room that still has its old shape. Keep those details ordinary enough to be believed and specific enough to distinguish The Seventeen Sealed Letters from another location.

**03 — voice separation.** The three roles in this plan do different work. One notices the immediate object, one protects the record’s limits, and one carries the emotional consequence.
