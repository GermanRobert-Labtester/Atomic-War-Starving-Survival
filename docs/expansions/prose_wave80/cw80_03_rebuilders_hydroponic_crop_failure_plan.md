## 1. Expansion thesis

The Seventy-Percent Silence expands a local wiretap between Mira and Holt about root-rot in recirculating nutrient loops, a forty-eight-hour crop loss, and the fear that dormitories will raid flour stores. When root-rot takes seventy percent of Tier 2 seed potatoes and a director orders silence before the dormitories storm the flour silos, what does secrecy protect and who pays for it?

**01 — source fidelity.** The anchor remains wiretap_rebuilders_hydroponic_crop_failure (The Seventy-Percent Silence). Every proposed sentence must stay attached to the cited local record, and every inference must remain visibly an inference. The source can support a human moment without supporting a new mechanic, route, authority, or outcome.

**02 — material attention.** The strongest details in this bank are physical: a boundary, a surface, a line of sight, a document, a marker, a schedule, or a room that still has its old shape. Keep those details ordinary enough to be believed and specific enough to distinguish The Seventy-Percent Silence from another location.

**03 — voice separation.** The three roles in this plan do different work. One notices the immediate object, one protects the record’s limits, and one carries the emotional consequence. Do not let all three speak in the same polished register or give one person the authority of the whole The Seventy-Percent Silence record.

**04 — player space.** The player is allowed to observe before deciding what the observation means. A pause, refusal, question, or incomplete answer can be the passage’s consequence. Do not turn every emotional beat into a prompt, reward, relationship delta, journal flag, or measurable improvement.

**05 — return scale.** A return to The Seventy-Percent Silence should make the earlier passage newly legible, not rewrite it. **06 — uncertainty.** Uncertainty is authored content here. It is not a hole that needs to be filled with a hidden culprit, a technical explanation, a secret route, or an invented survivor. **07 — dignity.** People associated with The Seventy-Percent Silence should not be reduced to the hazard, policy, machine, or record that surrounds them. **08 — content scale.** The 48-beat bank is deliberately editorial. A production owner may select one passage, several passages, or none. The size of this document is a writing resource, not a request to implement every candidate or to create a parallel narrative registry.

**09 — continuity check.** Before placement, compare this bank against newer data, especially records that mention wiretap_rebuilders_hydroponic_crop_failure or the surrounding region. **11 — diegetic surface.** A record, notice, conversation, or scene should look like something a survivor could encounter in the existing content owner. **12 — emotional movement.** The movement through The Seventy-Percent Silence should be felt as a change in attention. The player begins by seeing an object or condition, then hears a person attach meaning to it, then decides whether to carry that meaning forward. **13 — open ending.** Close on a human image rather than a verdict. A hand lowers, a page remains folded, a name is repeated once, a room continues its ordinary work, or a landmark stays where it was. The ending should leave room for the player and the current canon to do the next piece of work.

**14 — handoff.** If a content owner accepts a passage, preserve its attribution and keep the owner’s existing state, save, and routing seams. The handoff is complete only when the selected text, its source record, its trigger, and its audience all agree. Otherwise this remains editorial prose for review.
