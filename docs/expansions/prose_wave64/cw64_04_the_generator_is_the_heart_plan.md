# EXPANSION CW64-04 — The Generator Is the Heart

## A prose-first ecology-and-migration story collection about people, memory, and what the record cannot settle.

### Prose Wave 64: Children’s Drawn Worlds

## Batch brief

**Content type:** original game-content proposal with scene, diegetic-record, conversation, and conditional-return drafts.
**Content bank:** 48 distinct beat pairings, each with four alternative authored forms (192 candidate passages).
**Current local anchor:** art_b2_005 — The Generator.
**Tone:** grounded, human, restrained, material, and careful about what a damaged record can prove.
**Scope:** game content only; no production code, JSON data, route, quest, flag, simulation, or save changes.

## 1. Expansion thesis

The Generator is an authored artwork record in childrens_artwork_batch_2.json. A resident child, age nine, draws the generator from a child’s upward perspective as a dark rectangle with pipes and wires and a small Electrician figure working at the bottom. The child says the generator is the heart and that when it beats the lights come on. The Electrician says he has never thought of it that way. This plan expands the drawing as a meeting between labor, scale, and metaphor without explaining the generator or adding a mechanical system.

## 2. Story question

When a nine-year-old draws a massive generator from below and calls it the heart that brings on the lights, what does a machine become when a worker sees himself inside a child’s metaphor?
