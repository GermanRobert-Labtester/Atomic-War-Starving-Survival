**01 — source fidelity.** The anchor remains ritual_crust_for_the_waste/outer_sill_offering (The Outside Crumb). Every proposed sentence must stay attached to the cited local record, and every inference must remain visibly an inference. The source can support a human moment without supporting a new mechanic, route, authority, or outcome.

**02 — material attention.** The strongest details in this bank are physical: a threshold, a surface, a line of sight, a document, a marker, a schedule, or a room that still has its old shape. Keep those details ordinary enough to be believed and specific enough to distinguish The Outside Crumb from another location.

**03 — voice separation.** The three roles in this plan do different work. One notices the immediate object, one protects the record’s limits, and one carries the emotional consequence. Do not let all three speak in the same polished register or give one person the authority of the whole The Outside Crumb record.

**04 — player space.** The player is allowed to observe before deciding what the observation means. A pause, refusal, question, or incomplete answer can be the passage’s consequence. Do not turn every emotional beat into a prompt, reward, relationship delta, journal flag, or measurable improvement.

**05 — return scale.** A return to The Outside Crumb should make the earlier passage newly legible, not rewrite it. **06 — uncertainty.** Uncertainty is authored content here. It is not a hole that needs to be filled with a hidden culprit, a technical explanation, a secret route, or an invented survivor. **07 — dignity.** People associated with The Outside Crumb should not be reduced to the hazard, policy, machine, or record that surrounds them. **08 — content scale.** The 48-beat bank is deliberately editorial. A production owner may select one passage, several passages, or none. The size of this document is a writing resource, not a request to implement every candidate or to create a parallel narrative registry.

**09 — continuity check.** Before placement, compare this bank against newer data, especially records that mention ritual_crust_for_the_waste/outer_sill_offering or the surrounding region. **11 — diegetic surface.** A record, notice, conversation, or scene should look like something a survivor could encounter in the existing content owner. **12 — emotional movement.** The movement through The Outside Crumb should be felt as a change in attention. The player begins by seeing an object or condition, then hears a person attach meaning to it, then decides whether to carry that meaning forward. Nothing larger is required for the passage to matter.

**13 — open ending.** Close on a human image rather than a verdict. A hand lowers, a page remains folded, a name is repeated once, a room continues its ordinary work, or a landmark stays where it was. The ending should leave room for the player and the current canon to do the next piece of work.

**14 — handoff.** If a content owner accepts a passage, preserve its attribution and keep the owner’s existing state, save, and routing seams. The handoff is complete only when the selected text, its source record, its trigger, and its audience all agree. Otherwise this remains editorial prose for review.

A selected passage may mention ritual_crust_for_the_waste/outer_sill_offering by its display name, by a local nickname already present in the source, or by an ordinary physical detail. Do not add a second identifier merely to make the prose sound larger.

Keep proposed objects portable in meaning but bounded in authority. A page can be carried, a marker can be seen, and a sentence can be repeated without creating an item registry, a universal rule, or a new save section.

Let silence have more than one cause in the prose: respect, fatigue, uncertainty, refusal, distance, or the simple fact that the current record ends. Never use silence as permission to invent a secret.
