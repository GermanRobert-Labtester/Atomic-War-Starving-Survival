## 1. Expansion thesis

The Cough in the Dark expands the Midnight Bunk Disturbance event about crowded sleeping, a hacking cough, a shove, a threat, exhaustion, and two possible outcomes: Unchecked Argument or Quiet Mediation. The plan deepens friction and the fragile possibility of repair without adding medical advice, sleep rules, conflict tactics, mediation trees, or relationship formulas.

## 2. Story question

When a hacking cough from a top bunk becomes a shoved shoulder and a hissed threat between exhausted survivors, what can a dark room hear before an argument decides what the cough means?
