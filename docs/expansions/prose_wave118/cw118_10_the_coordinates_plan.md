**01 — source fidelity.** The anchor remains cassette_free_radio_3/the_coordinates (The Coordinates). **02 — material attention.** The strongest details in this bank are physical: a threshold, a surface, a line of sight, a document, a marker, a schedule, or a room that still has its old shape. Keep those details ordinary enough to be believed and specific enough to distinguish The Coordinates from another location.

**03 — voice separation.** The three roles in this plan do different work. One notices the immediate object, one protects the record’s limits, and one carries the emotional consequence. **04 — player space.** The player is allowed to observe before deciding what the observation means. A pause, refusal, question, or incomplete answer can be the passage’s consequence. **05 — return scale.** A return to The Coordinates should make the earlier passage newly legible, not rewrite it. **12 — emotional movement.** The movement through The Coordinates should be felt as a change in attention.
