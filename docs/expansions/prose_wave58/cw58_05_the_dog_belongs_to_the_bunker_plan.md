## 1. Expansion thesis

On The Dog is a graffiti posting triggered by friction_second_bunk_permitted, available from day 20 with weight 1.0. Its text says the dog is not yours, not anyone’s, decided to stay, eats what it is given, and must not be given to the children because the children will give it the children’s ration. This bank follows a community negotiating affection, hunger, and the animal’s own presence. It does not add a pet system, feeding rules, training, or ownership mechanic.

## 2. Story question

When a wall note says the dog is not yours or anyone’s, and warns children not to spend the children’s ration on it, how does a bunker care for an animal without pretending care is ownership?
