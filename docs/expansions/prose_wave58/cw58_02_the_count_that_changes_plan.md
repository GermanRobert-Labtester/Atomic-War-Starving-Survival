## 1. Expansion thesis

Three Tins Of Sardines, Computational is a graffiti posting triggered by ration_first_tin_logged, available from day 30 with weight 1.0. Its text says the bunker counted the tins before bed and at dawn; the count went up because someone used a broken shell to record the new count, and went down because someone used daylight to record what they took. This bank treats the wall as a record of counting, theft, labor, and the strange dignity of making a loss legible without naming the person who caused it.

## 2. Story question

When three tins are counted before bed and again at dawn, and both the count and the record change, how does a ration ledger distinguish arithmetic from the person who needed the tin?
