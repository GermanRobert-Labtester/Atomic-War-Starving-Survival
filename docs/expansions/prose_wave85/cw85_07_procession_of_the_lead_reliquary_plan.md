## 1. Expansion thesis

The First Ash Procession expands a local rite about an annual walk from chapel to perimeter wall, a heavy reliquary, seven stopping points, the Book of Half-Lives, the response Settling, and the return to an alcove. The plan treats the procession as a public act of carrying history without adding hazardous-material handling, route, shielding, or reliquary mechanics.

## 2. Story question

When a lead-lined reliquary carries a vial called the First Ash around a settlement perimeter, what does a procession protect: the material, the boundary, or the memory of a place no one can safely revisit?
