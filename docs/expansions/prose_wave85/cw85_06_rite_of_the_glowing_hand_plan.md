## 1. Expansion thesis

The Mark Under Blue Light expands a final initiation trial about darkness, ten minutes, a UV lamp, faint blue-white fluorescence, mineral residue in bunker water, a lead chasuble, and admission to an inner circle. The plan treats the rite as a story of misread bodies and belonging without adding UV examination, radiation, water, medical, or initiation instructions.

## 2. Story question

When mineral residue is mistaken for a sacred mark, how does a novice keep the dignity of a body that the congregation has already interpreted?
