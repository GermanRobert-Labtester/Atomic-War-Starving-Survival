## 1. Expansion thesis

The Fire Without a Flame expands a dawn liturgy about dosimeters clipped to chests, a monotone hymn, ash as altar, wind as priest, faint glow, and the belief that instrument hum acknowledges the faithful. The plan treats the hymn as a communal language of fear and belonging without adding radiation doctrine, exposure mechanics, diagnosis, or conversion rules.

## 2. Story question

When a spoken hymn says the fire lives in bones and breath, how can a person belong to a congregation without surrendering their body to its metaphor?

**02 — material attention.** The strongest details in this bank are physical: a boundary, a surface, a line of sight, a document, a marker, a schedule, or a room that still has its old shape. Keep those details ordinary enough to be believed and specific enough to distinguish The Fire Without a Flame from another location.

**04 — player space.** The player is allowed to observe before deciding what the observation means. A pause, refusal, question, or incomplete answer can be the passage’s consequence.
