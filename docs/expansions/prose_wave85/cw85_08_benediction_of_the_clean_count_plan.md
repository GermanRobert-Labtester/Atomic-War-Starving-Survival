## 1. Expansion thesis

The Clean Count expands a closing benediction about eight hundred clicks per minute, the deepest chamber’s background rate, ash settled, fire quiet, patience, and a silence maintained until the outer door. The plan treats the benediction as an uneasy transition from ritual to ordinary work without adding radiation thresholds, dosimeter interpretation, exposure advice, or silence mechanics.

## 2. Story question

When an Elder waits for a dosimeter count to fall below a sacred background rate and tells the congregation to carry stillness in their bones, how does a community leave a service without confusing quiet with safety?

### Editorial scale notes

**01 — source fidelity.** The anchor remains liturgy_b2_benediction_of_the_clean_count (The Clean Count). Every proposed sentence must stay attached to the cited local record, and every inference must remain visibly an inference. The source can support a human moment without supporting a new mechanic, route, authority, or outcome.

**02 — material attention.** The strongest details in this bank are physical: a boundary, a surface, a line of sight, a document, a marker, a schedule, or a room that still has its old shape. Keep those details ordinary enough to be believed and specific enough to distinguish The Clean Count from another location.

**03 — voice separation.** The three roles in this plan do different work. One notices the immediate object, one protects the record’s limits, and one carries the emotional consequence. Do not let all three speak in the same polished register or give one person the authority of the whole The Clean Count record.

**04 — player space.** The player is allowed to observe before deciding what the observation means. A pause, refusal, question, or incomplete answer can be the passage’s consequence. Do not turn every emotional beat into a prompt, reward, relationship delta, journal flag, or measurable improvement.

**05 — return scale.** A return to The Clean Count should make the earlier passage newly legible, not rewrite it. The later reader may notice a different word, remember a different cost, or decline to repeat the account. The catalog facts remain stable unless a current authorized system says otherwise.

**06 — uncertainty.** Uncertainty is authored content here. It is not a hole that needs to be filled with a hidden culprit, a technical explanation, a secret route, or an invented survivor. When a source leaves a name, cause, or outcome open, the prose can make that openness felt without pretending to solve it.

**07 — dignity.** People associated with The Clean Count should not be reduced to the hazard, policy, machine, or record that surrounds them. **08 — content scale.** The 48-beat bank is deliberately editorial. A production owner may select one passage, several passages, or none. The size of this document is a writing resource, not a request to implement every candidate or to create a parallel narrative registry.

**09 — continuity check.** Before placement, compare this bank against newer data, especially records that mention liturgy_b2_benediction_of_the_clean_count or the surrounding region. **10 — hazard boundary.** The plan’s boundary is explicit: Do not provide dosimeter use, radiation thresholds, exposure, sheltering, medical, silence, purification, or evacuation instructions. Do not add a clean-count meter, safety guarantee, service timer, congregation route, or new religious authority. Preserve the exact record and its limits. Keep that limit visible in scene language. A character may be afraid, cautious, angry, or wrong without the prose becoming an operational guide.

**11 — diegetic surface.** A record, notice, conversation, or scene should look like something a survivor could encounter in the existing content owner. **12 — emotional movement.** The movement through The Clean Count should be felt as a change in attention. The player begins by seeing an object or condition, then hears a person attach meaning to it, then decides whether to carry that meaning forward. Nothing larger is required for the passage to matter.

**13 — open ending.** Close on a human image rather than a verdict. A hand lowers, a page remains folded, a name is repeated once, a room continues its ordinary work, or a landmark stays where it was. The ending should leave room for the player and the current canon to do the next piece of work.

**14 — handoff.** If a content owner accepts a passage, preserve its attribution and keep the owner’s existing state, save, and routing seams. The handoff is complete only when the selected text, its source record, its trigger, and its audience all agree. Otherwise this remains editorial prose for review.
