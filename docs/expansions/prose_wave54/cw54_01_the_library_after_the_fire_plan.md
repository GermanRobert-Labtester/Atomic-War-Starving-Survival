# EXPANSION CW54-01 — The Library After the Fire

## A prose-first ecology-and-migration story collection about people, memory, and what the record cannot settle.

### Prose Wave 54: Rooms After the People

## Batch brief

**Content type:** original game-content proposal with scene, diegetic-record, conversation, and conditional-return drafts.
**Content bank:** 48 distinct beat pairings, each with four alternative authored forms (192 candidate passages).

## 1. Expansion thesis

The Municipal Library record describes a location with 12.0 radiationUSv, danger level 4, and 1.8 travel hours. Its loot authority includes books, wood blocks, scrap wood, cloth, a cigarette lighter, family photographs, paper scraps, and a low-chance sealed government document with degraded paper as its alternative. This bank treats the library as a damaged civic memory: a place where useful material, private faces, and official silence share shelves. It does not turn the site into a scavenging route or a document-recovery procedure.

## 2. Story question

When a municipal library stands after fire with books, family photographs, paper, and one rare sealed document still possible among the debris, what does a settlement choose to remember first?
