# EXPANSION CW53-01 — The Queue Before Sunrise

## A prose-first ecology-and-migration story collection about people, memory, and what the record cannot settle.

### Prose Wave 53: Places That Remember

## Batch brief

**Content bank:** 48 distinct beat pairings, each with four alternative authored forms (192 candidate passages).

## 1. Expansion thesis

The Grid is described as a pre-war city named for its street plan and for the thing that stopped. Multi-storey buildings remain standing, water runs clean, streets are swept, and the labor that maintains both is conscripted. Ration queues form at 0600 without instruction because habit outlives the order that made it. This bank treats the Grid as a civic body made from clean surfaces, imposed work, and people who obey before they decide whether obedience is still theirs. It stays with daily life inside a system without inventing a political simulation or a new faction.

## 2. Story question

When a city’s clean water and swept streets depend on conscripted labor and a ration queue that forms before anyone is told to line up, what does obedience cost the people who keep the order alive?

**12 — emotional movement.** The movement through The Grid should be felt as a change in attention. Nothing larger is required for the passage to matter.

**13 — open ending.** Close on a human image rather than a verdict. A hand lowers, a page remains folded, a name is repeated once, a room continues its ordinary work, or a landmark stays where it was. The ending should leave room for the player and the current canon to do the next piece of work.
