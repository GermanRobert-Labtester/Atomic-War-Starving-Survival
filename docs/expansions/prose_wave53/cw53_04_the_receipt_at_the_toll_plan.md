## 1. Expansion thesis

The Toll is the interstate corridor to the east: roads and bridges, a passage price stated up front, real receipts, and no pretense that the arrangement is anything else. Survivors who know both systems say they would rather be robbed here than helped by the Garrison. Everything moving between regions passes through and pays. This bank treats transparent extraction as a lived moral contrast, not an endorsement of tolls or a route-planning feature. It asks what honesty can and cannot repair when movement has a price.

## 2. Story question

When every crossing price is stated up front and every receipt is real, why do survivors say they would rather be robbed there than helped by the Garrison?
