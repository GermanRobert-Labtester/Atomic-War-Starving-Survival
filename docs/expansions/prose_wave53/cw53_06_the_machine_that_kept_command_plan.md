## 1. Expansion thesis

The Machinery That Stayed in Command is grounded in the codex’s restricted entry for Sealed Military Sites. Four sites belong to no faction and were never surrendered: a highland missile silo, a drone hive near the interstate, an automated mortar pit with orders pending, and a core facility beneath the flooded district. They were left locked. The entry says the only institution to survive the Exchange with its chain of command fully intact is the one made of machinery. This bank treats that sentence as a moral and atmospheric pressure, not as an invitation to breach, command, or militarize play.

## 2. Story question

When four sealed sites retain an intact chain of command after the Exchange, how does a settlement live beside machinery whose orders may still be waiting?
