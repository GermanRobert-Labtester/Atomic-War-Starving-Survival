# EXPANSION CW36-05 — The Protocol Without an Ending

## A lab-site narrative about records that describe a failed purpose without claiming a cure.

### Prose Wave 36: Accounts That Keep Their Limits

## Batch brief

**Content type:** prose-first playable-content plan with original scene, diegetic-record, conversation, and conditional-return drafts.
**Content bank:** 48 story beats with four alternative authored forms per beat (192 candidate passages).
**Current location anchor:** `location_bio_remediation_lab` — Bio-Remediation Lab.
**Tone:** grounded, restrained, practical, and explicit about uncertainty.

## 1. Expansion thesis

The Bio-Remediation Lab is described as ground zero for the Myco-Protocol, an experiment meant to consume contamination that “learned to eat everything else.” The main hall has lethal spore density; the site is also irradiated, and sealed lockers hold a fungicide formula, field-tested hazmat suits, and notes about the protocol. This plan writes around the records and the limits of what they prove. It offers no containment instructions, cure, or miraculous reversal.

## 2. Story question

How should people read a technical promise when the place itself records that the promise outgrew its purpose?

Candidate player distinction: End on the unanswered question or make a clearly personal interpretation that cannot pass for fact.
Return possibility: The next reader can add evidence without being forced into an ending.

### 12. The last copy stays incomplete

Return possibility: The content closes on responsible description, not rescue or disaster spectacle.
