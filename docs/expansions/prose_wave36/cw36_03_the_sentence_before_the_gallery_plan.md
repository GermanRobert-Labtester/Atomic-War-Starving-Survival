# EXPANSION CW36-03 — The Sentence Before the Gallery

## A pass-road story about the repeated warning that becomes a social handoff.

### Prose Wave 36: Accounts That Keep Their Limits

## 1. Expansion thesis

The Avalanche Gallery is a hundred metres of concrete snow shed, half-filled from the uphill side. Its location description says the uphill side remains load-bearing, the downhill side does not, and visitors repeat that sentence before entering. This plan turns the repeated sentence into a human artifact: a phrase can be useful, incomplete, and carried from one traveller to another without becoming engineering advice or proof that the route is open.

## 2. Story question

When a warning is repeated often enough to become shorthand, who is still responsible for what it says?
