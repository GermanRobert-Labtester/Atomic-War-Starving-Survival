## 1. Expansion thesis

The Clicking Beetle Rhyme is a local bunker children’s folklore record. Eight-year-olds skip over braided telephone wire in a gymnasium corridor, singing a click-a-click sequence about a beetle, yellow dust, ten, and running to the pen. This plan expands the rhyme as embodied uncertainty while keeping dust, clicking, and movement outside a new hazard or route authority.

## 2. Story question

When eight-year-olds skip a braided telephone wire and sing click once, yellow dust, ten, and a pen, what does a sound tell children when they cannot see its source?
