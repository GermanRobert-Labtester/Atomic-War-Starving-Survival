## 1. Expansion thesis

The Cabbage Soup Counting Song is a local bunker children’s folklore record. Children stir thin cabbage broth with bent aluminum mess spoons, counting leaves of green, grey, brown, and grit, with work all day, a falling roof, and eating every bit. This plan expands the song as labor and scarcity held in a meal while keeping cooking, food safety, and structural danger outside a new authority.

## 2. Story question

When children stir thin cabbage broth and count green, grey, brown, and grit while the roof falls down in the rhyme, what does a pot hold when work and worry share one spoon?
