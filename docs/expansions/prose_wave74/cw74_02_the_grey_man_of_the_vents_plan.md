## 1. Expansion thesis

The Grey Man of the Vents is a local bunker folklore tale whispered by mothers to keep toddlers from climbing into air intake grates. It gives the Grey Man asbestos dust, turbine grease, rubber fingers, no face, and a transformation into filter fluff. This plan expands the tale as care under fear while keeping vents, blackout, dust, and bodily danger outside any instruction or literal monster system.

## 2. Story question

When mothers whisper of a grey man made from dust and grease who pulls toddlers into exhaust fans during blackout, how can a story protect children without turning fear into a literal threat?
