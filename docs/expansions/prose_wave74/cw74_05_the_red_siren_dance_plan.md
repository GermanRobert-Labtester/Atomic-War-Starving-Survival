## 1. Expansion thesis

The Red Siren Dance is a local bunker children’s folklore record. During intermittent red rotating beacon pulses in a monthly seal test, children freeze as statues; a child who blinks or moves before the all-clear is dragged into a pretend Decon Shower. This plan expands the ritual as children processing warning signals while keeping decontamination, beacons, and emergency response outside a new mechanic.

## 2. Story question

When children freeze as red beacons pulse during a monthly seal test and drag a blinking child into a pretend Decon Shower, what does play borrow from a warning without becoming a procedure?
