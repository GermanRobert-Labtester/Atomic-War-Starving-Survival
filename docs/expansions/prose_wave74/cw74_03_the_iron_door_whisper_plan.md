## 1. Expansion thesis

The Iron Door Whisper is a local bunker children’s folklore record. Children touch the cold steel of Blast Door Alpha with tongue or palm and hold an ear to its rubber gasket to hear howling winter wind. The child who lasts longest is named Outpost King. This plan expands the dare as social theater around a boundary while keeping cold, steel, and door operation outside any instruction.

## 2. Story question

When children dare one another to press against freezing steel and listen for winter wind until one becomes Outpost King, what does a game call courage when the door itself is a boundary?
