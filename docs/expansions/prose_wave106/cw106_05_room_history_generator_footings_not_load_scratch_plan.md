## 1. Expansion thesis

Not Load expands the Generator Footings room history about four anchor bolts, a thinner left-rear shim, idle vibration, a pencil warning on the inverter panel, an absent observer, an unreplaced shim, and a vibration that became part of the room’s sound. The plan deepens craft memory and unfinished care without adding electrical diagnostics, generator operation, load rules, repair steps, or maintenance ownership.

## 2. Story question

When a pencil scratch says NOT LOAD beside a generator that vibrates because one shim is thinner, what does a room remember about the person who noticed before anyone repaired it?

**01 — source fidelity.** The anchor remains room_history_generator_footings/not_load_scratch (Generator Footings). Every proposed sentence must stay attached to the cited local record, and every inference must remain visibly an inference. The source can support a human moment without supporting a new mechanic, route, authority, or outcome.

**02 — material attention.** The strongest details in this bank are physical: a threshold, a surface, a line of sight, a document, a marker, a schedule, or a room that still has its old shape. Keep those details ordinary enough to be believed and specific enough to distinguish Generator Footings from another location.

**03 — voice separation.** The three roles in this plan do different work. One notices the immediate object, one protects the record’s limits, and one carries the emotional consequence. Do not let all three speak in the same polished register or give one person the authority of the whole Generator Footings record.

**04 — player space.** The player is allowed to observe before deciding what the observation means. A pause, refusal, question, or incomplete answer can be the passage’s consequence. Do not turn every emotional beat into a prompt, reward, relationship delta, journal flag, or measurable improvement.

**05 — return scale.** A return to Generator Footings should make the earlier passage newly legible, not rewrite it. **08 — content scale.** The 48-beat bank is deliberately editorial. A production owner may select one passage, several passages, or none. The size of this document is a writing resource, not a request to implement every candidate or to create a parallel narrative registry.

**09 — continuity check.** Before placement, compare this bank against newer data, especially records that mention room_history_generator_footings/not_load_scratch or the surrounding region. **11 — diegetic surface.** A record, notice, conversation, or scene should look like something a survivor could encounter in the existing content owner. **12 — emotional movement.** The movement through Generator Footings should be felt as a change in attention.
