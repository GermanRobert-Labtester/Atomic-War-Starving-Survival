## 1. Expansion thesis

Forty-Two Seconds expands the Cupola Breath room history about cooling breath, tuyeres, the walk from charging floor to water jacket, a timed chalk mark, heat stain, a regular breath when the lining is good, a shorter sharper breath when it goes, and workers hearing the difference. ## 2. Story question

When a cooling cupola exhales through its tuyeres for forty-two seconds, how can a chalk mark teach a crew to listen without becoming an operating rule?
