## 1. Expansion thesis

The Turbine’s Sleep expands the night-shift superstition about refusing impeller service from three to five in the morning and the friction that creates with emergency repair shifts. The plan deepens belief, fatigue, and disagreement without adding maintenance instructions, time-window rules, emergency procedures, water-system authority, or a superstition mechanic.

## 2. Story question

When a superstition says the water-circulation impeller must not be serviced between three and five in the morning, how does an emergency shift live beside a belief that was never written as law?
