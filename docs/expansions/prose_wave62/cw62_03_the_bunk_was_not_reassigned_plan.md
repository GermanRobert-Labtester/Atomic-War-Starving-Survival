## 1. Expansion thesis

The Bunk Was Not Reassigned is a local graffiti posting triggered by escalation_walkout_reconsidered, available from day 55 with weight 0.8. It says the board calls for reassignment after seven days, nine days have passed, and the blanket remains folded the way the absent person folded it, wrong corner first. It asks whoever is counting days on the wall to stop counting aloud because some people are still counting too. This plan treats the bunk as held space and the number as a shared ache without adding a timer or occupancy system.

## 2. Story question

When the board says to reassign a walkout bunk after seven days, nine days pass, and a blanket remains folded wrong-corner-first while the wall asks everyone to stop counting aloud, what kind of waiting can a room keep?

The prose preserves those unknowns through existing walkout, bunk, board, return, journal, or narrative consumers. Verbatim local text: "the board says reassign the walkout bunk after seven days. it has been nine. the blanket is folded the way they folded it, wrong corner first. whoever is counting days on this wall — stop counting out loud. some of us are still counting too." The wrong-corner fold is powerful because it is a habit nobody needs to explain in order to recognize. It can make the absent person present without promising that the person will return. The wall asks whoever counts to stop counting aloud because spoken numbers can make uncertainty feel like a deadline. That request is not a silence mechanic.
