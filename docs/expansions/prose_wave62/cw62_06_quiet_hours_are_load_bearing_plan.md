## 1. Expansion thesis

Quiet Hours Are Load-Bearing is a local graffiti posting triggered by friction_radio_frequency_fear, available from day 48 with weight 0.7. It addresses the frightened from the tired, says the frequency nobody names is quiet from 2200 to 0400, connects that quiet to sleep and steady hands on valves, gives fear the day shift, and insists this is not a compromise but a schedule, one of the only walls people can build from paper. This plan explores rest as collective infrastructure without adding a quiet-hours system or radio control.

## 2. Story question

When frightened listeners are told that the unnamed frequency stays quiet from 2200 to 0400 so sleep can steady hands on valves, how can a schedule be a wall without becoming a compromise?

The fuller reading treats quiet hours as a boundary defended by tired people rather than negotiated by an abstract schedule. The address from the tired to the frightened is important.
