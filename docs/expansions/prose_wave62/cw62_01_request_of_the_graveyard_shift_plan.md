## A prose-first ecology-and-migration story collection about people, memory, and what the record cannot settle.

## 1. Expansion thesis

Request of the Graveyard Shift is a local graffiti posting triggered by ration_scoop_rotated, available from day 33 with weight 0.8. It says the scoop rotated to the night crew, thanks the day crew in the only grammar available, promises not to short the day crew at 0300 while they sleep and the ladle is in the night crew hand, and declares that this wall is the count. This plan expands the posting as a story of reciprocal restraint, hunger, and an improvised record without adding a ration ledger or night-shift procedure.

## 2. Story question

When the scoop rotates to the night crew and the night crew promises not to short the day crew at 0300 while nobody is counting, what can a wall count that a ladle cannot?

## 3. Verified local anchor and source records
