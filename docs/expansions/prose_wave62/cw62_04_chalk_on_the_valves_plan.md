## 1. Expansion thesis

Chalk on the Valves is a local graffiti posting triggered by escalation_sabotage_investigated, available from day 60 with weight 0.9. It says an investigation found chalk marks on three valves, lists them as opened, closed, opened, and says somebody was testing which valves the shelter watches. The answer is said to be in the corridor and in the handwriting: we watch all of them, a claim the people were not always saying aloud and now are. This plan expands the posting as institutional self-consciousness without adding a valve procedure, sabotage puzzle, or surveillance system.

## 2. Story question

When an investigation finds opened, closed, opened chalk marks on three valves and the corridor answers that it watches all of them now, what does vigilance sound like after a failure of attention?

## 5. Human center

The emotional center is a community discovering that attention itself has been part of the failure. The declaration to watch all of them is not triumphant; it admits that the shelter had been saying less than it now needs to say. Chalk turns institutional vigilance into a public, imperfect promise.
