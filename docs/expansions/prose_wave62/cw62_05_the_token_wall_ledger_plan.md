## A prose-first ecology-and-migration story collection about people, memory, and what the record cannot settle.

## 1. Expansion thesis

The Token Wall Ledger is a local graffiti posting triggered by friction_hot_token_confiscation, available from day 42 with weight 0.8. It says hot tokens were confiscated and calls the wall the new ledger: who worked, who ate, who waited. It says the wall is slower than tokens, cannot be pocketed or confiscated without scrubbing the corridor to concrete for a week, and closes by saying the tokens were metal while the record is load-bearing. This plan treats the wall as contested memory, not as a new accounting system.

## 2. Story question

When hot tokens are confiscated and a wall becomes a slower ledger of who worked, who ate, and who waited, what kind of record is load-bearing when the corridor itself could be scrubbed?

## 3. Verified local anchor and source records

The posting record establishes the title, exact text, triggerWorldFlag, minDay, and weight. It does not establish token rules, work records, ration amounts, waiting order, the corridor, or whether anyone can actually scrub it. The prose preserves those unknowns through existing token, work, ration, corridor, journal, or narrative consumers. Verbatim local text: "they confiscated the hot tokens. fine. this wall is the new ledger: who worked, who ate, who waited. it is slower than tokens and it cannot be pocketed and it cannot be confiscated without scrubbing the corridor down to concrete, which would take the whole shelter a week. the tokens were metal. the record is load-bearing." The fuller reading treats the wall ledger as a challenge to the portability of token authority. Metal can be pocketed, confiscated, and assigned to a person; a wall is slower and harder to move, but it can still be erased or read selectively. The three categories are deliberately ordinary. Who worked, who ate, and who waited are not a complete economy. They are the kinds of facts people remember when an official object disappears. The phrase load-bearing turns memory into shelter language while keeping the metaphor visible. The wall is not literally holding the corridor together, and the plan must not add a ledger state or a work tally. ## 5. Human center

The emotional center is a material record fighting a material authority. Metal can be pocketed; chalk can be erased; both forms of power are incomplete. Calling the record load-bearing turns memory into part of the shelter’s structure while admitting that the wall can still be damaged.
