## 1. Expansion thesis

If the Hatch Goes is graffiti triggered by escalation_walkout, available from day 50 with weight 1.0. Its text says someone who wants the hatch first should be allowed to take it, that bruises on the frame are not ours, that night shift did not read the duty board, and that pushing first makes the hatch remember. This bank treats the note as a record of accumulated pressure rather than a tactical instruction. It follows a door, a schedule, and a community deciding what it means when people walk out or try to get ahead of them.
