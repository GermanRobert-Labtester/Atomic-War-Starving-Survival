# EXPANSION 106 — Not a Pool

## A prose-led extension for Nila Brant and Allocation 11, where a blank chart protects people from becoming a count someone can use.

### Wave 20: Useful Systems, Real Consequences — prose continuation: Terms of Staying

## 1. Expansion thesis

Treat Allocation 11 as a place with a boundary, not a hidden resource depot. Nila’s current roster content already defines the filter exchange, the one-person limit, the erased chart, and the scar at Allocation 13. The expansion develops alternative lines and returns around that content while leaving every identity blank. This is a prose-first game-content plan: the main deliverable is scene text, dialogue, records, and state-aware return passages. Any proposed prose is supplemental to the current authored content and remains a proposal until accepted through the game's existing narrative owner.

## 2. Story in one sentence

Nila will make room for one person under the terms she has named, but she will not turn a hiding place into a pool that can be counted, requisitioned, or explained twice.

## 3. Verified local anchor and current story

`characters.json` defines `npc_nila_brant` as an unlisted lamp-oil clerk who occupies Allocation 11 with three others, keeps their chart blank, wants filters without a lecture and silence, and offers a hiding place and overflow shelter. `duty_roster_locations.json` describes `loc_overflow_alloc_11` and `loc_overflow_alloc_13`; the former holds four people and a wiped fourteen-row roster. `duty_roster_quests.json` includes `quest_roster_blank_access` and `quest_roster_nila_eleven`, plus wider roster quests that refer to Nila and the blank allocation. No base `locations.json` entry defines Allocation 11. The current blank-access quest trades a filter for access and states that Nila will hide one person whose name is not already on a stool-form, not three. The Allocation 13 quest offers leaving a scar, rewriting it, or taking a rubbing for Sole; copying it closes access to 11. These are existing choices. This plan does not invent a more generous permission or disclose a living name. The JSON record proves that these names, places, and story conditions are authored; it does not by itself prove a route is currently reachable in every campaign. Keep the plan's proposed insertions subordinate to the live content and its current-state conditions.

## 4. Fixed canon and proposed text

Allocation 11 is explicitly a hiding place, not a pool. Four people sleep there; their names are not on the wall. The unnumbered disc authenticates nothing. Nila will withdraw if a living name is written in ink. The rules for the existing quest, including its one-person limit and the relation between 11 and 13, are fixed. Any visitor names and extra records remain blank. Existing quest IDs, choice IDs, character states, names, location descriptions, and branch conditions remain the authority. Every additional visitor, line, paper, gesture, or callback below is proposed writing, not new established history.

## 5. Human center

Privacy here is not a puzzle prize. Nila does not owe a story because the player found the hatch, and the people inside do not become a group inventory because four bodies share a room. The plan treats silence as a condition of trust, not a mystery to defeat. The player can witness and respond, but the character's life does not become a demonstration designed for the player's moral growth. Let material detail carry emotion; do not tell the player which feeling to have.

## 6. Voice and point of view

Nila speaks in short terms and will not explain twice. Her bluntness is useful; she does not owe the player a softened version. Other characters can misunderstand the blank chart, but the narration cannot quietly agree with them. Never put the hidden residents’ names, trades, or histories in the player-facing prose. Keep narration close to evidence: what someone counts, moves, refuses to sign, or leaves within reach. People speak from their job and their fatigue. Nobody explains their own symbolism.

## 7. Placement in the current story

Keep all additions within the existing duty-roster quest surfaces and their current flags. Access to 11 stays tied to `quest_roster_blank_access`; the scar choices stay tied to `quest_roster_nila_eleven`. Preserve the current consequences if the blank is copied, rewritten, or left. The content bank supplies optional wording, not a new route to the Overflow or a new census state. The sequence below proposes alternate or additional prose for existing content surfaces. It does not introduce a parallel quest chain, duplicate a registered encounter, or promise a new arrival. Use the current quest or location owner to decide where an accepted line belongs.

## 8. Player agency and consequence

The player can give or refuse the filter, respect or violate the current boundary only through the authored choice, and leave the blank unfilled. Do not reward disclosure, force Nila to justify privacy, or turn the hidden people into a rescue count. If the current quest says access closes, later prose must honor the closed hatch. Preserve the player's ability to help, refuse, wait, or leave. Consequence should be visible in an authored state that already exists or in text conditional on an existing branch; do not imply new state from a line alone.

## 9. Dignity, safety, and scope

Keep the access boundary explicit, but do not write directions for finding or entering the hidden rooms. No identities, roster reconstruction, authenticator workaround, or safe status claim is added. Keep every hazardous, medical, private, or coercive subject within the boundaries of the source. The prose can show limits and uncertainty without explaining a dangerous workaround, exposing a hidden person's identity, or making an unverified treatment promise.

## 10. Content integration boundary

This plan adds no production code, JSON, quest ID, encounter ID, location, faction, route, resource rule, score, or save field. If an editor later selects a fragment, verify the existing content schema and state consumer first, then place it with the current owning data. The plan itself is review material only.

They are alternatives for the same moment, not four mandatory encounters. Select a coherent subset and keep each fragment inside the current character state described above.
