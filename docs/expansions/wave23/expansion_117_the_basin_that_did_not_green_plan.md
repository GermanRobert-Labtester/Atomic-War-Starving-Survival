## 1. Expansion thesis

The ecological dead zone is a place where the strongest observation may be the thing that does not answer back. The current location description gives a wind-scoured basin, fractured basalt without lichen, no insect hum, desiccated animal remains beneath acidic ash crusts, and ecological collapse following intense isotope deposition. The proposed story follows two unnamed readers as they keep an account exact while one of them longs for the sentence to mean more. Its movement is from inventory, to restraint, to the difficult acceptance that a page can preserve absence without explaining what comes after it. The player-facing material should make the location feel inhabited by the act of observing, copying, disagreeing, and deciding what a sentence can support. The prose does not need a new system to create consequence: it can change the reader’s interpretation of a record while leaving the world state untouched.

## 2. Story question

Can a record keep a place present without pretending it can tell us whether life will return?

## 5. Human center

The human center is the difference between a person who wants a sentence to comfort a later reader and a person who wants it to remain true. Their care is practical: they decide what a copy can carry when its original observer is absent. Both proposed roles are unnamed; no death is individualized for effect. The emotion should come through what a person chooses to write, omit, copy, or leave unsigned rather than through a narrator announcing what the site means.

## 6. Voice and point of view

Use a spare field-record voice, letting ordinary speech brush against observation. Wind, stone, and missing sound carry texture without luxuriating in ruin. Avoid a puzzle, spectacle, or collectible laboratory. Let distinct readers disagree about wording, not about invented facts. Keep the author’s knowledge local. A field note cannot know the history of an unnamed visitor; a later reader cannot recover a date that was never recorded; a title cannot create a route. Vary sentence length and register by document purpose, not by changing the world’s facts.
