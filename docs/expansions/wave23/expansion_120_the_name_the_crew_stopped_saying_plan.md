## 1. Expansion thesis

The Old Crematory Industrial Stacks entry holds a careful tension: the site is named for a decommissioned industrial crematory complex, while survey crews repurposed the chimneys as vertical markers and stopped saying what they were. The description calls that silence its own kind of respect. This plan does not fill the silence with secret histories, victims, or a memorial the data does not describe. It follows two unnamed readers deciding how a working shorthand can coexist with an explicit record of the site’s former use. The player-facing material should make the location feel inhabited by the act of observing, copying, disagreeing, and deciding what a sentence can support. The prose does not need a new system to create consequence: it can change the reader’s interpretation of a record while leaving the world state untouched.
