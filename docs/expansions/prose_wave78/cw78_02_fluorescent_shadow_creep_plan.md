## 1. Expansion thesis

The Violet Corridor Measurement expands a journal entry about a failing lamp, a violet twitch against raw concrete, a corridor that appears narrower during flicker, and a writer who measures the ceiling twice while feeling wet sand across the chest. The plan treats measurement as a self-made anchor in an unstable perception, without adding a light-failure puzzle, suffocation mechanic, or diagnosis.

## 2. Story question

When a dying ballast clicks twice every six seconds and a corridor seems six inches narrower even though the rule confirms two meters forty, what can a measurement hold that fear cannot?
