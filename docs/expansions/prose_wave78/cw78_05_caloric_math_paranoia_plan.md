## 1. Expansion thesis

The Twenty-Eight-Calorie Margin expands a journal entry that subtracts an 82-kilocalorie walk from a 110-kilocalorie broth and names a 28-calorie daily margin. The plan treats arithmetic as a mind trying to control scarcity, while refusing to turn the figures into nutrition advice, movement cost, or a resource mechanic.

## 2. Story question

When a dweller calculates every walk against turnip broth and calls each unnecessary footstep a slow suicide, how can a survival ledger show fear without becoming a rule for living?
