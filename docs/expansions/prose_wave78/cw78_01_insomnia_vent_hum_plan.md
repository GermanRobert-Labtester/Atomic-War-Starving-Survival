## 1. Expansion thesis

The Vent-Hum Vigil expands a local psychological journal entry about the 03:14 air-intake shift, the 03:30 organ-like duct tone, the writer’s knowledge that turbulent air crosses rivets, and the bruised forehead left by listening. The plan treats sleepless attention as a human scene of evidence and vulnerability, preserving the writer’s distinction between cause and experience without adding a sleep meter, haunting, diagnosis, or audio mechanic.

## 2. Story question

When a battery-trickle blower drops to a low B-flat and a sleepless listener hears women singing in the fan, how can a night record honor both the known sheet metal and the felt choir?

**09 — continuity check.** Before placement, compare this bank against newer data, especially records that mention journal_psych_insomnia_vent_hum or the surrounding region.
