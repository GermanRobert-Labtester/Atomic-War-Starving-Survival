## 1. Expansion thesis

A mine can leave valuable machinery and ore behind while leaving its workers’ lives in the wrong columns. This prose bank follows proposed voices who argue over whether a shift should be remembered by what it produced, what it risked, or who was expected to return.

When work stops before the ledger does, who gets to define the last shift?
