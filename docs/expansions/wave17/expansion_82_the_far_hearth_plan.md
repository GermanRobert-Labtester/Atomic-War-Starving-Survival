## 8. Player-to-state flow

The player opens the existing route, reads source-backed current state, chooses one of the feature's explicit commands, confirms against refreshed state, and receives a result from the owning command. The host translates that result into presentation and persistence effects exactly once. On cancellation, stale state, failed validation or a closed panel, no speculative mutation is shown as success. The three subfeature flows are separately mapped: 1. A place on the reachable map → 2. A supply road that tells the truth → 3. People, structures, and a safe return.

## 9. Exactly three subfeatures

### 1. A place on the reachable map

Show only locations the existing expedition/map authority can actually reach, preview the authored outpost type, initial garrison and cost, then commit establishment once. Reject stale locations and insufficient source stock before mutation; do not let the panel invent a destination. Authority boundary: Expedition/location availability owns reachability; ColonySystem owns the resulting ColonyOutpost; the established inventory owner supplies real materials.

### 2. A supply road that tells the truth

Route deliveries and supply-line status through ColonySystem and the existing campaign inventory/transport owner. Make arrival, interruption and shortfall visible without a second stock counter, free cargo, or silently successful delivery. Authority boundary: ColonySystem owns line status and colony stored-supply projection; the existing inventory/logistics owner must own source deduction and transport feasibility.

### 3. People, structures, and a safe return

Connect assigned survivors and authored buildings to truthful occupancy, defense and morale descriptions; let the player stop expansion and plan an orderly withdrawal through existing expedition and survivor ownership. Authority boundary: ColonySystem owns its current population ID list and building records only after a legal command; campaign roster and expedition owners retain survivor and travel authority.

## 10. State and command contract

1. A place on the reachable map: use stable IDs, validate against the current source owner immediately before commit, make repeated UI delivery idempotent where the domain permits, and return an explicit accepted/refused/stale outcome.
