## 9. Exactly three subfeatures

### 1. Prepare a date without surprise costs

Provide a forward calendar view based on the canonical day clock and authored holiday dates. Show the date, known preparation window, current eligible group and complete cost before the player makes a commitment. Authority boundary: SeasonalCelebrationSystem owns holiday definitions and check results; campaign clock owns the current day; inventory owners provide affordability and committed stock.

### 2. Hold an inclusive, costed gathering

Offer the authored scale as a single command, explain affordability and what participation means, then report the Core result once. Skipping remains a valid player choice with its authored consequence shown ahead of time. Authority boundary: SeasonalCelebrationSystem applies authored cost, participant scaling, streak and morale rules; inventory and survivor owners remain authoritative for actual availability and eligibility.

### 3. Remember anniversaries without owning the dead

Expose founding, fallen and milestone remembrance through existing history facts, with a consentful date/name representation and an option to keep private notes private. Authority boundary: Campaign chronology and survivor/death records provide facts; SeasonalCelebrationSystem records its current anniversary type; chronicle/journal owner controls long-term campaign remembrance.
