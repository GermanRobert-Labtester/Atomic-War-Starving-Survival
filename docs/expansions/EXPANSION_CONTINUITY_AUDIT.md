# Expansion Continuity & Chronology Audit

## 1. Timeline & Chronological Alignment

All four expansions operate within a synchronized campaign chronology:

| Phase | Campaign Days | Primary Expansion Focus | Chronological State |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Phase 1: Freeze & Seam** | Days 1–60 | Holdfast & Survival Base | Desalination operational, Ice Road opens at Day 14 freeze window. |
| **Phase 2: Arbitration & Archive** | Days 60–160 | Standing Record & Crossing | Crossing Viaduct active; Standing Record archaeological sites accessible. |
| **Phase 3: Culpability & Inquest** | Days 160–240 | The Verdict & Reckoning | Machine log awakens; Geophone arrays read seismic surges; Evidence Ledger active. |
| **Phase 4: Reckoning & Resolution**| Days 240–360 | The Verdict Appeals & Endings | Final tribunals convened; Appeals processed; Campaign epilogues evaluated. |

## 2. Narrative Invariants Guarded
1. **No Temporal Mismatches:** Late-game Verdict evidence cannot be referenced or accessed during early Holdfast ice road quests.
2. **Faction Identity Continuity:** Faction names and lore remain strictly uniform across all four catalogs (e.g. `faction_the_scale`, `faction_the_cutters`, `faction_central_garrison`).
3. **No Resurrected NPCs:** Deceased historical figures in Standing Record memories are strictly framed through archival logs, recordings, or physical artifacts.
