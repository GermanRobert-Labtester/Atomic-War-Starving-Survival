## 1. Expansion thesis

Ghost Moth Swarm Front describes a warm humid wind carrying a pale cloud of ghost moths inland across the lowlands. Wings beat against outer intake grates, leaving sticky white fibers and powdery scales that choke air filters and threaten outdoor cultivation beds. Existing choices seal the intake perimeter or harvest silk-pupae clusters for emergency protein. This bank follows a shelter trying to name the swarm as weather, ecology, nuisance, and possible food without turning the event into pest-control or harvesting instructions.

## 2. Story question

When a warm wind carries a pale swarm against the shelter’s air grates, how does a community decide what to protect when the same living cloud threatens filters and crops?
