## 1. Expansion thesis

Iron Crow Railway Flyway describes hundreds of metallic-sheened crows descending onto rusted transmission towers along a railway cut. Their rhythmic cawing echoes across the valley before they continue south. The flock strips dead wood of grubs and cleans the tracks of carrion, leaving signs of predator activity. The existing choice logs flight vectors to infer fresh carrion or predator locations. This bank treats the crows as seasonal witnesses and the railway as a place where nonhuman movement can tell a story without becoming a tactical intelligence system.

## 2. Story question

When hundreds of metallic-sheened crows roost on transmission towers and clean the tracks, what does a flock reveal without becoming a surveillance instrument?
