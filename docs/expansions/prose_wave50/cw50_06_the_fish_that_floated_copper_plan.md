## 1. Expansion thesis

Toxic Slag Runoff Taints River Fish describes a ruptured upstream tailings dam releasing acidic heavy-isotope slurry into the river. Dead mirror carp float belly-up along the reeds, their gills bright copper. The event warns that fish harvested without thorough decontamination cause severe radiation sickness, and its authored choice quarantines river harvest until currents flush clean.
