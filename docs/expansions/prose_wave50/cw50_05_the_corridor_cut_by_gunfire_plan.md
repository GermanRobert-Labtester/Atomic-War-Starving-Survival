## 1. Expansion thesis

Faction Gunfire Blocks Migration Corridor describes mortar fire and fortified barricades at a highway junction severing a traditional descent route for feral goat herds. Cut off from winter forage, starving animals are forced into predator-rich canyon defiles; herd numbers fall while wolves circle closer to perimeter watchposts. This bank holds the moral asymmetry of a war that harms animals who never chose it, without turning the corridor into a military or wildlife-management tutorial.

When gunfire and barricades sever a herd’s winter route and wolves close in, what does a shelter owe animals trapped by a human conflict?
