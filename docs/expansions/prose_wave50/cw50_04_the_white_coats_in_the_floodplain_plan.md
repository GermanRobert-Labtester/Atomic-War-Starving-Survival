## 1. Expansion thesis

Lowland Cotton Hare Population Boom describes cotton hares multiplying across southern floodplains after a mild thaw. Their white summer coats flash across heather while hundreds of young feed on succulent scrub shoots. The event says snares along hedgerows can double the typical catch over the next week.
