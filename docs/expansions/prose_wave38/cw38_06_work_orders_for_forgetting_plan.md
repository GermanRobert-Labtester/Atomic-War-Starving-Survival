## 1. Expansion thesis

Lethe Water Treatment is a public plant on the surface and a memory operation below it. The labels are gone, the work orders remain, and the sub-level holds the raw materials of forgetting beside people who were careful about their own memories. This prose bank follows an archivist, a former visitor, and a medicine keeper as they decide whether a document can be handled without turning another person’s loss into a resource.

## 2. Story question

Who has the right to keep a record of what people were asked to forget?

### Wild card
