## 1. Expansion thesis

The Wire-Heads built a camp inside a cargo plane and wired the fuselage with their own circuits. They arrange blown fuses like relics, sell bypass codes and decrypted keys, and set prices that do not move for sympathy. This prose bank follows a buyer, a Wire-Head keeper, and a listener trying to understand whether exactness is a kind of mercy or another way to keep people out.

## 2. Story question

When a community trusts circuits more than words, how can a promise be made legible?
