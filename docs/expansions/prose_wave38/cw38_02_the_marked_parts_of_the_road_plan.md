## 1. Expansion thesis

The highway choke is a road made of delayed decisions: unexploded ordnance under the tarmac, markers fading pink, and a median trade that remembers the first people who tried to clear a path. This prose bank follows a courier, a marker-keeper, and a person waiting on the far side as they argue over whether a route can be inherited without inheriting its risk.

## 2. Story question

Who owns a warning when the marks fade before the danger does?
