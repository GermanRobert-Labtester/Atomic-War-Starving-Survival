## 1. Expansion thesis

A flooded depot preserves a city’s machinery under water while the people who work there preserve a different kind of knowledge: where a footfall comes back late, where a pipe rings hollow, and when a price is really a refusal. This prose bank follows a settlement visitor and Sump-Dredger voices who argue over whether useful knowledge can be shared without turning a living hazard into a route.

## 2. Story question

When the people who know the water will not draw you a map, what does trust look like?

### Interlinked
If an existing consumer already exposes the anchor, a later journal, radio, codex, item, faction, or expedition passage may echo one sentence with its original attribution. The echo changes who hears it, not what the source says.

### Wild card
A proposed reader misremembers a detail and is corrected by somebody who was there. The correction should remain small and human. Never use the wild card to reveal a hidden route, secret operator, miracle device, or new canon casualty.
