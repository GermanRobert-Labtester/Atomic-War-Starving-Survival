## 1. Expansion thesis

A Last Wish Kept is a narrative event after the shelter does what was asked. The wish is kept, the room changes, voices run lower and aim at one another instead of the walls, and nobody mentions the empty bunk even though everyone knows what it cost to honor the last of it. This bank is about aftermath without spectacle: the emotional architecture of a room that has performed one final act of care and must now live with the silence it made possible.

## 2. Story question

When a shelter fulfills a final wish and the room’s voices turn toward one another, how can an ending remain a shared act instead of a victory lap?
