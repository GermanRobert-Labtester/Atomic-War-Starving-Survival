## 1. Expansion thesis

The Candle in the Duct event places a tallow stub in an air-intake niche during a dawn vigil. The machinist finds it smoking, names wasted fat and soot in the duct, and points to risk written on the filter. Believers call the light a memorial beacon for a lost shift; the machinist says the duct does not care what the light meant. Both are right, and the burned-out stub sits between them. Existing choices curtail the fuel or designate an unventilated stone alcove for memorial lights. This bank stays with a community trying to keep respect from becoming denial and safety from becoming contempt.
