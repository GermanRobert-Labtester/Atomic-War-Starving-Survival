## 1. Expansion thesis

A Shadow on the Return Trail describes a gaunt mongrel trailing fifty paces behind an expedition. It stops when the scouts stop, sits when they check bearings, and waits at the airlock with ribs visible beneath scarred gray fur. The event does not state where the dog came from or what the shelter does next. This bank follows the quiet pressure of an animal’s patience, the expedition’s return becoming a household question, and the difference between being followed and being claimed.

When a gaunt mongrel follows an expedition without asking to join and waits at the airlock threshold, what does a shelter owe a creature that has already chosen it?
