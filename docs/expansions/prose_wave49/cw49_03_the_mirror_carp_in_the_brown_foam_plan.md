## 1. Expansion thesis

The Estuary Mirror Carp Surge event describes heavy silver-scaled mirror carp pushing upriver against a spring-thaw current. Armored backs break brown foam in shallow gravel shoals, and for several weeks basket nets and weir traps can yield substantial fresh protein. Existing choices harvest spawning carp in bulk or assay the current for dissolved isotope runoff before harvesting. This bank treats abundance as movement rather than ownership, keeping hunger, contamination, and the river’s seasonal life in the same scene without providing fishing or water-testing instructions.

## 2. Story question

When a river fills with spawning carp and the shelter can harvest or test the water first, what does abundance owe the current carrying it?
