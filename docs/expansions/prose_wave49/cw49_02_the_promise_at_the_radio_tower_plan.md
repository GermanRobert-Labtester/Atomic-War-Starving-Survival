## 1. Expansion thesis

The Unfinished Promise event describes a survivor asking authorization to go to a radio tower on a ridge that routes go around. A fallen comrade promised to climb it, and the promise remains unkept. The requester knows the route is dangerous; the record says the asking is not about the route. Existing choices forbid the journey or authorize a partnered recovery with a veteran scout. This bank follows grief trying to turn an absence into an appointment, and a shelter deciding whether honoring a promise means reenacting its danger.

## 2. Story question

When a survivor wants to reach a dangerous radio tower because a fallen comrade promised to climb it, what is the journey carrying if it is not about the route?
