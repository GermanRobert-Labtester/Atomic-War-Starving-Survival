## 1. Expansion thesis

Flooded Culvert Whine begins after heavy runoff fills the perimeter ditch. A sharp, choked whine cuts through the water noise; a soaked pup clings to a floating packing crate caught against the storm grate. Pulling it out costs warm towels and a ration bowl, while driving it off saves food but leaves the bunker silent in a hollow way. This bank stays close to the event’s compressed moral choice, letting tenderness, scarcity, and the sound of an animal in distress coexist without animal-handling or flood-response instruction.
