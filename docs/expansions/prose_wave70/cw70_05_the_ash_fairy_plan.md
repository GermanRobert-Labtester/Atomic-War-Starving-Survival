## 1. Expansion thesis

The Ash Fairy is a local children’s folklore record in childrens_folklore_expansion.json. A seven-year-old who has never seen a blue sky says the ash fairy is the grey, makes rain and snow grey, came when the sky fell, and will leave when the sky comes back. The child says the fairy is neither bad nor good and believes it will go. This plan expands the tale as a child’s ethical and atmospheric story while keeping sky, ash, weather, and the promised return outside any environmental prediction or literal creature system.

## 2. Story question

When a seven-year-old who has never seen a blue sky says an ash fairy is the grey, not good and not bad, and will leave when the sky comes back, what does hope look like when it is built from inherited weather?
