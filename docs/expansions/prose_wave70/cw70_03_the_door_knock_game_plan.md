## 1. Expansion thesis

The Door Knock Game is a local children’s folklore record in childrens_folklore_expansion.json. Children play in the main corridor by knocking on the wall and answering their own questions: who is there, not the wind or air, not the friend or kit, perhaps the boots, perhaps the dark, and maybe let them in. They do not know what sin means; they know the maybe. This plan expands the game as a ritual of uncertainty and consent language without establishing a visitor, threat, door rule, or hidden route.

## 2. Story question

When children knock on a wall and answer with wind, air, friend, kit, boots, dark, and a maybe that is called sin, what does permission feel like when nobody knows who is outside?
