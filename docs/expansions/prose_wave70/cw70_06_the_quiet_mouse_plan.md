## 1. Expansion thesis

The Quiet Mouse is a local children’s folklore record in childrens_folklore_expansion.json. Children tell other children the quiet mouse is the smallest, lives in the wall, makes no sound, wins the quiet game, and becomes the model for anyone who is quiet. The not-mouse is the giggling child who loses. This plan expands the tale as a social game about belonging, silence, and the tenderness required when a playful rule becomes too close to a judgment.

## 2. Story question

When children say the smallest mouse lives in the wall, wins by being quiet, and turns every giggle into a not-mouse, what does winning mean when the game makes a child’s identity depend on one sound?
