## 1. Expansion thesis

The Seed Woman is a local children’s folklore record in childrens_folklore_expansion.json. Children who visited the seed vault tell of a woman in the cold with forty-seven seeds, a spring that wakes her, a farmer who receives the seeds, and a cold frame that becomes spring. The children saw the 47; the 47 is the story. This plan expands the tale as a child’s seasonal memory of stewardship while keeping the vault, seeds, farmer, cold frame, and number outside new agriculture or inventory authority.

## 2. Story question

When children who visited a cold vault say a seed woman sleeps with forty-seven seeds until spring, what does waiting become when the story keeps turning sleep into planting and planting into return?
