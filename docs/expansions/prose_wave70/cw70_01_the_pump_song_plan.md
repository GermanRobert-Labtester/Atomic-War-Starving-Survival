## 1. Expansion thesis

The Pump Song is a local children’s folklore record in childrens_folklore_expansion.json. Children sing while jumping over a braided rope in the south corridor: pump, two litres a day, fill your cup, two for morning, two for night, two for the children, and the right as ration. The rope is the pump and the pump is the rope. The children do not know why two; they know two. This plan expands the song as embodied memory around water language while keeping the lyric’s numbers separate from any current water authority.

## 2. Story question

When children jump a braided rope and sing about two litres in the morning, two at night, and water as life, what does a number hold when the children know two but do not know why?
