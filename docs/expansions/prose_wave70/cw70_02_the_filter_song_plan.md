# EXPANSION CW70-02 — The Filter Song

## A prose-first ecology-and-migration story collection about people, memory, and what the record cannot settle.

## 1. Expansion thesis

The Filter Song is a local children’s folklore record in childrens_folklore_expansion.json. Children in the school corner clap in a circle: filter, air, ninety percent is not air, change the filter, change the air, and mask and face exchanging places. The clapping is the filter, and the children do not know what ninety percent means; they know it is not air. This plan expands the song as a child-made language for unseen dependence while keeping air, masks, and percentages outside any instructional authority.

## 2. Story question

When children clap that ninety percent is not air and that the mask is the face, what does a number become when no child in the circle knows what it means?
