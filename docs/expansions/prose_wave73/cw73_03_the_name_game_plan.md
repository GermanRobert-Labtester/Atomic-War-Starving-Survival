## 1. Expansion thesis

The Name Game is a local children’s folklore record in childrens_folklore_expansion.json. Children in the shelter yard ask one another recursive questions from name and me through here, now, meal, bread, flour, number, secret, quartermaster, book, and back to name. They do not know the secret; they know it is the number. This plan expands the game as circular identity and institutional mystery while keeping names, secrets, books, and quartermaster records outside new authority.

## 2. Story question

When children ask name, me, here, now, meal, bread, flour, number, secret, quartermaster, book, and name around a circle, what does identity become when every answer returns to the beginning?
