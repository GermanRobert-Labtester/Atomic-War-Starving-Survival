## 1. Expansion thesis

The Spring Rhyme is a local children’s folklore record in childrens_folklore_expansion.json. Children at the allotments jump an irrigation hose, treating hose as rope and spring as jump. They have seen green and say spring is real, while the lyric moves through sprout, potato, farmer, cup, tea, wall, sun, before, and after. This plan expands the rhyme as witnessed seasonal hope while keeping agriculture, weather, and irrigation outside new authority.

## 2. Story question

When children jump an irrigation hose and say spring is green, sprout, potato, cup, wall, sun, before, after, and real, what does a season become when the children have seen only one proof?
