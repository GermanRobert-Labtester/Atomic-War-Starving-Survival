## 1. Expansion thesis

The Winter Counting is a local children’s folklore record in childrens_folklore_expansion.json. Children in the school corner count winters on their fingers: one first, two long, three last, three spring, cold, colder, coldest, and over. They do not know if three is last; they know three is spring and over. This plan expands the rhyme as seasonal uncertainty held in the hand while keeping weather, calendars, and forecasts outside a new system.

## 2. Story question

When children hold up three fingers for winter, spring, coldest, and over while not knowing if three is last, what does a season become when counting is the only calendar?
