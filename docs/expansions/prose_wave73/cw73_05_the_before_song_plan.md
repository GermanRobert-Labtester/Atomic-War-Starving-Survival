## 1. Expansion thesis

The Before Song is a local children’s folklore record in childrens_folklore_expansion.json. Children sing at bedtime, taught by a teacher who remembers the before. The song links blue sky, clear rain, gold sun, old world, grey now, spring after, and a before that comes after. The children do not remember the before; they remember by singing. This plan expands inherited memory as a bedtime practice while keeping weather history and spring outside new authority.

## 2. Story question

When children sing of blue, gold, old, grey, now, after, and a spring that is the before that comes later, how can memory be sung by people who never witnessed it?
