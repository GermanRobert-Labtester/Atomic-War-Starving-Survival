## 1. Expansion thesis

The Bread Song is a local children’s folklore record in childrens_folklore_expansion.json. Children at the evening meal pat their stomachs and exchange bread, flour, hour, slice, thin, stomach, eating, win, and sin. They do not know what sin means; they know bread is thin and eating is sin. This plan expands the song as embodied scarcity language while keeping hunger, food, guilt, and rationing outside a new mechanic.

## 2. Story question

When children pat their stomachs and sing that bread is flour, thin is in, eating is sin, and thin is win, what does a meal mean when the children know the words but not the guilt?
