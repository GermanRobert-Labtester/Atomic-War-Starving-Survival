## 1. Expansion thesis

The Chef Cooking is an authored artwork record in childrens_artwork_batch_2.json. A resident child, age nine, draws the Chef at a wood stove stirring a pot, with an apron and a hat the child has added even though the Chef does not actually wear one. Steam rises in curly lines. The child says she makes the best food. The Chef says the drawing is pinned to the kitchen wall next to the Sunday Special drawing, and the kitchen is starting to look like a gallery. This plan expands the image as labor seen through affection, playful invention, and a working room made briefly ceremonial.

## 2. Story question

When a nine-year-old draws the Chef stirring a pot, adds a hat she does not wear, and the kitchen pins the page beside the Sunday Special, what does affection change when it enters a working room?
