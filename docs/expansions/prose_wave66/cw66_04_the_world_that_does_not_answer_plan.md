## A prose-first ecology-and-migration story collection about people, memory, and what the record cannot settle.

## 1. Expansion thesis

The Radio Host is an authored artwork record in childrens_artwork_batch_2.json. A resident child, age eleven, draws the Radio Host at his microphone mid-speech, one hand raised and mouth open. The microphone is rendered in detail while the radio equipment behind him remains abstract. The child says he talks to the world, that the world does not answer, but that he keeps talking. The Radio Host says the drawing is the best portrait anyone has ever made of him. This plan expands the portrait as address, persistence, and the dignity of a voice whose audience cannot be confirmed.

## 2. Story question

When an eleven-year-old draws a radio host speaking into a detailed microphone and says the world does not answer but he keeps talking, what does a voice become when persistence is its only reply?
