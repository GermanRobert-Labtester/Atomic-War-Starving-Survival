## Shared thread

The plans use an optional set of separate paper copies called the North Service Packet. It connects forestry stores, utility switching, and a materials test prepared for a winter route and clinic delivery. A later note cancels that delivery after the clinic moves its patients. No master authority or hidden conspiracy is proposed. Each story can be played and understood on its own; visiting all three sites adds only a bounded account of what the surviving copies establish and what remains unknown.

The common creative concern is ordinary work under pressure: what people wrote down, what crossed a route, what was left out of a short label, and how a later reader can act without turning an incomplete record into a promise.

## Evidence and promotion

The plans cite current location, expedition, world-map, damaged-map-zone, and cartography data. The Materials Research Sublevel plan also checks the existing Core boundary and avoids adding a materials or regional-grid authority. Each document records its own source details and promotion checks. Those sources must be re-read before implementation; these drafts are not integration approval, ownership claims, or proof that every proposed route is reachable.

The plans are content proposals for later review under INTEGRATION_PLANS.md, WORKTREE_OWNERSHIP.md, TEST_POLICY.md, KNOWN_DEBT.md, and AI_AGENT_WORKFLOW.md. No central queue, shared ledger, source code, tests, or gameplay data were changed for this wave package.
