## 1. Expansion thesis

The radio telescope array is made of house-sized dishes that groan in the wind, warm receivers on a backup line, and headphones that work while the sky answers nothing. This bank follows a relay operator, a guard, and a reader of maintenance logs through the moral pressure of an instrument that still appears prepared for contact. Their task is not to invent a signal but to give silence a careful audience and keep the difference between capacity and communication intact.

## 2. Story question

What does it mean to keep listening when the machine is ready and the universe offers no answer the record can verify?

### Wild card
