## 1. Expansion thesis

The shattered observatory is a place where an old instrument still turns and the people who visit it need the difference between looking and claiming. A teacher counts fewer stars, a guard keeps weather from becoming spectacle, and a reader protects a logbook whose final line is less a conclusion than an admission. This bank treats the dome as a human room of attention: a place for records, grief, and the modest courage of saying that the sky is changed without pretending to know everything it means.

## 2. Story question

When the sky has become evidence of damage, how can a person keep looking without turning observation into ownership?

## 5. Human center

The emotional center is the need to look at a damaged world without making the act of looking another form of possession. The teacher wants an honest count; the guard wants the room to remain survivable; the logbook reader wants the last writer to be heard as a person rather than mined for a prophecy.

### Wild card
