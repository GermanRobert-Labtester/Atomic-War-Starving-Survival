## 1. Expansion thesis

The mirror factory is a dangerous field of broken glass and a strangely intact surface that reflects a coat in a back room. The expedition report explains that the coat was later found in that back room and that the mirror was showing it; the atmosphere record leaves the first sight uncanny without declaring a haunting. This bank follows the people who saw, carried, wore, and recorded the coat. It keeps the mirror literal enough to remain grounded and strange enough to remain memorable.

## 2. Story question

When an image seems to contain a room the body cannot find, how can a person honor what was seen without making it supernatural?
