## 1. Expansion thesis

The submerged arcology preserves a failed promise in luxurious materials: dated records, open gold, crystal that can carry water, and a waterline that has stopped rising for now. This bank follows people who approach the dry upper floors without turning the place into a treasure room. They argue about whether the missing glass is a mystery, a practical choice, or a private act that should remain private. The prose keeps comfort, hunger, value, and memory in the same room.

## 2. Story question

When comfort survives as an artifact and survival survives as an absence, what does a person owe the things left behind?

## 5. Human center

The human center is the uncomfortable difference between what a household once called comfort and what a later person calls useful. One voice appraises objects; another remembers how the rooms were meant to feel; a third protects the right of an absent person to have taken one ordinary thing without becoming a legend.
