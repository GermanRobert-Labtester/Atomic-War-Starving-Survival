## 1. Expansion thesis

The batching plant offers the raw materials of a future city and the immediate warning of a building whose main beam has cracked. This bank follows an engineer, a mason, and a person who only wants a wall that will not move. They discuss cement bags, rebar, dust, hoppers, and the moral pressure to call a dangerous storehouse useful. The plant’s narrative value lies in the tension between making foundations and admitting that the place itself may not be one.

## 2. Story question

How can a community imagine rebuilding while standing inside a structure that has not finished deciding whether it will stand?

### Wild card
