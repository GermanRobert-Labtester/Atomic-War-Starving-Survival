**04 — player space.** The player is allowed to observe before deciding what the observation means. A pause, refusal, question, or incomplete answer can be the passage’s consequence. **09 — continuity check.** Before placement, compare this bank against newer data, especially records that mention contraband_modified_filter_cartridge or the surrounding region.
