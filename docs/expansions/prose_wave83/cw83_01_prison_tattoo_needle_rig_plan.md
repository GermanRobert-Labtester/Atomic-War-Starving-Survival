**01 — source fidelity.** The anchor remains contraband_prison_tattoo_needle_rig (The Oilstone Tattoo). Keep those details ordinary enough to be believed and specific enough to distinguish The Oilstone Tattoo from another location.

**04 — player space.** The player is allowed to observe before deciding what the observation means. A pause, refusal, question, or incomplete answer can be the passage’s consequence. **14 — handoff.** If a content owner accepts a passage, preserve its attribution and keep the owner’s existing state, save, and routing seams.
