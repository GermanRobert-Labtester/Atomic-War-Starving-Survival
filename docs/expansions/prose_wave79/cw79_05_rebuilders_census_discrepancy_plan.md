## 1. Expansion thesis

The Six Unregistered Portions expands a local wiretap about a census discrepancy, three unprocessed children, two Quiet House visitors, and one person who eats from the communal pot without claiming a name. The plan treats counting as an ethical and administrative problem, preserving the ghost-eater joke and the Chef’s nightly count without adding a surveillance quest, ration algorithm, or identity hunt.

## 2. Story question

When a census lists 147 residents but ration counts find 153 mouths, how can a registrar make room for children, visitors, and an unnamed nightly eater without turning hunger into suspicion?
