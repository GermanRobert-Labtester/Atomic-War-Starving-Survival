## 1. Expansion thesis

The Small Tin Division expands the memorial rite record about unclaimed belongings, close peers, a desperate economy, and the decision to keep one relic while returning the rest to shelter life. The plan deepens grief, guilt, and material memory without adding inventory ownership, funeral procedure, grief meters, or recovered-body mechanics.

## 2. Story question

When close peers divide a dead person’s meager effects—a broken comb, a pencil stub, a bloodstained glove—and keep only one piece as a relic, what does choosing one object admit about all the objects left behind?

## 5. Human center

The emotional center is the refusal to pretend that keeping everything would be more loving. The rite makes one object carry the intimacy of the person while admitting that the shelter cannot exempt every remaining thing from need.
