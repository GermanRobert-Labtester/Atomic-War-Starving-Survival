## 1. Expansion thesis

The Truce Has a Table expands the Grand Treaty Barter Fair record about five days of preparation, a mess hall, a checked airlock, exchanged necessities, and a truce that keeps fingers off triggers for now. The plan deepens ceremony, suspicion, and the fragile labor of sitting together without adding barter, weapons, faction, water, or event mechanics.

## 2. Story question

When paranoid delegations check weapons at an airlock and trade scrap and water under a promised four-day truce, what does a formal peace protect when every hand still remembers the trigger?
