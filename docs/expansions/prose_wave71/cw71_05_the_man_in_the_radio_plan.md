## 1. Expansion thesis

The Man in the Radio is a local children’s folklore record in childrens_folklore_expansion.json. Children hear a relay operator and imagine a man living in air and radio, talking as proof that he is there, alone but not alone through talking. They know he is the operator, do not know his name, and call his voice the name. This plan expands the account as a child’s map of distant labor and presence while keeping radio operation, identity, and transmission outside a new system.

## 2. Story question

When children know a relay operator is a man but call him the man in the radio because they know his voice and not his name, what does a voice become when distance is the only address?
