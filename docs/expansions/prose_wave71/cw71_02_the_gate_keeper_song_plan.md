## 1. Expansion thesis

The Gate Keeper Song is a local children’s folklore record in childrens_folklore_expansion.json. Children near the gate play one keeper and make others guess a word: gate and door, wall and gate, keeper and key, friend and one who knows, word and gate. They do not know the real word; they make up the word. This plan expands the game as improvised belonging and uncertainty around a boundary while keeping the actual gate, access authority, and password outside the prose proposal.

## 2. Story question

When children near the gate play keeper, key, friend, word, and knowing while admitting they do not know the real word, what does a made-up password protect?
