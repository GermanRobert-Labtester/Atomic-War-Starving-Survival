## 1. Expansion thesis

The Lady in the Well is a local children’s folklore record in childrens_folklore_expansion.json. Children at the pump say a lady is the water, deep, cold, clear, and giving; thanking the well brings more, while not thanking brings less. They know the water is real and do not know if the lady is real. This plan expands the story as gratitude and uncertainty around a shared source while keeping water output, pump operation, and any moral exchange outside a new mechanic.

## 2. Story question

When children at the pump thank a lady they say is water and not real, what does gratitude become when the water is certain but the person-shaped giver is not?
