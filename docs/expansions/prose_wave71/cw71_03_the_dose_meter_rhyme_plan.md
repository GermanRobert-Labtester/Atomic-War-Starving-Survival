## 1. Expansion thesis

The Dose Meter Rhyme is a local children’s folklore record in childrens_folklore_expansion.json. Children jump rope in the north cupola where old sentries taught them to listen to a dosimeter: tick quick and dust thick, tick slow and meter low, fast is the run, slow is the go. The meter is the bug, the bug is the click. The children do not know what the tick means; they know fast is run and slow is go. This plan expands the rhyme as inherited alert language while keeping radiation, dosimetry, and movement decisions outside a new rule system.

## 2. Story question

When children jump rope to a dosimeter’s tick and call fast the run and slow the go without knowing what the tick means, how can a sound carry caution without becoming a safety instruction?
