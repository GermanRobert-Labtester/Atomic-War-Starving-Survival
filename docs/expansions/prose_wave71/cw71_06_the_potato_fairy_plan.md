## 1. Expansion thesis

The Potato Fairy is a local children’s folklore record in childrens_folklore_expansion.json. Children see a farmer cry at a sprout after a long time without first green. They call the green a fairy, the farmer a planter, the fairy a grower, and planting and growing the two that make a potato. They do not know why the farmer cried. This plan expands the tale as children witnessing labor, relief, grief, and first growth while keeping planting, crop outcomes, and the fairy outside new agriculture authority.

## 2. Story question

When children see a farmer cry at the first green sprout and call the green a potato fairy, what does a harvest hope look like when nobody knows why the tears came?
