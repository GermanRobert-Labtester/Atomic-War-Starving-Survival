## 1. Expansion thesis

The Candle Counting is a local children’s folklore record in childrens_folklore_expansion.json. Children at the evening meal count candles on the mess hall table: one burning, two gone, three waiting for dawn, four melting, five thin, six out, and darkness entering. They exchange dark, candle, wick, spark, last, and all. They do not know what happens at zero; they know zero is dark. This plan expands the count as communal evening attention while keeping candle use, lighting, and darkness outside a new mechanic or safety authority.

## 2. Story question

When children count candles down through melting, thinness, darkness, wick, spark, and zero at an evening meal, what does a number become when nobody knows what comes after the last flame?
