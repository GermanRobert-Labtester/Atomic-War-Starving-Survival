## 1. Expansion thesis

The Sentry Rifle Cairn is a local epitaph record about Stepan’s six years of night watch, his treadmill training with a twenty-kilo pack for a walk to Smolensk that never happened, his death facing south, and a fiancée’s photograph and ring beside a dosimeter. This plan expands the memorial as duty, deferred travel, and the dignity of making a weapon still without adding weapon handling or a patrol system.

## 2. Story question

When a disarmed rifle stands upright in black river stones and its removed bolt rests like a coin over a dead man's eyes, what does a watch remember after the watcher is gone?
