## 1. Expansion thesis

Sulfur Knob Outcrop is a fumarolic rock formation that vents persistently and can be seen from great distances as a triangulation target. A discovery manifest places a recovered epitaph there: a respirator mask on a pine branch for Tunnel Sweeper Gavin, whose memorial says he salvaged copper through heavy grey snow and died young. The bank follows the tension between a landmark used by instruments and a memorial that asks people to walk softly. The vent is not a puzzle and the mask is not equipment advice; both are limits on what the place will let the player claim.

## 2. Story question

When a visible outcrop carries a memorial for someone who breathed the heavy grey snow, how should the living approach the name?
