## 1. Expansion thesis

The Blanket at the Footboard expands the empty-bunk memorial rite about twenty-four hours, a sacred untouched bunk, crowded dormitory, folded wool, and a body that will not return. ## 2. Story question

When a deceased dweller’s bunk remains untouched for twenty-four hours in a crowded dormitory, how does a folded blanket make absence occupy more room than a body did?

**01 — source fidelity.** The anchor remains memorial_rite_empty_bunk_night/unmade_bunk_island (The Unmade Bunk Night). The source can support a human moment without supporting a new mechanic, route, authority, or outcome.

**02 — material attention.** The strongest details in this bank are physical: a threshold, a surface, a line of sight, a document, a marker, a schedule, or a room that still has its old shape. Keep those details ordinary enough to be believed and specific enough to distinguish The Unmade Bunk Night from another location.

**03 — voice separation.** The three roles in this plan do different work. One notices the immediate object, one protects the record’s limits, and one carries the emotional consequence. Do not let all three speak in the same polished register or give one person the authority of the whole The Unmade Bunk Night record.

**04 — player space.** The player is allowed to observe before deciding what the observation means. A pause, refusal, question, or incomplete answer can be the passage’s consequence. Do not turn every emotional beat into a prompt, reward, relationship delta, journal flag, or measurable improvement.

**05 — return scale.** A return to The Unmade Bunk Night should make the earlier passage newly legible, not rewrite it. **06 — uncertainty.** Uncertainty is authored content here. It is not a hole that needs to be filled with a hidden culprit, a technical explanation, a secret route, or an invented survivor. **11 — diegetic surface.** A record, notice, conversation, or scene should look like something a survivor could encounter in the existing content owner. **12 — emotional movement.** The movement through The Unmade Bunk Night should be felt as a change in attention. **13 — open ending.** Close on a human image rather than a verdict. A hand lowers, a page remains folded, a name is repeated once, a room continues its ordinary work, or a landmark stays where it was. The ending should leave room for the player and the current canon to do the next piece of work.

**14 — handoff.** If a content owner accepts a passage, preserve its attribution and keep the owner’s existing state, save, and routing seams. The handoff is complete only when the selected text, its source record, its trigger, and its audience all agree. Otherwise this remains editorial prose for review.

The proposed bank keeps returning to The Unmade Bunk Night because repetition is part of survival writing. A person sees the same surface twice and notices a different cost. That change belongs to attention, not to an invented state.

A selected passage may mention memorial_rite_empty_bunk_night/unmade_bunk_island by its display name, by a local nickname already present in the source, or by an ordinary physical detail. Do not add a second identifier merely to make the prose sound larger.

Keep proposed objects portable in meaning but bounded in authority. A page can be carried, a marker can be seen, and a sentence can be repeated without creating an item registry, a universal rule, or a new save section.

Let silence have more than one cause in the prose: respect, fatigue, uncertainty, refusal, distance, or the simple fact that the current record ends. Never use silence as permission to invent a secret.

Where a source names a hazard, write the people’s relationship to that hazard rather than a method for defeating it. Fear, caution, grief, and disagreement are content; operational guidance belongs to an authorized system owner, not this bank.

The document’s length does not widen the canon. It gives the writer room to choose among small moments at The Unmade Bunk Night while keeping the source boundary visible in every form. A longer bank is useful only when its alternatives remain honest and distinct.

The last editorial pass should ask whether the passage could belong to someone standing near The Unmade Bunk Night with limited time, imperfect memory, and no need to impress a reviewer. If the answer is yes, the bank has done its work: it has made a place available for play without pretending the place belongs to the plan.
