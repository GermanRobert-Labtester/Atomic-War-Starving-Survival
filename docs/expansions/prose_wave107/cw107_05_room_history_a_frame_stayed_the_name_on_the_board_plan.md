## 1. Expansion thesis

The Name on the Board expands the A Frame Stayed room history about an unwritten first death, a midnight watch, the far frame, a corridor walk under blower noise, an immovable frame, stripped bunk, boiled pad, wiped runner, an absent third pad, stencil gaps, legible paper tags, and the custom of writing each next name. The plan deepens memorial continuity without adding death procedures, sanitation guidance, bunk assignment, medical care, or a memorial system.

## 2. Story question

When a bunk frame stays after a death and the crew writes the next person’s name on a board, how can a shelter make room for the living without erasing who was carried out?

#### Conversation fragment 001 — First death — First death

The tag keeper and the new arrival meet over the omission. One of them wants the detail to settle the room. The other keeps the record open. The death was not written up, making omission part of the history.

“What was recorded?”

“The first death was not dramatic enough to be written up.”
