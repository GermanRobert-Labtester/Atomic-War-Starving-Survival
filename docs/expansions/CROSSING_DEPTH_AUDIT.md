# Crossing Depth Audit (Expansion 04)

## 1. Subsystem Coverage

Nobody's Charter / The Crossing represents the legal, humanitarian, and political friction of border arbitration.

### Key Metrics
- **Quests:** 20 quests (12 core, 8 border & humanitarian cases).
- **Encounters:** 14 encounters (incl. 4 major crises: mass refugee surges, military blockades, quarantine lockdowns, syndicate bribes).
- **Locations:** 13 locations across the viaduct, weighbridge, stallrow, and underwrite halls.
- **Systems:** `CrossingArbitrationSystem`, `CrossingSession`, `TradingSystem`.

## 2. Deepened Mechanics
- **Arbitration Bench:** Defector asylum claims, uninspected medical contraband, impounded vehicle salvage mortgages, destitute refugee sponsorship, and contaminated grain trials.
- **Crisis Management:** Fire-hose crowd control at the gate, half-track blockade negotiations, market health cordons, and transit bribe rejections.
- **Trade & Treaties:** Emergency Flotilla mooring rights, scrap lathe blockade runners, and fallout storm border lockdowns.
