## 1. Expansion thesis

Pre-War Civilian Shelter B-12 was privately funded beneath a residential complex. Its excavation record points toward domestic survival gear, medical supplies, and handwritten diaries, with shorter depths and lower structural risk than the military cache nearby. This bank follows a diary reader, a household claimant, and an excavation witness through the intimate scale of a shelter built by people who expected to return to their own rooms. It keeps the music-box relic and diary reference as evidence of ordinary life, not as a sentimental reward.

### Interlinked

### Wild card
A proposed reader misremembers a detail and is corrected by somebody who was there. The correction should remain small and human. Never use the wild card to reveal a hidden route, secret operator, miracle device, or new canon casualty.
