## 1. Expansion thesis

The Pack Opened Once expands the clinic history about an unnamed person, an opened and resealed suture pack, mismatched medical tape, a sealed pre-Exchange expiry date, a usable pack, a useful basin, and a missing mixing bowl. The plan deepens continuity and absence without adding medical procedure, inventory ownership, identity reconstruction, or clinic authority.

## 2. Story question

When a clinic pack was opened by someone no longer on the roster, resealed with different tape, and remains usable beside a vanished mixing bowl, what does a room keep when it cannot keep the person?
