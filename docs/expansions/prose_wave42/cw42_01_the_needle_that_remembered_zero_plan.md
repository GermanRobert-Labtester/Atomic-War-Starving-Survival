## 1. Expansion thesis

The electrical substation is a yard of dead transformers and tangled busbars, but its surviving control room holds tools, spares, and an analogue voltmeter with a cracked bakelite case. A final-wish record gives that instrument to an electrician who remembers a mentor’s warning about readouts that do not move. This bank follows the person who wants the old needle, the reader who refuses to make a dead yard safe by nostalgia, and the quiet act of mounting a tool where somebody else can notice when it stops being true.

## 2. Story question

When a dead yard still contains the tool someone learned to trust, what does it mean to bring a measurement home?

### Wild card
A proposed reader misremembers a detail and is corrected by somebody who was there. The correction should remain small and human. Never use the wild card to reveal a hidden route, secret operator, miracle device, or new canon casualty.
