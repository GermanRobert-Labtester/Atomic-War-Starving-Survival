## 1. Expansion thesis

The Foundry West stacks rise over residual chemical fires, while Iron Synod canons turn scrap, slag, alloy, oil, brass, files, and memorial ingots into a religious language. Discovery records attach the foundry to ritual canons and institutional rules, but the content does not require a lecture on metallurgy. This bank follows a worker, an outsider, and a memorial keeper through the difference between reverence for labor and obedience to a doctrine that can harden people as easily as metal.

## 2. Story question

When a foundry turns materials into doctrine, how can a person remember that metal was made for people before it was made sacred?

## 5. Human center

The emotional center is the way work becomes sacred when people need labor to mean more than exhaustion. A worker may find dignity in the canon and still question its punishments. An outsider sees rules about metal; a memorial keeper sees names pressed into floors. The stacks hold both human craft and institutional hardness.
