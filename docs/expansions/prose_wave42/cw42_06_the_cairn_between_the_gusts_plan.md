## 1. Expansion thesis

Wind Gap Ridge is a narrow pass swept by near-constant high wind. Surveyors shout readings between gusts, while the discovery manifest places a spent-casing cairn and the epitaph of Sentry Martha Kline at a ridge overlook. Her marker remembers holding a switchback while a nursery convoy descended. This bank follows a survey caller, a memorial keeper, and a traveler who learns that the ridge’s loudest record may be the sentence spoken during the brief silence.

## 2. Story question

When a ridge lets surveyors speak only in the gaps between gusts, how can a memorial remain audible without becoming a command?
