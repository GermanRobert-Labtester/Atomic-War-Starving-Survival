**01 — source fidelity.** The anchor remains journal_day_268_fuel_crisis/dangerous_territory (Day 268: Fuel Crisis). Keep those details ordinary enough to be believed and specific enough to distinguish Day 268: Fuel Crisis from another location.

**03 — voice separation.** The three roles in this plan do different work. One notices the immediate object, one protects the record’s limits, and one carries the emotional consequence. **04 — player space.** The player is allowed to observe before deciding what the observation means. A pause, refusal, question, or incomplete answer can be the passage’s consequence. **05 — return scale.** A return to Day 268: Fuel Crisis should make the earlier passage newly legible, not rewrite it. **12 — emotional movement.** The movement through Day 268: Fuel Crisis should be felt as a change in attention.
