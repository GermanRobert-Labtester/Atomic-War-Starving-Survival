## 1. Expansion thesis

Brine-Pan Hollow is a fortified salt camp. Its fire-watch rota is the closest thing the settlement has to government. Fair rationing and boiler vigilance are values; fuel shortages create disputes. Varn manages shifts and rationing, Elena trades weighed drums, and Petyr tends flues and condensers. This expansion follows people reading a shared rota without adding a schedule, allocation, or repair system. The bank below is intended to produce playable narrative texture: a player can discover, compare, question, refuse, or return to a passage while the existing game systems continue to own state. The fragments are written as content, not as a feature roadmap.

## 2. Story question

How does a rota hold people together when work and fuel do not divide evenly?

## 5. Human center

The person on watch knows a written line cannot carry every burden. Varn’s discipline can protect and tire people; Elena and Petyr represent different forms of trust. The emotional pressure should come through what a person records, omits, repairs, asks, or leaves unsigned rather than a narrator naming what the location means.
