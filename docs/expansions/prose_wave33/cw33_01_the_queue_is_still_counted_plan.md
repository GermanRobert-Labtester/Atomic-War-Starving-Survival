# CONTENT EXPANSION CW33-01 — The Queue Is Still Counted

## Lina keeps a place in the line by remembering that a count is a claim someone can check.

## 1. Expansion thesis

The Municipal Baths hold heat in tile and sound in the room. First-winter mattresses remain in rows, wet where the roof dripped and sleepers avoided their heads. The shelter emptied quickly because coughs and arguments echoed. Lina was found keeping queue count after adults stopped. She is seven, maybe eight; she hums when frightened, teaches a lesson back wrong to check attention, wants usefulness without charity, and will not hide fear on command. This expansion keeps counting human-sized: a place held, an error shown, an adult willing to answer twice. It does not make Lina a mascot or clerk. The bank below is intended to produce playable narrative texture: a player can discover, compare, question, refuse, or return to a passage while the existing game systems continue to own state. The fragments are written as content, not as a feature roadmap.

## 2. Story question

When a child can show the work behind a count, what does the room owe besides the answer?

Her fear is not a defect to cure. The emotional pressure should come through what a person records, omits, repairs, asks, or leaves unsigned rather than a narrator naming what the location means.

### 1. The room returns a number — First clean count

### 2. A count becomes a question of trust — Tile carries a cough

### 3. A boundary stays visible — Room emptied

### 4. Usefulness is not an assignment — Useful, not charity

An offer sounds like pity; Lina redirects toward what she can do.

### 5. The adult learns to answer twice — Count is not a name
