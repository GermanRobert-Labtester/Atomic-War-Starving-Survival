## 1. Expansion thesis

The municipal wastewater plant is a place where a shift could be measured by what stopped making noise. Its discovery material preserves two slow-sand observations, each carrying its own author and limits. The expansion treats those records as human documents rather than a how-to manual: a worker tries to make a clean handoff; a later reader notices what the entry cannot certify; a person assigned to the next shift chooses not to claim more than the page proves. Methane hazard context remains a warning attached to the location. Nothing here promises safe entry, potable water, or successful treatment. The bank below is intended to produce playable narrative texture: a player can discover, compare, question, refuse, or return to a passage while the existing game systems continue to own state. The fragments are written as content, not as a feature roadmap.

## 2. Story question

How does a record of careful work remain honest when the result it cannot certify matters most?

## 5. Human center

The human center is a shift handoff written by someone who knows the next person will want certainty. The writer can leave a precise account of what they observed and still refuse to certify what they cannot test. That restraint is not failure or cowardice; it is care for the people who might trust a confident sentence. The plant’s hours become personal through small choices: whether to sign, whether to copy, what to leave blank, and how to say “I do not know” without abandoning the person who will read it. The emotional pressure should come through what a person records, omits, repairs, asks, or leaves unsigned rather than a narrator naming what the location means.

## 6. Voice and point of view

Use an observational register for the logs, a practical voice for the handoff, and a later reader’s careful questions. Do not reproduce or extend technical steps from the records into operational instruction. Proposed workers stay unnamed unless an existing source names them. Keep the writing free of real-world water-safety recommendations: this is fictional lore that explicitly cannot certify safety. Keep each author’s knowledge local. A field note cannot know what an unnamed visitor thought; a later reader cannot recover a date that was never recorded; a title cannot create a route. Vary sentence length and register by document purpose, not by changing established facts.

## 8. Player agency

The player may read both logs, compare how their authors describe uncertainty, ask whether either claims a safe result, or leave the archive without an answer. The player does not operate the plant through dialogue or choose a treatment outcome. A choice of which record to read first can be a presentation order only if an existing surface supports it; it cannot alter the water, the facility, or the characters. Do not add a moral-choice menu simply to make quiet prose interactive. Preserve the player’s refusal, ability to leave, and the authority of the characters who own their words.

## 9. Continuity, dignity, and safety

Keep the setting fictional and physically grounded. No absent person receives a biography merely to heighten emotion. Technical and medical context remains descriptive and non-instructional.

## 11. Narrative sequence

### 1. Disturbance — the shift did not end cleanly

A paper waits for the incoming worker. The plant is described as a remembered worksite, not as a present operational opportunity.

### 2. Discovery — two observations

The player sees two logs and learns to distinguish one writer’s account from another’s.

### 3. Interpretation — what was seen

A scene makes the gap between observation and certification emotionally legible without supplying a technical answer.

### 4. Complication — methane context

The location hazard stays present in the reading context; no one explains a safe procedure.

### 5. Choice — copy carefully or leave blank

An editorial voice must decide whether to preserve a gap rather than fill it with a guess.

### 6. Return — the handoff reaches another reader

A later callback thanks the record for being honest, not for proving a result it never claimed.

## 12. Creative variants

### Grounded

Use one handoff and the two logs as separate quiet records, with no equipment scene.

### Interlinked

Allow a return callback only if a current archive surface can preserve each source’s attribution.

### Wild card

Tell one beat as a blank line intentionally left for the next worker, with no result supplied by the game.

## 13. Alternative forms and editorial rubric

The four forms under each beat are alternatives, not four required encounters. Scene drafts stage an observation; record drafts give it a plausible author and audience; conversation fragments expose a practical point of friction; consequence vignettes allow a later reader to recognize changed context. Select only forms that fit an existing content owner.

A selected fragment should answer who made it, why they made it, who might read it, and what the author cannot know. Keep objects specific to this location. Let a line do practical work before it carries a theme. Remove exposition that a worker would not say aloud, repeated catastrophe language, unearned revelation, and wording that could be mistaken for a new mechanic. Where existing game state matters, state it in the source owner’s terms and do not duplicate its authority.
