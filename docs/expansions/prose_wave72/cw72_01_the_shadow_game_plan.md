# EXPANSION CW72-01 — The Shadow Game

## A prose-first ecology-and-migration story collection about people, memory, and what the record cannot settle.

### Prose Wave 72: Children’s Folklore as Identity and Absence

## 1. Expansion thesis

The Shadow Game is a local children’s folklore record in childrens_folklore_expansion.json. Children in the corridor make shadows on a wall with a lamp, exchanging shadow, child, light, dark, going, coming, and not-you. They say the shadow is not the dark and that the dark is the no-shadow. This plan expands the game as identity held in changing light while keeping lamp operation, darkness, and bodies outside a new mechanic.

## 2. Story question

When children make shadows with a lamp and say the shadow is the you that is not you, what remains of a person when the light goes out and comes back?
