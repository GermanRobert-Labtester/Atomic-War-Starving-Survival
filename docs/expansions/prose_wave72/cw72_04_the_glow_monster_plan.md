## 1. Expansion thesis

The Glow Monster is a local children’s folklore record in childrens_folklore_expansion.json. Older children tell younger children an invisible monster lives in dirt, water, and bone, taking hair first, teeth second, strength third, and breath last. The younger children touch hair and teeth. The record says neither group knows the monster is radiation and that knowing is not-knowing. This plan expands the story as fear and care passed between children while keeping radiation, health, and exposure outside any instruction or literal entity.

## 2. Story question

When older children tell younger children that an invisible glow monster takes hair, teeth, strength, and breath while admitting nobody knows what it is, what does fear do when it has no visible body?
