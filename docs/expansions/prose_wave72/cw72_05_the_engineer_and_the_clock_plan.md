## 1. Expansion thesis

The Engineer and the Clock is a local children’s folklore record in childrens_folklore_expansion.json. Children who visited the boiler room say the engineer removed a wall clock, put it in the generator, made it a heart and bone, and made the generator live. They know the engineer and the clock but do not know what a bone is for a generator. This plan expands the tale as observed labor translated into body language while keeping repair, power, and machine state outside a new system.

## 2. Story question

When children say an engineer gave a generator a clock for a heart and bone, what does skilled work become when a repair is remembered as making a machine live?
