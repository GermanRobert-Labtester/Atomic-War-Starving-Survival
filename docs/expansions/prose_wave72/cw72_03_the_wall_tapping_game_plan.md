## 1. Expansion thesis

The Wall Tapping Game is a local children’s folklore record in childrens_folklore_expansion.json. Children at the north wall of the shelter yard tap carved names: one tap for the name, two for the all, with first, second, and third hands carving twelve, twenty-three, and twelve before the third stops. The children do not know the names; they know the tapping. This plan expands the game as embodied memorial attention while keeping identities, counts, and authorship outside a new archive or memorial mechanic.

## 2. Story question

When children tap carved names and say a third hand stopped carving, what does remembering hold when the children know the tapping but not the people named?
