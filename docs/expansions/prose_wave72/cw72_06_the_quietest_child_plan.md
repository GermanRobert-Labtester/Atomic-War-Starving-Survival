## 1. Expansion thesis

The Quietest Child is a local children’s folklore record in childrens_folklore_expansion.json. Children in Ward C say the quietest wins by not giggling, not breathing, not moving, and being not-there. They close their eyes and hold their breath, equating winning with disappearance. This plan expands the record as a critique of a game rule that turns absence into victory, keeping the children’s chant and its danger boundary visible without providing behavior instruction.

## 2. Story question

When children say the quietest wins by not giggling, not breathing, not moving, and not being there, what does winning demand before the game becomes a danger?
