## 1. Expansion thesis

The Counting Children Game is a local children’s folklore record in childrens_folklore_expansion.json. Children at the mess hall count one through five, ask who is not alive, exclude those eating, sleeping, playing, or crying, and count one missing from the line. They do not say who is missing; they know. This plan expands the game as collective noticing and protected privacy while keeping attendance, death, and absence outside a new status system.

## 2. Story question

When children count who is eating, sleeping, playing, crying, and missing from the meal while refusing to say who is absent, what does silence protect?
