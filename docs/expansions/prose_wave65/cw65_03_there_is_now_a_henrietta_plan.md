# EXPANSION CW65-03 — There Is Now a Henrietta

## A prose-first ecology-and-migration story collection about people, memory, and what the record cannot settle.

### Prose Wave 65: Children’s Instrumented Witnesses

## Batch brief

**Content type:** original game-content proposal with scene, diegetic-record, conversation, and conditional-return drafts.
**Content bank:** 48 distinct beat pairings, each with four alternative authored forms (192 candidate passages).
**Current local anchor:** art_b2_009 — The Chicken.
**Tone:** grounded, human, restrained, material, and careful about what a damaged record can prove.
**Scope:** game content only; no production code, JSON data, route, quest, flag, simulation, or save changes.

## 1. Expansion thesis

The Chicken is an authored artwork record in childrens_artwork_batch_2.json. A resident child, age six, draws one of the three greenhouse chickens with surprising anatomical accuracy: comb, feathers, feet, and eye. The child says her name is Henrietta. The Botanist says there is no chicken named Henrietta. The child answers, There is now. This plan expands the drawing as observation becoming relationship, while keeping the chicken count, the child’s claim, and the Botanist’s correction inside the source boundary.

## 2. Story question

When a six-year-old draws one greenhouse chicken with anatomical care, names her Henrietta, and answers that there is now a chicken by that name, what does naming add to a living place?

## 3. Verified local anchor and source records

The cited record establishes the artwork ID, artist label, title, medium, day, tags, and exact description. It does not establish which chicken is drawn, whether any label is adopted by the shelter, the child’s name, the Botanist’s reasons, or whether Henrietta remains the child’s name later. The prose preserves those unknowns through existing artwork, child, chicken, greenhouse, botanist, journal, or narrative consumers. Verbatim local record: "A drawing of one of the three greenhouse chickens. The chicken is rendered with surprising anatomical accuracy — the child has observed it carefully. The comb, the feathers, the feet, the eye. The child says: Her name is Henrietta. The Botanist says there is no chicken named Henrietta. The child says: There is now." The fuller reading keeps the name as a child’s act of attention rather than a registry change. The drawing is accurate in bodily detail and imaginative in social claim. That combination lets the child see a creature closely while also giving it a place in language. The Botanist’s statement can be practical, amused, irritated, or protective, but the source does not settle the tone. A candidate passage may let the adult ask whether a name needs permission, whether the chicken answers, or whether the child is naming the drawing as much as the bird. None of those questions needs a system response. The three-chicken count remains a source fact, while Henrietta remains a possible relationship layered over it. A return can show the child looking for the same comb or eye, the Botanist using the name once by accident, or another resident refusing it while still recognizing the bird. The prose should not promise that naming changes care, ownership, breeding, or animal behavior. It can show that attention makes a particular life harder to treat as interchangeable. The child says there is now because the page has made room for a person-like history without needing to make the chicken human. Keep the drawing exact, the name tender, and the authority plural. A page about Henrietta can stay beside the greenhouse record without forcing the Botanist to surrender the official language. The child may point to the eye and say that a name is how one remembers which bird was watching. The adult may answer with the count of three and still use the name once, quietly, when no one is asking for a decision. Another resident may prefer no name and still help keep the drawing dry. These small choices let naming become a relationship rather than a system. If the drawing returns after a season, the feathers can be rendered differently while the source count remains unchanged. The artwork can make one chicken particular without assigning the bird a voice, a backstory, or a production role. The child’s there is now remains a claim of attention, and the greenhouse can hold it beside measured facts.
