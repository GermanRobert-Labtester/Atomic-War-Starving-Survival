## 1. Expansion thesis

The Dosimeter is an authored artwork record in childrens_artwork_batch_2.json. A resident child, age seven, draws a close-up dosimeter with the needle, numbers, click-wheel, and glass rendered with surprising precision. The child says the device is what they listen to: when it clicks fast, they go inside; when it clicks slow, they can go outside. The Therapist notes that the child has drawn it the way adults draw a religious icon. This plan expands the image as inherited caution, sound made visible, and the human burden of turning an instrument into a household authority.

## 2. Story question

When a seven-year-old draws a dosimeter with the precision adults reserve for sacred objects, what does a child hear when a click becomes the difference between going inside and going outside?
