## 1. Expansion thesis

The Ration Tin Memorial is a local epitaph record. Seven empty tins form a pyramid beside the mess hall kitchen, labeled PEACHES, BEEF, TURNIPS, RICE, BEANS, MACKEREL, and MILK, with a tallow candle lit each morning. The wall remembers Anna’s sourdough, her death, the last Monday loaf, and a starter the Chef still feeds. This plan expands food memory without adding recipes or ration mechanics.

## 2. Story question

When seven labeled tins hold a candle beside a wall saying Anna’s bread smelled like the world before and her starter is still alive, what does a memorial feed after the baker is gone?
