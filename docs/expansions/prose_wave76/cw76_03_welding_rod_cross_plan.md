## 1. Expansion thesis

The Welding-Rod Cross is a local epitaph record. Two welding rods are tack-welded above a niche, stained with red hematite dust, beside an inscription honoring Oleg’s four-micron bearing tolerances, a cracked ladle, rescued apprentices, and unmatched lathe work. This plan expands the marker as craft memory without adding a metalworking tutorial or machine authority.

## 2. Story question

When crossed welding rods honor Oleg, who turned axles into water-pump shafts and pushed apprentices clear before molten iron took him, what does precision mean when no one matches it?

The handoff is complete only when the selected text, its source record, its trigger, and its audience all agree. Otherwise this remains editorial prose for review.

Keep proposed objects portable in meaning but bounded in authority. A page can be carried, a marker can be seen, and a sentence can be repeated without creating an item registry, a universal rule, or a new save section.

Let silence have more than one cause in the prose: respect, fatigue, uncertainty, refusal, distance, or the simple fact that the current record ends. Never use silence as permission to invent a secret.

A final reading should sound like a person who has lived with the place, not like a catalog that has learned to emote. Keep the nouns concrete, keep the claims attributed, and let the last sentence leave a little air around the player’s choice.

The last editorial pass should ask whether the passage could belong to someone standing near The Welding-Rod Cross with limited time, imperfect memory, and no need to impress a reviewer.
