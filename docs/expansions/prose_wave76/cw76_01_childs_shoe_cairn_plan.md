## 1. Expansion thesis

The Child’s Shoe Cairn is a local epitaph record. A small blue rubber boot is nailed to a forearm-high wooden stake, laced shut with twine, with no name spoken. The Undertaker places probable yarrow in the boot and asks anyone who knows the child’s name to speak it; nobody does. This plan expands the marker as an ethics of unnamed remembrance while preserving the record’s uncertainty.

## 2. Story question

When a blue boot is nailed to a stake with no name spoken at burial, what does a memorial hold when the wind carries the only invitation to remember?
