## 1. Expansion thesis

The Radio Antenna Memorial is a local epitaph record. Copper wire tied to a rooftop antenna hums toward a cairn, while an inscription remembers Maya’s years listening for her brother, finding an automated loop, sitting silent, then answering distress calls and saving fourteen shelters. The wire still hums and 142.5 can almost be heard. This plan expands listening as grief transformed into service without adding a radio route or frequency mechanic.

## 2. Story question

When a wire hums above a cairn and remembers Maya tracing her brother’s voice, what does listening become after grief turns toward answering strangers?
