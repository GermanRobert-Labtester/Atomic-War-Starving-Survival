## 1. Expansion thesis

The Gray-Marked Token expands a contraband record about a hand-carved soapstone mold, lead slugs, a 1978 Annex brass ration token, half an ounce of extra weight, gray marks, and a cashier named Martha whose sight has failed under sodium lights. ## 2. Story question

When a counterfeit lead slug matches a ration token’s diameter but stains the dispenser and escapes notice under sodium lights, what does value become when weight can lie?
