## Wave theme

These stories examine records and signals that carry authority farther than their witnesses: an instruction whose original conditions are gone, a custody order with no recorded ending, and a mapped carrier with no identified voice. Each plan gives the player human stakes and meaningful dialogue choices while leaving unsupported facts unresolved.

## Source and promotion notes

Expansion 71 is limited to the Crater Core map fragment called “Dead-Hand Frequency Card.” It does not supply a numeric frequency, attempt a signal, connect the card to unrelated Dead-Hand content, or claim the vault can be entered. Expansion 72 quotes the row K transfer-sheet wording but does not identify its contents, prove a trial or dissolution occurred, or treat “never claimed” as proof that property has no owner. Its camp coat is a separate present-day object.

Expansion 73 builds on the current Civil Defense Carrier fingerprint mapping to Broadcast Bunker Echo and the existing host behavior that records active-emissions telemetry and a medium-confidence map rumor. Catalog identity confidence 0.85 is not a probability that a person is speaking. The plan keeps Echo distinct from Hidden Relay Bunker and adds no route, expedition, decoded message, or faction claim.

Before implementing any plan, recheck current content authority, reachability, data consumers, save ownership, and character-name collisions. These documents change no production code or game data; documentation-only plans require no runtime test.
