## 1. Expansion thesis

The Double-Broth Stamp expands a contraband record about a neoprene vibration pad, a scalpel-carved commissary seal, a tiny chip in the capital S, a ration slip, and a double-broth promise. ## 2. Story question

When a carved stamp can turn one ration slip into double broth without a two-hour audit line, what does bureaucracy teach a hungry person to forge?
