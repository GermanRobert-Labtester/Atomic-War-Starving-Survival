# EXPANSION 129 — Keep This One, Mira

## Four recorded pieces, a separate collar, and a home that does not explain either away.

### Wave 25: What the Record Leaves

## Batch brief

**Content type:** prose-first location content plan with scene, record, conversation, and conditional revisit drafts.
**Quantity:** 18 story beats, four selectable forms per beat.
**Current location anchor:** `suburban_house` — Suburban House.
**Tone:** material, restrained, observant, and careful about the source’s unknowns.
**Canon sensitivity:** cited location and linked content records define the available facts; any other route or content reachability remains unproven.
**Scope:** editorial text only; no production code, JSON, route, quest, encounter, flag, system, or save change.

## 1. Expansion thesis

The Suburban House is already carrying two authored stories that must remain distinct. `cassette_sets.json` places the four-part Father’s Tapes set here, addressed to Mira; `final_wishes.json` sends `the_vet` to a neighbor’s house and returns a collar when the house is empty. The house description and an expedition report also differ about the roof and access. This plan deepens the listening around those existing records while refusing to combine Mira’s family with the neighbor’s dog, invent Mira’s fate, or smooth away conflicting site descriptions. The player-facing material should make the location feel inhabited by the act of observing, copying, disagreeing, and deciding what a sentence can support. The prose does not need a new system to create consequence: it can change the reader’s interpretation of a record while leaving the world state untouched.

## 2. Story question

What can a house preserve when two kinds of memory belong to different people?

## 3. Verified local anchor

`suburban_house` — Suburban House; danger 2, travel 1.0 hour, base radiation 10 rads/hour. `locations.json` describes an intact home, boarded windows, a working-draw stove, empty pantry, toolbox, and seeded garden. `exp_report_suburban_house_01` (the_courier, day 31) describes a partially collapsed roof and different access; preserve both records as separate accounts. `fathers_tapes` has four parts and `hidden_cache_location: suburban_house`. `wish_vet_neighbors_pet` separately returns the neighbor’s collar.

## 4. Fixed canon and open space

The four tapes have a fixed order and exact authored content. The Neighbor’s Pet wish belongs to `the_vet` and has its own trigger and completion: the house is empty, the bowl is dry and cracked, and the collar is carried back. The source does not establish the dog’s fate or Mira’s present location. Do not conflate the families or resolve the roof discrepancy. The location description and linked records cited in this plan are fixed anchors. Existing characters retain their authored identity and choices; any additional working role is editorial only.

## 5. Human center

The emotional center is domestic care transmitted through practical instructions, ordinary memories, an apology, and a collar from a different story. The listener’s response may change, but the recorded voice and the wish outcomes do not. A house can hold more than one history without making them one family. The emotion should come through what a person chooses to write, omit, copy, or leave unsigned rather than through a narrator announcing what the site means.

## 6. Voice and point of view

Let the tapes sound like a person speaking to one named listener. Keep any new note brief, attentive to pauses and mundane details, and clearly separate from the recording. Avoid sentimental narration that tells the player what each memory means. Keep the author’s knowledge local. A field note cannot know the history of an unnamed visitor; a later reader cannot recover a date that was never recorded; a title cannot create a route. Vary sentence length and register by document purpose, not by changing the world’s facts.

## 7. Placement and current reachability

Existing hooks include `fathers_tapes`, `wish_vet_neighbors_pet`, and `exp_report_suburban_house_01`. Any selected prose must follow the current cassette and wish owners and preserve their conditions. The location ID alone does not prove a callback or new display point. All fragments are optional editorial drafts. None is evidence that the location is currently player-reachable. If an editor later chooses any text, first verify the map/location owner, current content schema, actual consumer, and the source condition under which it can be shown.

## 8. Player agency

The player may listen to the existing four-part set, read an editorial listening note, or undertake the current vet wish under its existing condition. No extra tape, fifth part, new wish step, pet outcome, or new memory reward is proposed. Let the player decide whether to read, ask one practical question, correct a transcription, or move on. Do not create a moral-choice menu merely to make quiet prose feel interactive. The people who work at the site retain control over their words and papers.

## 9. Continuity, dignity, and safety

Do not write new words for the taped father or Mira as though they were canonical recordings. Do not declare Mira alive, dead, present, or absent. Do not connect her household to the neighbor’s dog. Avoid resolving the location/report roof and access differences or adding search instructions. Any newly introduced working role remains anonymous and editorial. Existing named characters keep their source identity and boundaries. Unknowns remain unknown; no absent person is given a biography to heighten emotion. Keep technical context descriptive and non-instructional.

## 10. Existing hooks and implementation boundary

**Existing content anchors:** the current `locations.json` record and the linked catalogs named in section 3. **Unverified:** any player-facing route, encounter, text consumer, state condition, or return trigger beyond what those records explicitly establish. **Classification:** editorial content proposal; no implementation category is claimed until an owner and consumer are verified.

This plan changes no code or game data. Do not add a parallel discovery registry or a new route solely to host these drafts. The plan is review material, not authorization to create a new gameplay authority.

## 11. Variation and selection

The four forms under each beat are alternatives, not a sequence of four required encounters. Scene drafts stage the observation; record drafts give it a plausible author and audience; conversation fragments expose a practical disagreement; revisit vignettes let a later reader recognize changed wording. Select only the forms that fit an existing content owner. Shorten freely while retaining the source facts and each fragment’s boundary.

## 12. Editorial rubric

A selected fragment should answer who made it, why they made it, who might read it, and what the author cannot know. Keep the physical object specific enough to belong to this location. The line should do practical work before it carries theme. Avoid exposition that a worker would not say aloud, repeated catastrophe language, unearned revelation, and duplicated record wording.
