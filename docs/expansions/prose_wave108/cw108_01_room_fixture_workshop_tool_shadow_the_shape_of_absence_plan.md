## 1. Expansion thesis

The Shape of Absence expands the workshop fixture about a painted tool silhouette, a missing tool with no surrounding gap, a shadow older than the current crew, and nobody matching a tool to it. The plan deepens inherited craft and unanswered ownership without adding tool catalogs, repair guidance, theft investigations, workshop systems, or a hidden item.

## 2. Story question

When a painted tool silhouette remains on a workshop pegboard with no tool and no gap around it, what does the shape remember that the inventory never could?

**01 — source fidelity.** The anchor remains room_fixture_workshop_tool_shadow/the_shape_of_absence (The Tool Shadow). **02 — material attention.** The strongest details in this bank are physical: a threshold, a surface, a line of sight, a document, a marker, a schedule, or a room that still has its old shape. Keep those details ordinary enough to be believed and specific enough to distinguish The Tool Shadow from another location.

**03 — voice separation.** The three roles in this plan do different work. One notices the immediate object, one protects the record’s limits, and one carries the emotional consequence. Do not let all three speak in the same polished register or give one person the authority of the whole The Tool Shadow record.

**04 — player space.** The player is allowed to observe before deciding what the observation means. A pause, refusal, question, or incomplete answer can be the passage’s consequence. Do not turn every emotional beat into a prompt, reward, relationship delta, journal flag, or measurable improvement.

**05 — return scale.** A return to The Tool Shadow should make the earlier passage newly legible, not rewrite it. **12 — emotional movement.** The movement through The Tool Shadow should be felt as a change in attention. Nothing larger is required for the passage to matter.

**14 — handoff.** If a content owner accepts a passage, preserve its attribution and keep the owner’s existing state, save, and routing seams. Keep proposed objects portable in meaning but bounded in authority. A page can be carried, a marker can be seen, and a sentence can be repeated without creating an item registry, a universal rule, or a new save section.
