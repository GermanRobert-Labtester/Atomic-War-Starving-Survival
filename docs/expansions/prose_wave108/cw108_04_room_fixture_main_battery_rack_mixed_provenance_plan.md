## 1. Expansion thesis

Mixed Provenance expands the main-room battery fixture about eight cells in two rows, an original top row, a bottom row added from an emergency lighting bank, pre-war cells, a post-Exchange retrofit, mixed provenance, and visible capacity difference. ## 2. Story question
