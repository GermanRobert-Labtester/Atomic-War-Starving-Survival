## 1. Expansion thesis

The 129.6 broadcast is a stale narrative beacon tied to a suburban house. Across five days a mechanical tone repeats every thirty seconds, wind moves through an open door, a metallic scrape appears and stops, the battery weakens, and the signal ends with one final tone.
