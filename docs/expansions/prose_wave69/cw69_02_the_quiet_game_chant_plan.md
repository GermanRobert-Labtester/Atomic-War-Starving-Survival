## 1. Expansion thesis

The Quiet Game Chant is a local children’s folklore record in childrens_folklore_expansion.json. Children in Ward C chant in whispers before the boots come: quiet like the mouse in the wall, do not breathe or speak, the weak are strong, and quiet is the game and the seek. The children who giggle lose; those who do not giggle win. They do not know why the boots come, but know the quiet game. This plan expands the chant as a child-made container for uncertainty, keeping the boots, the unknown reason, and the quiet practice bounded.

## 2. Story question

When children whisper that quiet is the game and the game is the seek before boots walk Ward C, what kind of preparation can be remembered without explaining the danger that started it?
