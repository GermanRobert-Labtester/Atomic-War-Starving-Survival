## 1. Expansion thesis

The Sun Drawing Game is a local children’s folklore record in childrens_folklore_expansion.json. Children chant while drawing the sun with chalk on the shelter yard wall: draw the sun with a face, the face is smiling, the sun is not, the sun is gone but the face remains. The teacher told them the sun has no face, yet the children draw one anyway. The record says they know the difference and do not explain it. This plan expands the game as shared make-believe, borrowed outside knowledge, and the intelligence of keeping an image separate from its subject.

## 2. Story question

When children draw a smiling face on a sun they have been told has no face, what does a chalk image know when the children know the difference between the drawing and the thing?
