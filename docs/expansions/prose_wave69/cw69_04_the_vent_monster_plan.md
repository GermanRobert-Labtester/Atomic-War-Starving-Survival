## 1. Expansion thesis

The Vent Monster is a local children’s folklore record in childrens_folklore_expansion.json. Older children whisper that a monster lives in the big pipe, hums when lights go out, and sounds like singing. The chant repeatedly says the monster is not real, is the air, and is in your head, while ending with a warning not to put an ear on the pipe too long. Younger children do it anyway, and the pipe becomes the monster in their retelling. This plan expands the folklore as a boundary story about sound and imagination without turning it into a ventilation or medical warning system.

## 2. Story question

When older children whisper that the vent monster is not real, is the air, and still gets into your head if you listen too long, what does a bedtime warning do when the pipe remains only a pipe?
