## A prose-first ecology-and-migration story collection about people, memory, and what the record cannot settle.

## 1. Expansion thesis

The Generator Heart is a local children’s folklore record in childrens_folklore_expansion.json. A child tells a recursive story that the generator has a heart, the heart is the hum, the engineer is the doctor, oil is medicine, and a new brass bone comes from a clock. The child held the lamp while visiting the boiler room and saw the clock become the bone. The story says the generator is alive because the heart is the hum. This plan expands the tale as a child’s translation of maintenance, keeping the engineer’s work and the machine’s metaphor distinct.

## 2. Story question

When a child who visited the boiler room says the generator has a heart, the engineer is a doctor, oil is medicine, and brass is a bone from a clock, what does a machine become in a story built from observed work?
