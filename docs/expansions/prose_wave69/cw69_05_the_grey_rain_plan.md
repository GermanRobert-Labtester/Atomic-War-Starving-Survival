## 1. Expansion thesis

The Grey Rain is a local children’s folklore record in childrens_folklore_expansion.json. A child of nine says rain was clear before, that the sky no longer cries, and that rain, grey, ash, and sky are one another. The child has never seen clear rain; they know the rain is grey, and the record repeats that grey is the knowing. This plan expands the account as inherited weather, recursive language, and the limits of describing a past no one in the room has witnessed.

## 2. Story question

When a nine-year-old who has never seen clear rain says the grey is the sky, the ash, and the knowing, what does a child inherit when the before exists only in other people’s words?

The source supports memory, atmosphere, and the dignity of partial experience. It does not establish climate, ash exposure, rain quality, weather prediction, or a child belief system. Keep the grey as a child’s knowing, keep clear rain in the realm of testimony, and let the recursive lines remain a form of thought rather than a puzzle to solve.
