## 1. Expansion thesis

The Water Committee is a conditional, active faction in the crossing region. It wants filter media and sanitation salts; it offers clean-water rights, well-head access, and quality certification. Its signature warns that the sand filter does not care about politics and that tainted water passes its danger to everyone. Its access rule requires sediment inspection, contributions of clean filter charcoal or chlorine-titration chits, and respect for closed filtration beds. This bank stays with public trust, technical language, and the human fear behind a tap, without turning the committee into a water-treatment manual.

## 2. Story question

When a committee offers clean-water rights and quality certification in exchange for filter media and sanitation salts, how does a town make a public promise about water without making chemistry a substitute for trust?
