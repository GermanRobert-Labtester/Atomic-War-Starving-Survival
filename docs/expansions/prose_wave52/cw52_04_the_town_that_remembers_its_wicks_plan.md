## 1. Expansion thesis

The Lamplighters are a conditional, active faction in the crossing region. They want fuel stores and reflector glass; they offer street lighting, night watch escort, and route visibility. Their signature says a dark street is an argument someone decided to settle with iron, and their access rule ties communal lighting to a maintenance pool while warning that tampering or siphoning leaves a quarter dark. This bank explores light as memory, labor, and contested safety without turning the faction into an escort or lighting system specification.

## 2. Story question

When a faction offers light, night watch escort, and route visibility in exchange for fuel and reflector glass, how does a town decide whether darkness is a public failure or a shared limit?
