## 1. Expansion thesis

The Registry Dispute places two convoy lineages against one ledger at Stallrow. Without arbitration, supply wagons remain impounded on the causeway. The quest asks the player to review manifests and watermarked seals, summon three neutral backers, and deliver a final ruling at the viaduct gate. Its choices uphold the elder lineage under the charter or seize the disputed cargo for the crossing arsenal. This bank treats paperwork as a pressure on living people and keeps the escalation branch morally legible without making adjudication a mechanical tutorial.

## 2. Story question

When two convoy lineages claim the same registration ledger and supply wagons wait on a causeway, can a ruling restore movement without turning inheritance into ownership by force?
