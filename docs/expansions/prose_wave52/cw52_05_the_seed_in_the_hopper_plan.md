## 1. Expansion thesis

The Granary Wardens are a conditional, active faction in the crossing region. They want staple grain and burlap sacks; they offer ration distribution, emergency grain draw, and spoilage inspection. Their signature says the grain-crib lock is not to keep hunger out but to ensure seed remains in the hopper ninety days from now. Their access rule requires minuted ration allotments, intact dry sacks, and no concealed spoilage. This bank explores the moral weight of reserve, inspection, and the people who stand in line without making the faction a food-management tutorial.

## 2. Story question

When a faction guards grain so seed remains ninety days from now, how can a settlement honor ration allotments without making hunger wait politely outside the lock?
