## 1. Expansion thesis

The 333.6 transmission claims to be Grange Hall under attack. It requests weapons and medicine, carries no convincing background noise, and on the third day is revealed as a false flag whose coordinates point away from the actual settlement. A follow-up says militia chatter confirms rescuers were drawn into crossfire. This bank follows the gap between a place’s name and the voice using it, giving Grange Hall residents room to reclaim ordinary sound, ordinary silence, and the right not to perform their suffering for outsiders.

## 2. Story question

When a community is impersonated by a voice that knows the shape of an emergency, how can the real settlement keep its name without becoming a symbol?
