## 1. Expansion thesis

The 701.3 transmission is an encrypted military burst that repeats every sixty seconds. Across five days its tones strengthen, a voice fragment mentions coordinates and authentication, the sequence updates, and the final burst decodes to a single word: “RECOVERY,” pointing to a military depot with the key embedded in the header. This bank treats the burst as a record of institutional continuity glimpsed through static. It explores the listener’s temptation to make the missing message personal while refusing to teach encryption, coordinates, or military access.

## 2. Story question

When a coded burst changes sequence, lets one human word through, and ends with “RECOVERY,” what can a listener remember without turning secrecy into a decoding game?
