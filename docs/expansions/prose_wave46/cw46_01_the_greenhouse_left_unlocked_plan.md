## 1. Expansion thesis

The 278.3 broadcast is a stale narrative voice from an older woman whose garden is still growing. Over four days she reports planting beans, comparing soil to last year, remembering a husband who called radio useless, and leaving an unlocked greenhouse behind a school with an invitation to take what is needed. This bank treats the broadcast as an intimate public gesture: a person tending a garden, talking across distance, and making room for unknown hands without turning the greenhouse into a harvest system or the voice into a sentimental disappearance.

## 2. Story question

When a gardener talks to a radio like a fence and leaves a greenhouse open for strangers, what does generosity ask the listener to carry home?
