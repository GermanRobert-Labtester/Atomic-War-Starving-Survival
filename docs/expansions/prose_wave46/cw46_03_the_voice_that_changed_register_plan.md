## 1. Expansion thesis

The 410.7 broadcast begins as a desperate request for help finding a sister in a warehouse. It offers medicine and food, asks responders to bring weapons, and on the third day reveals an adult imitating a boy; the promised supplies are bait and the warehouse is a holding pen. The follow-up records extortionists boasting over an intercepted rescue party. This bank focuses on stolen family language, the violence of impersonation, and the survivors who must hear a childlike plea without turning it into a test of cleverness.

## 2. Story question
