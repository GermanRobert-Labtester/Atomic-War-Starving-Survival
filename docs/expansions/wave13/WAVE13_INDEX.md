## Wave theme

These stories follow the wording people carry forward from incomplete records: an emergency label, a remembered catalog date, and a stock claim. Each plan gives the player authored scenes, distinct voices, in-world documents, and meaningful dialogue choices. Their outcomes are narrative records and relationships, not new systems.

## Source and promotion notes

The plans use current location, expedition, world-map, and damaged-map-zone anchors. They keep separate authorities distinct: the Municipal Seed Vault’s map-zone fragment describes an old library basement while its location description says courthouse basement; the unrelated seed-library records are not merged into that story. Expansion 69’s 18 April catalog cover is a proposed authored insert, not verified source content, and remains conditional on content-owner review. Expansion 70 quotes CHIMERA-7-4-9 from the manifest but does not treat it as a working credential or combine it with the separate water-damaged elevator digits.

Before implementing any plan, recheck current content authority, route reachability, data consumers, save ownership, and character-name collisions. These documents do not change production code or data.
