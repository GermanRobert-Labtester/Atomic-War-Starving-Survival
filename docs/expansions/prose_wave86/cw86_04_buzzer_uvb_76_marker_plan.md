## 1. Expansion thesis

The Carrier Drops expands a local record about a 25-tone-per-minute buzzer on 4625 kHz, sixteen years of open frequency, Day 3,412 at 04:12, a raspy voice, an alphanumeric phrase, and the buzzer returning. The plan treats interruption as a shock in a listening community without adding frequencies, signal analysis, codebreaking, or emergency-response mechanics.

## 2. Story question

When a sixteen-year buzzer loses its carrier for one minute and a rasping voice inserts a name-like string, what do listeners owe an interruption that may be a warning, a mistake, or the only human sentence left?
