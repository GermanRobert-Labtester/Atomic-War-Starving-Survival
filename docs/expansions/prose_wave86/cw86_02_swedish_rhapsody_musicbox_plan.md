## 1. Expansion thesis

The Child in the Spring expands a local record about a high metallic music-box chime, a winding clockwork spring, a child’s voice, five-figure groups, absolute silence, and dwellers unsure whether the recording is forty years old or yesterday. The plan treats uncertainty as shared room drama without adding audio restoration, age verification, decoding, or radio mechanics.

## 2. Story question

When a mechanical chime speeds up as its spring winds down and a child’s voice reads figures into a silent radio room, how does a listener decide whether the past is speaking or merely repeating?
