## 1. Expansion thesis

The Four-Hour Folk Tune expands a local cipher-station record about an electronic music box, an eighteenth-century folk tune, a synthesized female voice, five-figure groups, a coastal transmitter, and a decaying RTG. ## 2. Story question

When a coastal transmitter repeats two bars of an old folk tune before a synthetic woman recites five-figure groups, what do listeners inherit: a message, a ritual, or the shape of a world that still keeps time without them?
