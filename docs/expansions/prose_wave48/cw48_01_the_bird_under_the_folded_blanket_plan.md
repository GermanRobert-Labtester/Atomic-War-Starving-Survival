## 1. Expansion thesis

The Missing Keepsake event describes a carved wooden bird missing from the third bunk for three shifts. The child who carved it has not noticed; the adult whose pillow it stood against has, and begins folding the blanket over the place where it used to stand. The corridor knows without asking. The authored choices return the bird to the bunk or leave it with the adult who needed it more. This bank follows the quiet argument between making, memory, need, and the social pressure of everyone pretending not to look.

## 2. Story question

When a carved bird disappears from a child’s bunk and an adult folds a blanket over the empty place, what does a community owe a keepsake it cannot agree who should hold?
