## 1. Expansion thesis

The Bio-Remediation Fungal Mat Assay event finds an unusual black-and-gold fungal mat in mineral sludge inside a damp concrete overflow ditch. Preliminary dosimeter sweeps say the mycelium is absorbing and concentrating radioactive isotopes from runoff, leaving the outflow significantly cleaner. This bank follows the tension between discovery and use: a living process can be hopeful without being safe, understood, or ready to become infrastructure.
