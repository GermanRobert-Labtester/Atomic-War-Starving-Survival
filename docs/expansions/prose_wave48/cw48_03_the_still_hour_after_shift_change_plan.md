## 1. Expansion thesis

The Still Hour event describes a quiet observance in a dormitory corner after shift change. Adherents breathe with the intake pulse and recite former dwellers’ names; nerves the sedatives did not reach have begun settling there, and the infirmary has noticed without objecting. The existing choices permit the observance or enforce lights out. This bank explores ritual as a practical form of attention, the limits of medical language, and the way a corridor can become still without becoming empty.
