## 1. Expansion thesis

Quiet Is Full expands the Quiet House Elder’s profile about a house where quiet is the rule, silence is different, and people come because quiet is full. The plan deepens collective listening and the ethics of a refuge without adding noise meters, access rules, sanctuary mechanics, or social-control systems.

## 2. Story question

When the elder says quiet is not silence because quiet is full of people, how can a house protect a shared stillness without turning absence into a rule no one can question?
