# EXPANSION CW91-06 — The Not-Writing

## A prose-first silence-and-passage story collection about people, absence, and what the record cannot safely authorize.

### Prose Wave 91: Silence, Passage, and the Names We Carry

## Batch brief

**Content type:** original game-content proposal with scene, diegetic-record, conversation, and conditional-return drafts.
**Content bank:** 48 distinct beat pairings, each with four alternative authored forms (192 candidate passages).
**Current local anchor:** npc_smuggler — The Smuggler, the Not-Writing.
**Tone:** grounded, human, restrained, material, and careful about what a damaged record can prove.
**Scope:** game content only; no production code, JSON data, route, quest, flag, simulation, or save changes.

## 1. Expansion thesis

The Not-Writing expands the Smuggler’s profile about moving goods between shelters, carrying extra medicine, a letter, and a child’s toy through checkpoints and dark roads, and treating omission as trade. The plan deepens secrecy, care, and institutional blind spots without adding contraband routes, evasion tactics, inventory mechanics, or security procedures.

## 2. Story question

When a smuggler moves medicine, a letter, and a child’s toy through forms that do not allow them, what does not-writing protect when the shelters need what they refuse to name?

## 5. Human center

The emotional center is the cost of carrying what a form cannot admit. The smuggler’s secrecy can protect a child or a patient while also denying others the chance to know what was risked. Not-writing is care, danger, and a refusal to let paperwork become the whole truth.

## 6. Voice and point of view

- **Smuggler:** The reader hears movement described as identity and omission described as a burden rather than a trick.
- **Recipient:** The witness receives something the form would have rejected and must decide how much gratitude can be spoken.
- **Checkpoint reader:** The later echo sees the blank space where a lawful record would have expected a name.

Each speaker knows only what the cited source, their own proposed observation, or an explicitly attributed memory could give them. No proposed voice represents a whole faction or settlement.

## 11. Narrative sequence

The six movements below organize an editorial bank; they are not six required visits or a fixed quest chain. Beat order may change if the existing consumer calls for a shorter or non-linear presentation.

### Movement 1: Between shelters

The first movement hears movement begin in the space between places. It does not map a road or name a checkpoint. The movement begins with the movement. It stays within the current source boundary and lets the people in the room disagree about meaning without creating a new rule.


### Movement 2: Forms and blanks

The second movement notices what a form cannot admit. No evasion tactic is described. The movement begins with the boundary. It stays within the current source boundary and lets the people in the room disagree about meaning without creating a new rule.


### Movement 3: The medicine

### Movement 4: The letter

### Movement 5: The toy

The fifth movement lets a child’s toy make the trade human. The child remains unnamed. The movement begins with the tenderness. It stays within the current source boundary and lets the people in the room disagree about meaning without creating a new rule.


### Movement 6: Not-writing

The sixth movement leaves omission as care and cost at once. The smuggler remains a person, not a system. The movement begins with the exposure. It stays within the current source boundary and lets the people in the room disagree about meaning without creating a new rule.

### Editorial scale notes

**01 — source fidelity.** The anchor remains npc_smuggler (The Smuggler, the Not-Writing). Every proposed sentence must stay attached to the cited local record, and every inference must remain visibly an inference. The source can support a human moment without supporting a new mechanic, route, authority, or outcome.

**02 — material attention.** The strongest details in this bank are physical: a boundary, a surface, a line of sight, a document, a marker, a schedule, or a room that still has its old shape. Keep those details ordinary enough to be believed and specific enough to distinguish The Smuggler, the Not-Writing from another location.

**03 — voice separation.** The three roles in this plan do different work. One notices the immediate object, one protects the record’s limits, and one carries the emotional consequence. Do not let all three speak in the same polished register or give one person the authority of the whole The Smuggler, the Not-Writing record.

**04 — player space.** The player is allowed to observe before deciding what the observation means. A pause, refusal, question, or incomplete answer can be the passage’s consequence. Do not turn every emotional beat into a prompt, reward, relationship delta, journal flag, or measurable improvement.

**05 — return scale.** A return to The Smuggler, the Not-Writing should make the earlier passage newly legible, not rewrite it. The later reader may notice a different word, remember a different cost, or decline to repeat the account. The catalog facts remain stable unless a current authorized system says otherwise.

**06 — uncertainty.** Uncertainty is authored content here. It is not a hole that needs to be filled with a hidden culprit, a technical explanation, a secret route, or an invented survivor. When a source leaves a name, cause, or outcome open, the prose can make that openness felt without pretending to solve it.

**07 — dignity.** People associated with The Smuggler, the Not-Writing should not be reduced to the hazard, policy, machine, or record that surrounds them. Let a person have a practical task, a private limit, a bad day, a memory that is incomplete, or a reason not to answer. Keep the human center larger than the prop.

**08 — content scale.** The 48-beat bank is deliberately editorial. A production owner may select one passage, several passages, or none. The size of this document is a writing resource, not a request to implement every candidate or to create a parallel narrative registry.

**09 — continuity check.** Before placement, compare this bank against newer data, especially records that mention npc_smuggler or the surrounding region. Confirm names, truth classes, branch outcomes, and display ownership. A matching phrase is not proof that a passage remains current.

**10 — hazard boundary.** The plan’s boundary is explicit: Do not provide smuggling, checkpoint evasion, concealment, medicine, transport, route, contraband, security, or document-forgery instructions. Do not add an inventory route, stealth mechanic, checkpoint system, delivery quest, hidden ledger, or new smuggler authority. Preserve the exact profile and its limits. Keep that limit visible in scene language. A character may be afraid, cautious, angry, or wrong without the prose becoming an operational guide.

**11 — diegetic surface.** A record, notice, conversation, or scene should look like something a survivor could encounter in the existing content owner. It may be a torn page, a spoken recollection, a label, a pause at a threshold, or a later sentence repeated with one word corrected. It should not look like a system specification inside the fiction.

**12 — emotional movement.** The movement through The Smuggler, the Not-Writing should be felt as a change in attention. The player begins by seeing an object or condition, then hears a person attach meaning to it, then decides whether to carry that meaning forward. Nothing larger is required for the passage to matter.

**13 — open ending.** Close on a human image rather than a verdict. A hand lowers, a page remains folded, a name is repeated once, a room continues its ordinary work, or a landmark stays where it was. The ending should leave room for the player and the current canon to do the next piece of work.

**14 — handoff.** If a content owner accepts a passage, preserve its attribution and keep the owner’s existing state, save, and routing seams. The handoff is complete only when the selected text, its source record, its trigger, and its audience all agree. Otherwise this remains editorial prose for review.

The proposed bank keeps returning to The Smuggler, the Not-Writing because repetition is part of survival writing. A person sees the same surface twice and notices a different cost. That change belongs to attention, not to an invented state.

A selected passage may mention npc_smuggler by its display name, by a local nickname already present in the source, or by an ordinary physical detail. Do not add a second identifier merely to make the prose sound larger.

The strongest callback is often a correction. Someone says a word too confidently, another person supplies the source’s narrower wording, and the room continues. That is enough movement for a scene whose subject is memory.

Keep proposed objects portable in meaning but bounded in authority. A page can be carried, a marker can be seen, and a sentence can be repeated without creating an item registry, a universal rule, or a new save section.

Let silence have more than one cause in the prose: respect, fatigue, uncertainty, refusal, distance, or the simple fact that the current record ends. Never use silence as permission to invent a secret.

Where a source names a hazard, write the people’s relationship to that hazard rather than a method for defeating it. Fear, caution, grief, and disagreement are content; operational guidance belongs to an authorized system owner, not this bank.

Where a source names an institution, show its paperwork and its consequences without making the institution omniscient. A form can be current and still miss a person. A schedule can be obeyed and still be unjust.

The handoff question is simple: can a reviewer point from each important claim to a current source and from each proposed passage to an existing consumer? If either answer is no, keep the passage in editorial review.

The document’s length does not widen the canon. It gives the writer room to choose among small moments at The Smuggler, the Not-Writing while keeping the source boundary visible in every form. A longer bank is useful only when its alternatives remain honest and distinct.

A final reading should sound like a person who has lived with the place, not like a catalog that has learned to emote. Keep the nouns concrete, keep the claims attributed, and let the last sentence leave a little air around the player’s choice.

The last editorial pass should ask whether the passage could belong to someone standing near The Smuggler, the Not-Writing with limited time, imperfect memory, and no need to impress a reviewer. If the answer is yes, the bank has done its work: it has made a place available for play without pretending the place belongs to the plan.

The plan can end with the ordinary world still in motion: weather, work, a person walking back toward a settlement, or a room waiting for its next reader. The content has consequence because somebody noticed and somebody else chose what to do with that notice.

A clean handoff leaves the source visible, the voice distinct, and the ending quiet enough to be revised if the canon moves.
