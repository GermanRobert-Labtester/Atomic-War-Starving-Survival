# EXPANSION CW60-01 — The Two Chalk Knuckles

## A prose-first ecology-and-migration story collection about people, memory, and what the record cannot settle.

### Prose Wave 60: Thresholds, Marks, and Listening

## 1. Expansion thesis

Two Chalk Knuckles by the Inner Dog is a local graffiti posting triggered by friction_spoken_name_blame, available from day 3 with weight 1.0. It records two vertical chalk slashes above the dog-leg airlock handle, a Saturday swab detail that cleans them, a Sunday morning redraw, and the shared phrase that two taps come before stepping into the rubber suit. Nobody asks who holds the chalk. This plan expands the posting as a story about anonymous ritual, maintenance, and the comfort of repeating a gesture without turning it into an instruction.

## 2. Story question

When two chalk slashes above an inner dog-leg airlock handle are scrubbed on Saturday and returned by Sunday, what does a small ritual remember about the people who must cross it?
