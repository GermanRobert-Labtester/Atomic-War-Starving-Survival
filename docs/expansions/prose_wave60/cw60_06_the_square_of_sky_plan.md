## 1. Expansion thesis

A Square of Sky on Level 3 is a local graffiti posting triggered by friction_ritual_fuel_curtailed, available from day 12 with weight 0.9. It records a neat rectangle on the blank concrete bulkhead of Dormitory B, drawn with a hoarded blue grease pencil, containing two clouds shaped like potato sacks and a yellow circle. A taped note says Do not paint over. Teacher approved. This plan treats the false window as a shared exercise in keeping an outside image alive inside a sealed room, without adding a classroom, weather, or visual simulation system.

## 2. Story question

When a blue grease-pencil window on a concrete bulkhead shows potato-sack clouds and a yellow circle, and a note says teacher approved, what kind of outside can a dormitory keep without pretending the wall has opened?
