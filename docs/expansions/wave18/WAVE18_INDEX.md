## Wave theme

A mark earns trust when the person using it can see its author, limit, and consequence. Anete’s checks belong beside the feeder; Rika’s count does not grant an address; Joren’s blank crew field is not an answer; Pavel’s map carries the limit of its source; Marek’s margin records a disagreement without pretending to settle it.

## Archive and source boundaries

All content is proposed and subject to review against live data before implementation. The relevant encounter records have no fixed `requiredLocationId`, so they do not prove a guaranteed route or new map connection. Hazardous locations remain hazardous; no plan adds procedure or access instructions.

Expansion 06 already develops Harven/Voss-era material at Checkpoint Gamma. Plan 86 focuses on Marek’s own posted terms and current arc, and leaves that prior material untouched. Plan 85 preserves the identity and fate of Pavel’s absent travelers as unresolved. The five plans change no production code, gameplay data, expansion master catalog, or integration ledger. No runtime tests apply to these documentation-only plans.

Character counts are Unicode character counts from the saved Markdown files, including whitespace and punctuation.
