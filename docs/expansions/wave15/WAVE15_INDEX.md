## Wave theme

Each story asks what a count or instruction can responsibly carry. The press sign names a product boundary; the mess portion measures a serving but cannot settle the question of a private reserve; Lina's count is accurate for one queue at one moment, not a permanent total or a child's duty to manage adults. The characters keep authority over their own work and choices.

## Source and promotion notes

Expansion 74's location, expedition, character, and lore facts come from current data. The exact four-word note, visitors, labels, and dialogue are proposed prose, not existing canon. The plan does not explain Ruth's silence or turn the note into a personal revelation.

Expansion 75 expands the existing “Which Kind of Cook” quest. It preserves arvo_amnesty and arvo_expose, the current consequences, the quartermaster and expelled-cook states, and the unresolved state. All additional scenes and dialogue are proposed content; no inventory, ration, or governance authority is added.

Expansion 76 expands the existing Lina quest chain and encounter. It preserves the current take-in/supplies choices, lesson choices, apprenticeship choice, and NPC states. The baths are treated as a former sleeping shelter and a present queue location only where existing content supports it. Any dose or medical text remains subject to the current medical content owner.

These are documentation-only plans, not implementation claims. Before implementation, recheck current data, character-state reachability, quest and journal owners, route access, and all shared save or inventory seams. No production code or game data is changed by this wave.
