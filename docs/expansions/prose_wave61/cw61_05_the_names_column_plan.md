## 1. Expansion thesis

The Names Column, by the Ladder is a local graffiti posting triggered by friction_work_shirker_accusation, available from day 40 with weight 0.9. It says somebody wrote SHIRKER by the ladder in chalk, that chalk wipes while the wall does not, and asks readers to remember the last shift the accused pulled: four hours in the sump, no rotation, no complaint logged. It closes by calling the wall the only witness the community can afford. This plan explores accusation, labor memory, and the difference between a visible label and an unrecorded burden without adding a work roster or disciplinary system.

## 2. Story question

When someone writes SHIRKER by the ladder, chalk can be wiped but the wall remembers four hours in the sump with no rotation and no complaint logged, who is a wall allowed to accuse?
