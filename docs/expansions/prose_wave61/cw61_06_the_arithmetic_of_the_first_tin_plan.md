## 1. Expansion thesis

The Arithmetic of the First Tin is a local graffiti posting triggered by ration_first_tin_logged, available from day 26 with weight 1.0. It says the first tin goes to the pump crew, that the pump crew has not eaten first since the storms, and asks readers to do the math before shouting. It calls the priority maintenance rather than favoritism and asks that explanation to be written somewhere shouting can read it. This plan expands the posting as a debate about visible work, hunger, and the social meaning of an allocation without adding a ration calculator or priority rule.

## 2. Story question

When the first tin goes to the pump crew who have not eaten first since the storms, and the wall calls that maintenance rather than favoritism, how can a ration argument make a person visible without becoming a formula?
