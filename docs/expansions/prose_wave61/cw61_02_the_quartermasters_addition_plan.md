## 1. Expansion thesis

The Quartermaster’s Addition is a local graffiti posting triggered by friction_radio_dial_argument, available from day 35 with weight 0.8. Its text says the dial costs batteries, batteries cost walks, walks cost people, and that before anyone tunes anything they should write what they hope to hear on the board beside the ration tally. It closes by declaring hope a consumable that is now inventoried. This plan treats the posting as a dry account of opportunity cost and emotional bookkeeping, without adding a resource ledger or radio procedure.

## 2. Story question

When the dial costs batteries, batteries cost walks, walks cost people, and hope is written beside a ration tally as an inventoried consumable, what does a community owe its own wanting?
