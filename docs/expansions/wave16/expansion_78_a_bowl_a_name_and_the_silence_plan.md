# EXPANSION 78 — A BOWL, A NAME, AND THE SILENCE

## A prose-first game-content expansion for Elder Sava’s refugee camp, its daily rota, and the work of keeping memory from becoming a weapon.

### Wave 16: Marks That Let Others Pass

## 1. Creative promise

This expansion gives the camp ordinary days around its current story beats. It does not turn Sava’s practice into a faith test, faction conversion, or puzzle about what she secretly believes. The meal is food; the naming is done by people who remember; the silence is not a dramatic cue for the player to fill. Care appears in material actions: the fire is banked, the watch relieved, the bowl washed, and the name pronounced correctly. This is an authored content pack, not a feature roadmap. It supplies proposed playable prose, dialogue, field notes, and repeat-visit aftermath around current ASHFALL data. It does not claim any new line or scene is already in the game.

## 2. One-sentence story

Elder Sava’s refugee camp, its daily rota, and the work of keeping memory from becoming a weapon. The player learns that a mark earns trust only when its limits remain visible to the person who has to use it.

## 3. Verified local anchor

The Forward Roster Camp is a dozen tents and a salvaged container between the Exchange and the Garrison checkpoint. Its watch schedule is chalked on the container door, and the fire pit sits equidistant from two lights. Elder Sava is a Hearth Elder who keeps the meal, the naming of the dead, and the silence after. Her first quest lets the player stand the fence, help move the camp, or walk on. A later quest asks whether she should mediate a quarrel between camps or whether the hours spreading in her name should be quietly funded. Her late arc distinguishes mediator, rota keeper, and withdrawn elder. The current narrative encounter has no fixed location restriction in its record. The character’s authored location and quest chain are the narrative anchor; an encounter row alone does not prove a guaranteed route or new map connection.

## 4. New prose and fixed canon

The current character, location, quest, encounter, and arc facts above are fixed. Each new visitor, phrase, work slip, journal fragment, staging detail, and repeat visit below is a proposal. Recheck each proposal against current data before implementation. This plan changes no resource yield, faction ownership, route access, character death, or quest choice ID.

## 5. The emotional center

A bowl is set down with its rim facing the player. Sava reads names from a page whose corner is held by a nail. A second pot warms behind her; the watch schedule is chalked on the container door. A fire appears on the ridge where the camp has no fire. Sava pauses long enough to ask whether the player has come to stand the fence, help the camp move, or keep walking. She offers the bowl before the answer and does not treat it as a vote. This small scene is the scale for the pack. The player can help, misunderstand, ask, or leave; the story does not confuse closeness with permission. The anchor character remains a worker with a life outside the player’s visit.

## 6. Dialogue sample

Proposed exchange: The player asks whether they pray here. Sava sets another bowl on the table and answers that they eat here. The player says that is not an answer. Sava says it is the answer she has. The player asks about the names and Sava answers: We say them while we can. The player asks what comes after. Sava replies: The part where nobody has to tell me what to feel. She turns the page so the next reader can see it. Player responses may be shorter or silent. The scene still works if the visitor says nothing. A pause does not imply that a hidden option waits behind it.

## 7. The current character arc

Open with the bowl and the choice already present in the encounter. Follow the selected answer through the camp rota and a second camp’s interpretation of it. Stage one mediation or one quiet funding conversation according to the existing branch. End with the player attending the verdict-free hour as a worker. No belief meter or recruitment system is proposed. Keep first, evolved, and late states distinct. Do not show a late-state outcome before its required choice or quest completion. Do not create another hidden ending. If a player has not met the character, do not imply they have been waiting specifically for the player.

## 8. Point of view and scene grammar

The narration stays close to what the visitor can verify: the sound a part makes, where a line is marked, what remains on a page after correction, who chooses to wait, and who has begun the next task. It does not report another person’s private thoughts as fact. If a proposal gives someone a feeling, that feeling belongs in permitted speech or a personal record.

## 9. Cast and voice

Sava’s voice is practical and unhurried. Camp residents can disagree about a fence or a move without becoming a faceless flock. Each camp keeps its own history and chores; neither one gets reduced to the single sentence it quotes from Sava. She can mediate an argument without becoming the owner of either camp.
## 10. Choice discipline

The current quest choices are the only major branches in this plan. Small responses may change the next line or which insert appears, but do not introduce a second hidden branch structure. The player can disagree, ask for clarification, or leave. Do not imply they chose an option absent from current data.

## 11. Human consequences without scorekeeping

Consequences appear in who returns, which wording changes, which task continues, and whether a boundary is respected. Do not invent a morale bonus, relationship meter, resource fee, or reputation effect. Mechanical consequences would require a separate evidence-based design decision.

## 12. Proposed content bank

The inserts below are candidate game text, not a promise that every entry will ship. A production pass should select, sequence, and shorten them to fit the current encounter and character-state owners. Each candidate keeps a particular voice, an observable action, and a branch-sensitive aftermath. Remove any insert that conflicts with the live state or duplicates an existing authored line.
