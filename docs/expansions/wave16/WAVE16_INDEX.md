## Wave theme

A mark earns trust only when its limits remain visible to the person who must use it. Kaspar’s fractions are estimates, Sava’s rota is corrigible, Emil’s notebook separates measurement from interpretation, Niko keeps his route in memory and defines the safe section, and Veda closes a line when its paint cannot be read.

## Archive and source boundaries

These plans are documentation-only proposals; their candidate text is not current canon until reviewed and accepted by the content owner. The recurring character encounters have no fixed location restriction in their current records, so their presence does not prove a guaranteed route or new map connection.

Expansion 03 already proposes Standing Record content at the Bus Reversal Loop and the Kittiwake. Plans 80 and 81 explicitly leave those earlier location concepts untouched: Niko’s plan stays on his independent corridor and employment arc, while Veda’s plan stays on her trap circuit and does not reinterpret the survey log. The other three plans use distinct character anchors from this wave’s recent content.

Character counts are Unicode character counts from the plan files. This wave changes no production code, gameplay data, expansion master catalog, or integration ledger. No runtime tests apply to these documentation-only plans.
