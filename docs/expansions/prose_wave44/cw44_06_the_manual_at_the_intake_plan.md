## 1. Expansion thesis

The 203.1 broadcast comes from an isolated water-treatment worker whose pump has seized and whose membrane is failing. Over four days the voice moves from clipped distress to a final transmission leaving a technical manual and tools at the intake valve, with a request that the station not die. The follow-up records lights near the intake and later says the operator is safe and replacement membranes are secured. This bank writes the moral and emotional shape of that handoff while refusing to reproduce the manual or invent a maintenance tutorial.

## 2. Story question

When a worker leaves tools and a manual at an intake valve before going silent, how can a community inherit the work without pretending the page is the worker?
