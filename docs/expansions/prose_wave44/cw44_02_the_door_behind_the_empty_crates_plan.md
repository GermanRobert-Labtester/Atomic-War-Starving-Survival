## 1. Expansion thesis

The False Distress Signal event sends a scavenger team to a quiet building staged with mannequin survivors and empty crates. A door slams, a tripwire tightens, and an ambush begins; the event offers existing choices to fight through, negotiate surrender, or signal for backup, with the latter starting a rescue expedition at Raider Trap Site. The location record describes that site as a road rigged with tripwires and buried spikes, with a visible hiding place and collected salvage. This bank writes the aftermath of a staged welcome without expanding the event into a tactics guide.

## 2. Story question

When an empty display is built to make a survivor step inside, what can a return expedition repair besides the door?

**01 — source fidelity.** The anchor remains raider_trap_location (Raider Trap Site). Every proposed sentence must stay attached to the cited local record, and every inference must remain visibly an inference. **02 — material attention.** The strongest details in this bank are physical: a boundary, a surface, a line of sight, a document, a marker, a schedule, or a room that still has its old shape. Keep those details ordinary enough to be believed and specific enough to distinguish Raider Trap Site from another location.
