## 1. Expansion thesis

The Reservoir District Water Tower serves a pre-war reservoir district; its record says mist reduces visibility while the tower remains structurally sound. A separate water-treatment broadcast on 203.1 speaks from an isolated worker who has hand-filtered through a failed pump, leaves a manual and tools at an intake valve, and asks that the station not die. This bank lets the tower hold the quiet public shape of water while the radio carries the private exhaustion of keeping water possible. It does not merge the records or invent a route between them.

## 2. Story question

When a sound water tower stays structurally sound inside a veil of reservoir mist, what does a clear landmark mean to people who cannot see far enough to feel certain?
