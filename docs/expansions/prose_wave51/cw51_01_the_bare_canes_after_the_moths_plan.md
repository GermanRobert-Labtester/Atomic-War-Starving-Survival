## 1. Expansion thesis

Moth Blight Decimates Surface Forage describes caterpillar swarms stripping wild berry canes, willow saplings, and wild grain patches down to bare wood. With surface vegetation devastated, ground rodents invade agricultural silos and shelter pantries in search of food. The event authorizes one response: seal grain barrels with bitumen pitch and set poison traps in corridors. This bank stays with the altered landscape, the fear of losing stored food, and the uncomfortable fact that the rodents are also responding to hunger. It treats the authored response as a branch, never as a pest-control lesson.

## 2. Story question

When a moth bloom leaves berry canes, willow shoots, and grain patches bare, how does a shelter protect its stores without treating hungry animals as an enemy to erase?
