## 1. Expansion thesis

Human Bootprints describes a second trail crossing the approach to a set trap. The prints are half-filled with ash; they stop beside the trap, circle once, and head back toward the ruins. Nothing was taken. Someone only wanted to know where the settlement was looking. This bank follows the pressure of being observed, the difference between evidence and accusation, and the way a small circle in dust can alter a room’s sense of safety without proving who made it.

## 2. Story question

When ash-filled bootprints cross a set trap, circle it, and return toward the ruins without taking anything, how does a settlement live with proof that its attention has been noticed?
