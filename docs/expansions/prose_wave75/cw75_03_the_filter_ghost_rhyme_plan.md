## 1. Expansion thesis

The Filter Ghost Rhyme is a local bunker record taught by technicians to keep apprentices attentive: grey paper, white paper, black paper, soot, impeller scream, and an ash-ghost that swallows porridge and dreams. The record states that blackened filters indicate dangerous ash breakthrough. This plan expands the training rhyme as inherited technical attention while keeping the operational rule source-owned and unchanged.

## 2. Story question

When technicians teach apprentices grey paper, black paper, soot, an impeller scream, and an ash-ghost swallowing porridge and dreams, what does a warning become when it is taught as a nursery rhyme?
