## 1. Expansion thesis

The Red Light Freeze Game is a local bunker folklore record. When fluorescent strips fail and a red 12-volt emergency circuit activates, children sit cross-legged with fists over their ears until the diesel generator roars. This plan expands the game as sensory memory around a power interruption while keeping electricity, hearing, and emergency response outside a new mechanic.

## 2. Story question

When children chant red light, iron stone, and nobody move until diesel is blown while emergency lights fail, what does stillness mean when the game borrows a real power change?
