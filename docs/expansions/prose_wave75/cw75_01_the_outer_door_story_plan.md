## 1. Expansion thesis

The Outer Door Story is a local bunker folklore record about Old Peter, a dog-leg ladder, a copper pry-bar, a latch that hisses two millimeters, salt fingers, and a shadow on an airlock floor. This plan expands the tale as inherited longing and boundary memory while keeping the outer door, sky, and transformation outside a new route or hazard authority.

## 2. Story question

When Old Peter touches an outer-door wheel to see the sky and becomes a shadow on the airlock floor, what does a forbidden glimpse cost when nobody remembers the sky?
