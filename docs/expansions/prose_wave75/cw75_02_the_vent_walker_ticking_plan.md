## 1. Expansion thesis

The Vent-Walker Ticking is a local bunker folklore record. Children explain metallic popping in ductwork as the Step of the Vent-Walker, a figure with three brass knees. The record gives the grounded truth that expansion joints groan when heater banks switch between day and night load, while children avoid leaving food wrappers near grates. This plan expands the folklore as sound, maintenance, and playful taboo without adding a vent creature or operational rule.

## 2. Story question

When children hear expansion joints pop and imagine a three-kneed walker that smells grease, what does infrastructure become when a harmless wrapper can feel like bait?
