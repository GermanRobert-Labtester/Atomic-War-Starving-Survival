## 1. Expansion thesis

The Missing Subfloor is a local bunker children’s folklore record. Children imagine a Floor Three-and-a-Half between Level 3 storage and Level 4 sumps, hidden in pre-Exchange blueprints, with three clean orange-juice pools and thousand-watt sunlamps that never get hot. This plan expands the impossible floor as shared consolation and spatial imagination while keeping blueprints, access, and resources outside a new map or route.

## 2. Story question

When children insist a Floor Three-and-a-Half holds orange-juice pools and cool sunlamps between storage and sumps, what does an impossible room provide when the real bunker has no open sky?
