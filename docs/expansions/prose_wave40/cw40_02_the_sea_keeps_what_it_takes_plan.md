## 1. Expansion thesis

The Black Flotilla outpost is a place where wrecks, favors, funerals, and signals are priced against weather and grief. Odile Vanter settles claims with a lead tag and a flat voice. Cass Polder weighs recoverable value against the cost of naming a wreck honestly. Uma Tarran keeps codes and burial lists in a wax-sealed ledger. This bank follows their separate kinds of custody around the tethered hulks and contested coastal claims, while refusing to turn a dive site into a route guide.

## 2. Story question

How can a salvage community keep a claim, a burial list, and a living person in the same ledger without making the sea into a fortune?

## 5. Human center

The emotional center is custody: who gets to say a wreck has a name, who gets to mourn a crew, and who gets to repeat a signal. The outpost survives by making claims legible without making loss clean. Its people know that a tag can settle an argument and still fail to return a body.

## 6. Voice and point of view

- **Codekeeper Uma:** Uma keeps challenge bands and burial lists together, treating every broadcast as a promise to strangers.
