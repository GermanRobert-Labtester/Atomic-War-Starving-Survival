## 1. Expansion thesis

The shelter quarters are rows of bunks, curtains, a shared washbasin, journals, dosimeters, damp wool, and a calendar crossed off since the exchange. The authored Mirror Choice places Darin in that room with blood on his shirt and a confession that asks the mentor for judgment. Four outcomes preserve different costs: accountability, quiet protection, hearing the full story before exile, or disowning him. This bank stays with the ordinary room around the choice, where privacy and public standards meet one bunk curtain at a time.

## 2. Story question

When a community watches one of its own cross a moral line, how can a room keep both accountability and the person who crossed it?
