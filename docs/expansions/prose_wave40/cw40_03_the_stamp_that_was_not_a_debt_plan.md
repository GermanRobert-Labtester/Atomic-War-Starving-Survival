## 1. Expansion thesis

The Grain Exchange is a public ritual built from posted rates, a hand-cranked scale, a chalk board, and four representatives who agree to sit together because the alternative is worse. A rope marks the weapons line, the silo leans without choosing a direction, and a route coordinator carries a stamp that improves caravan space without becoming a bill. This bank follows the ordinary labor that keeps neutrality credible: weighing, waiting, refusing a hidden favor, and writing a rate where four hands can see it.

## 2. Story question

When a neutral table works because everyone is hungry, how can a favor pass through it without becoming ownership?

**Use a scene** when a verified consumer can stage an observable moment without implying unsafe traversal or a technical procedure.
