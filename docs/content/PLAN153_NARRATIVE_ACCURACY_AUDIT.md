The authored prose remains intact. Runtime presentation supplies context so an artifact claim cannot be mistaken for current simulation truth.

| Claim family | Audit result | Runtime wording/boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Cobalt sacred cpm values | doctrine and ritual notation; not validated dose limits | “Sacred count named in the liturgy: … cpm”; current hazard remains `RadiationSystem` |

| Epitaph causes of death | memorial testimony; identity and causes are not reconciled to live mortality state | “Cause of death recorded on marker: …”; no survivor death, medical diagnosis, grief or memorial creation |
| Unprotected pilgrimage / burial language | intentionally presented as the belief or marker’s account; physical outcome is not simulated | preserve the artifact text, label provenance and retain no supernatural confirmation |
| Named epitaph people | exact canonical survivor/NPC matches were not found | names remain display identities; no phantom entities and no mortality links |

No record was rewritten because the content’s clipped, material, institutional voice is the player value. Records that make unsafe or supernatural claims are bounded by truth labels and absent mechanics. If future canon proves an identity or a physical event, that reconciliation must be authored explicitly and routed through the owning system.

## Cultural quality pass

- The groups are presented as institutions and local traditions shaped by radiation, industrial work, acoustic monitoring and memorial practice.
- The UI does not label adherents irrational or grant technical numbers scientific authority.
- No belief meter, sanity meter, faction standing, morale reward or supernatural system was introduced.
- Self-harm-adjacent ritual imagery has no player reward or executable action.
- Memorial text is shown as testimony with unresolved identity where appropriate, without turning the dead into live roster entities.
- No authored hymnal has a verified performed-song mapping in the live Oral Lore catalog; the two catalogs remain separate until a curated cross-reference is authored.
