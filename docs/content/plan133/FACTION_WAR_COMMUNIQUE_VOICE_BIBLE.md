# Faction War Communiqué Voice Bible (Plan 133)

> Forty institutional voices contesting the public memory of the war. Preserved from
> the existing 18 and extended to 22 new entries. Every new entry was authored against
> this bible; deviations would flatten the corpus into one writer (§3.6).

## Central Garrison — the Continuity Office

**Register**: official continuity, sanitized causality, passive voice, procedural
certainty, the bureaucratic absorption of violence into administration.
**Favorite nouns**: assessment, coordination, unauthorized, continuity, sanctioned,
grid, distribution, inspection, review, notice, channels, record.
**Tactics**: procedural denial; jurisdictional narrowing ("not our grid / not our
command / administrative, not political"); casualty minimization by abstraction;
safety-order framing; occupation framed as "continuity of distribution"; quiet
corrections that never name the earlier claim; concede-nothing ceasefire language.
**Avoid**: moustache-twirling threats, direct motive admission, first-person plural
warmth, slang. The Office never says "we shot at" — it says "an ordnance impact
coincided with".
**Signature openers** (bounded reuse): "The Continuity Office confirms…", "…this
office notes", "Notice/Order ###". Never start every entry the same way.
**Titles**: institutional noun phrases ("Continuity of Distribution, Formally
Secured") — "On the…" skeletons must not dominate (baseline 4 uses retained; new
entries avoid the skeleton).

## Rebuilders — the Exchange

**Register**: practical civic counter-record. Work crews, manifests, geometry,
witnessed facts, arithmetic. The people who actually move the food.
**Favorite nouns**: roster, route, scale, crew, stores, rope, shift, load, ledger,
count, stall, carrier.
**Tactics**: manifests and reweighing as counter-evidence; eyewitness practicality
("that's geometry"); labor/supply-chain framing; anger at euphemism ("call it what it
is"); **admission of uncertainty where evidence is weak**; internal disagreement
stated in public because the alternative is letting the Garrison write the story;
corrections and benchmarks ("we'll call it what the post comes down").
**Avoid**: permanent righteousness, revolutionary slogans in every entry, omniscience
— they guess wrong sometimes (d550 authorNote) and say so (d582).
**Titles**: colloquial declarative sentences ("There Was No Convoy", "Call It What It Is").

## Ash Sign — the shrine

**Register**: measured doctrinal prose grounded in ash/radiation/pattern language.
**Favorite nouns**: reading, measure, ground, count, sign, pilgrim, pattern, cairn,
numbers, verse.
**Tactics**: pattern interpretation; doctrinal confidence before the shrine strike;
**doctrinal fracture and public humility after it** (d583); the discipline of
distinguishing observable measurement from metaphysical interpretation; honest
uncertainty stated as a promise ("we will not paper the gap with a verse").
**Avoid**: supernatural certainty unsupported by canon (the shrine's foreknowledge is
mundane vigilance in doctrinal dress — d521 authorNote); mental-illness caricature;
claiming to know who strikes. The shrine measures; it does not prophecy.
**Post-strike arc**: d583 retraction → d591 smaller claim → d577 restless numbers
(observation without doctrine) → d602 measurement-vs-meaning discipline. Never
backslide into confident doctrine after d583.

## Forward Roster — the spur road post

**Register**: improvised legitimacy, checkpoint rules, local defense, reluctant
institutionalism. Twelve people who became thirty.
**Favorite nouns**: road, rate, post, coat, checkpoint, standing, clean passage, ink,
column, toll, manifest.
**Tactics**: local legitimacy ("somebody should stand where the occupying starts");
toll/accounting justification ("the toll is the price of standing"); posted rates as
the distinction from both the Garrison's paperwork and the Toll's whim; defensive
self-definition against "bandits" ("bandits don't post rates in ink");
non-recognition of larger powers ("we didn't get a vote"); post-ceasefire
institutionalization without asking permission.
**Avoid**: generic rebel rhetoric; hostility to the Rebuilders (they walked out of a
meeting, not the district — d575); any statement before the Roster's public identity
exists (no communiqués before day 573).
**Titles**: plain-spoken statements ("What We Are, Plainly…", "Nobody Asked Us…").
**Signature closer** (bounded reuse, 2–3 uses max): "Come through clean."

## Cross-surface discipline

- Radio is immediate/spoken/signal-aware; communiqués are formal and filed.
- Journal is private witness; communiqués are intentionally public record.
- Dialogue is overheard; communiqués are addressed to the district.
- Never reuse radio lines (audited against all 33 broadcasts), journal bodies (26),
  or dialogue bodies (40) — checked by review and normalized phrase scan (§14).
- No entry names a private individual (institutional voice; persons live in
  journal/dialogue).

## Repetition audit (§14) — applied rules

- "On the…" title skeleton: baseline 5 total (4 Garrison, 1 Ash Sign) kept; **zero new
  uses** (new Garrison titles are noun-phrase institutional).
- "The Continuity Office confirms" opener: ≤3 total across the Garrison corpus
  (checked; 2 after expansion).
- "We do not (fully) know": honest-uncertainty phrase capped — existing 3 uses kept,
  new uses limited to Ash Sign (d561/d577) and Rebuilders (d582 existing); no new
  overuse.
- "records show"-type constructions distributed: Rebuilders (ledger/roster/manifest),
  Garrison (filed/recorded), not interchangeable.
- authorNote: every new note classifies truth-layer status in its own words — no
  template sentence reused (audited).
