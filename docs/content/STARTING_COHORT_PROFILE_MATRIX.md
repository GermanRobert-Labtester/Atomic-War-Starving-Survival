# Starting Cohort Profile Matrix

All profiles contain three canonical survivors and only supported initial
condition fields. Differences are roster composition, plausible condition
tradeoffs, and early dependencies, not permanent classes.

| Profile | Members | Opening tradeoff |
|---|---|---|
| Standard Holdfast | Sarah Chen, Mikhail, Elena Vasquez | Legacy benchmark: medical, security, and food resilience with Mikhail's acute-radiation burden |
| Triage Ward | Riley Cooper, Ariana Cruz, Jamie Chen | Two medical roles and one repair role; low early morale and limited food production |
| Repair Crew | Jamie Chen, Casey Garcia, Hayden Reyes | Strong maintenance coverage; weaker health and higher early thirst |
| Growers & Stewards | Taylor Morgan, Sage Green, Reese Flores | Food and morale coverage; weak expedition/security coverage |
| Convoy Remnant | Alex Raymond, Morgan Lee, Drew Paterson | Expedition/security coverage; high hunger/thirst and radiation burden |
| Civilian Improvisers | Jordan Kim, Rowan King, Elliot Bennett | Flexible social/building coverage; no specialist medical or security role |

Triage Ward uses canonical generic medic/nurse definitions and is not an
automatic medical unlock. The other profiles likewise do not grant skills,
traits, research, recipes, supplies, or faction standing.
