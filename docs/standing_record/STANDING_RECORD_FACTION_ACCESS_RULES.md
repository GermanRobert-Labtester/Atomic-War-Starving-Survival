# Standing Record Faction Access Rules & Jurisdictional Conditions

## 1. Jurisdictional Mechanics

In ASHFALL, an access rule defines the behavioral boundary required to trade, travel, or receive services from an organization without triggering hostility, expulsion, or withdrawal of labor.

---

## 2. Authoritative Access Rules

### 2.1 The Overlay (`faction_the_overlay`)
> *"Scrape three plates without writing a lived name or a Continuity number, and Overlay labour withdraws. They do not raid. Rooms go dark of juniors. Posts stay posts."*
- **Mechanism:** Passive strike / procedural withdrawal. If survivors treat the cadastral markers as mere scrap metal or alter site records, surveying assistance evaporates without violence.

### 2.2 The Scale (`faction_the_scale`)
> *"Maintain metered cistern quotas and settle overdraw debts before each dawn bell; tampering with flow restrictors terminates drawing rights at every district conduit."*
- **Mechanism:** Volumetric cutoff. Drawing beyond allotted quota without settling trade balance locks hydrants and shuts pipeline valves across the industrial corridor.

### 2.3 The Compact (`faction_the_compact`)
> *"Submit boundary disputes to formal council hearing and accept sworn survey markers; violence against field registrars voids all historical parcel claims."*
- **Mechanism:** Administrative delegitimization. Resorting to physical coercion against surveyors or neighbors expunges land deeds from the central register.

### 2.4 The Underwrite (`faction_the_underwrite`)
> *"Pay risk premiums in advance and report cargo manifests honestly; concealing hazardous goods or defaulting on contract indemnity forfeits depot protection."*
- **Mechanism:** Indemnity cancellation. Breaching safety disclosure or failing escrow payments cancels armed escort protection and locks diesel pumps.

### 2.5 The Cutters (`faction_the_cutters`)
> *"Pay column road maintenance tolls in iron or coal and obey bridge axle limits; running a closed pass during blizzard warnings closes all waystation gates to your wagons."*
- **Mechanism:** Waystation lockout. Violating seasonal weight limits or toll requirements leaves caravans stranded without shelter on the frozen spans.

### 2.6 The Fleet (`faction_the_fleet`)
> *"Register cargo manifests at Berth 9 and pay wharfage in copper or pitch; docking uninspected hulls or discharging tainted bilge bars your vessels from coastal waterways."*
- **Mechanism:** Harbor blacklisting. Discharging toxic bilge or running uninspected contraband bars watercraft from lock transit and wharf berths.

### 2.7 The Rebuilders (`faction_the_rebuilders`)
> *"Honor minuted trade quotas and refrain from carrying weapons onto the grain exchange floor; hoarding seed chits during planting season results in immediate civic expulsion."*
- **Mechanism:** Civic expulsion. Bringing weapons into the grain exchange or speculative hoarding of heirloom seeds terminates access to communal silo reserves.

### 2.8 The Garrison (`faction_the_garrison`)
> *"Present authorized travel chits at the outer wire and submit to weapons inspection; evading road checkpoints or concealing contraband results in immediate internment."*
- **Mechanism:** Martial internment. Evading checkpoints or bypassing rifle sentries converts neutral travelers into active hostiles.
