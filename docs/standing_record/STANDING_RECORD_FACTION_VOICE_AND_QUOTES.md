# Standing Record Faction Voice, Philosophy & Signature Quotes

## 1. Dialectical Style Guide

Each Standing Record faction speaks in a distinctive organizational register shaped by its material base:
- **Bureaucratic/Cadastral** (The Overlay, The Compact): Flat, legalistic, anchored in physical land and surveying terminology.
- **Volumetric/Accounting** (The Scale): Exact, measured, treats fluid and life as mathematical balances.
- **Actuarial/Commercial** (The Underwrite): Calculates risk, premiums, indemnity, and collateral.
- **Labor/Mechanical** (The Cutters): Exhausted, blunt, speaks of ice, steel cables, and cold steel.
- **Nautical/Operational** (The Fleet): Weathered, salt-crusted, concerned with caulking, tides, and hull integrity.
- **Agrarian/Civic** (The Rebuilders): Minuted, communal, emphasizes calorie yields and collective planting.
- **Martial/Decreed** (The Garrison): Terse, directive, emphasizes chain of command, checkpoints, and ammunition counts.
