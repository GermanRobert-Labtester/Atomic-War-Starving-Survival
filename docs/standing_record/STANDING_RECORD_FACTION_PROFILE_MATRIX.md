# Standing Record Faction Profile Matrix

## 1. Differentiation & Institutional Architecture

This matrix documents the 12 core attributes for all eight Standing Record factions as mandated by Plan 98 Section 6, proving that no two factions are mere cosmetic reskins.

---

### 1.1 The Overlay (`faction_the_overlay`)
- **Core Institution:** The Cadastral Survey & Ground Registry Bureau.
- **Geography:** Pan-regional (`all_regions`), mobile triangulation teams.
- **Resource Pressure:** Precision drafting inks, brass instrument parts, lamp oil for night readings.
- **Desired Goods:** `brass_fittings`, `sr_stencil_pot`, `lamp_oil`.
- **Offered Benefit:** `cadastral_keys`, `travel_correction_on_named_sites`.
- **Access Philosophy:** Procedural; access granted solely to parties who acknowledge cadastral markers.
- **Baseline Posture:** `conditional` (starting trust: 0).
- **Coercive Leverage:** De-listing a settlement or route from the master survey effectively un-maps it.
- **Vulnerability:** Unarmed survey clerks reliant on local protection.
- **Internal Contradiction:** Insists the land matches its pre-war ledger even as fallout permanently alters terrain.
- **Visual / Badge Motif:** Brass transit compass and surveyor plumb-bob.
- **Likely Cross-Plan Consumers:** Wasteland Map, Expeditions, Navigation UI.

---

### 1.2 The Scale (`faction_the_scale`)
- **Core Institution:** Hydraulic Accounting & Conduit Administration.
- **Geography:** Industrial Belt (`industrial_belt`), pumping stations and sluice gates.
- **Resource Pressure:** Pipe corrosion, valve seizing, activated charcoal filter exhaustion.
- **Desired Goods:** `brass_valve_bodies`, `filter_charcoal`, `pipe_sealant`.
- **Offered Benefit:** `potable_ration_quota`, `sluice_transit_clearance`, `flow_rate_telemetry`.
- **Access Philosophy:** Volumetric quota discipline; unmeasured consumption is treated as theft.
- **Baseline Posture:** `conditional` (starting trust: 0).
- **Coercive Leverage:** Turning sluice valves to shut off clean water flow to downstream communities.
- **Vulnerability:** Highly concentrated, vulnerable pipeline manifold hubs.
- **Internal Contradiction:** Keeps survivors hydrated while turning water access into an oppressive rationing bureaucracy.
- **Visual / Badge Motif:** Hydro-balance scale with fluid balance chambers.
- **Likely Cross-Plan Consumers:** Shelter Water Grid, Caravan Logistics, Regional Quests.

---

### 1.3 The Compact (`faction_the_compact`)
- **Core Institution:** Civic Deed Archive & Boundary Arbitration Council.
- **Geography:** Dead Suburbs (`dead_suburbs`), ruined municipal halls.
- **Resource Pressure:** Paper rot, ink fading, destruction of physical title deed vaults.
- **Desired Goods:** `parchment_rolls`, `iron_gall_ink`, `surveyor_transit_glass`.
- **Offered Benefit:** `cadastral_boundary_deeds`, `arbitration_records`, `survey_marker_waypoints`.
- **Access Philosophy:** Legal arbitration; claims are honored only if supported by archived paper.
- **Baseline Posture:** `neutral` (starting trust: 0).
- **Coercive Leverage:** Denying legitimacy to survivor land seizures and settlements.
- **Vulnerability:** Fragility of paper archives against damp, rot, and fire.
- **Internal Contradiction:** Preserves non-violent legal mediation, but its archived titles enforce old inequalities.
- **Visual / Badge Motif:** Sealed wax deed scroll crossed with an ink stylus.
- **Likely Cross-Plan Consumers:** Narrative Settlements, Wasteland Gazetteer, Quests.

---

### 1.4 The Underwrite (`faction_the_underwrite`)
- **Core Institution:** Freight Risk Actuary & Armed Escort Syndicate.
- **Geography:** Industrial Belt (`industrial_belt`), logistics yards and fuel tank farms.
- **Resource Pressure:** Armor plate fatigue, high fuel consumption, weapon wear on escort runs.
- **Desired Goods:** `hardened_plate_carriers`, `heavy_machine_oil`, `sealed_logistics_manifests`.
- **Offered Benefit:** `convoy_underwriting`, `armed_escort_vouchers`, `depot_fuel_draws`.
- **Access Philosophy:** Actuarial safety; risk is assessed and priced into mandatory protection fees.
- **Baseline Posture:** `conditional` (starting trust: 0).
- **Coercive Leverage:** Blacklisting caravans or convoys from insured passage and depot fuel draws.
- **Vulnerability:** Dependent on steady fuel reserves and high mercenary retention.
- **Internal Contradiction:** Provides dependable transit safety, but completely excludes those too poor to pay premiums.
- **Visual / Badge Motif:** Heavy padlock enclosing an oil droplet.
- **Likely Cross-Plan Consumers:** Caravan Trade System, Expedition Logistics, Rail Transit.

---

### 1.5 The Cutters (`faction_the_cutters`)
- **Core Institution:** Alpine Road Maintenance & Ice Trench Cleaving Fraternity.
- **Geography:** The Cut (`the_cut`), frozen gorges and rail viaducts.
- **Resource Pressure:** Metal shear fatigue, winch cable snapping, extreme hypothermia.
- **Desired Goods:** `hardened_ice_spikes`, `black_coal_briquettes`, `steel_winch_cable`.
- **Offered Benefit:** `cleared_corridor_passage`, `heavy_haulage_sledges`, `span_waystation_shelter`.
- **Access Philosophy:** Sweat equity; passability belongs to whoever clears the ice at dawn.
- **Baseline Posture:** `conditional` (starting trust: 0).
- **Coercive Leverage:** Halting snow-clearing operations, allowing avalanches to close key mountain corridors.
- **Vulnerability:** Dispersed crews facing brutal exposure and isolated waystations.
- **Internal Contradiction:** Indispensable to mountain transit, but charges ruthless tolls to travelers trapped by blizzards.
- **Visual / Badge Motif:** Heavy cleaving wedge and crossed sledgehammers.
- **Likely Cross-Plan Consumers:** Rail System, Expedition Chokepoints, Weather System.

---

### 1.6 The Fleet (`faction_the_fleet`)
- **Core Institution:** Coastal Barge Haulers & Estuary Shipwright Guild.
- **Geography:** Deep Coast (`deep_coast`), docks, salt marshes, and estuaries.
- **Resource Pressure:** Saltwater corrosion, hull seam leaks, lack of pitch and copper nails.
- **Desired Goods:** `tarred_hemp_rigging`, `marine_pitch_caulk`, `copper_hull_nails`.
- **Offered Benefit:** `barge_ferry_berth`, `coastal_salvage_tolls`, `tide_table_nav_charts`.
- **Access Philosophy:** Seaworthiness; cargo that risks the vessel is dumped overboard without remorse.
- **Baseline Posture:** `conditional` (starting trust: 0).
- **Coercive Leverage:** Controlling all coastal barge transport and ferrying across tidal rivers.
- **Vulnerability:** Wooden hulls vulnerable to rot, storms, and coastal raider ambushes.
- **Internal Contradiction:** Opens isolated maritime zones, but exploits transport scarcity into severe monopoly tolls.
- **Visual / Badge Motif:** Iron ship caulking iron over wave chevrons.
- **Likely Cross-Plan Consumers:** Marine Expeditions, Coastal Trade, Resource Harvesting.

---

### 1.7 The Rebuilders (`faction_the_rebuilders`)
- **Core Institution:** Agrarian Reconstruction & Communal Seed Depository Bloc.
- **Geography:** Ash Flats (`ash_flats`), alluvial riverbed communes.
- **Resource Pressure:** Soil radiation contamination, crop blight, pest infestation, draft tool breakage.
- **Desired Goods:** `viable_heirloom_seeds`, `refractory_kiln_bricks`, `sterilized_field_dressings`.
- **Offered Benefit:** `staple_grain_bushels`, `soil_rotation_almanac`, `communal_silo_storage`.
- **Access Philosophy:** Communal labor continuity; harvests are shared with communes that planted them.
- **Baseline Posture:** `neutral` (starting trust: 0).
- **Coercive Leverage:** Withholding seed grain and emergency drought stores from uncooperative settlements.
- **Vulnerability:** Fixed agricultural land vulnerable to raiders and sudden radioactive ash clouds.
- **Internal Contradiction:** Strives for long-term food security, frequently denying immediate aid to starving refugees.
- **Visual / Badge Motif:** Wheat sheaf bound by a surveyor measuring chain.
- **Likely Cross-Plan Consumers:** Greenhouse System, Settlement Economy, Food Logistics.

---

### 1.8 The Garrison (`faction_the_garrison`)
- **Core Institution:** Checkpoint Fortification & Regional Sentry Command.
- **Geography:** Ash Flats (`ash_flats`), Fort Karkov fortress and perimeter ditches.
- **Resource Pressure:** Munitions depletion, rifle barrel wear, medical plasma spoilage.
- **Desired Goods:** `smokeless_powder_kegs`, `machined_rifle_extractors`, `preserved_medical_plasma`.
- **Offered Benefit:** `checkpoint_transit_chits`, `sentry_perimeter_watch`, `tactical_hazard_briefings`.
- **Access Philosophy:** Tactical discipline; zero entry permitted without inspection and verified transit chits.
- **Baseline Posture:** `conditional` (starting trust: 0).
- **Coercive Leverage:** Closing border checkpoints and enforcing search-and-seizure on travelers.
- **Vulnerability:** Resented by local populations and heavily reliant on external munitions supplies.
- **Internal Contradiction:** Provides orderly defense against marauders, but imposes coercive military martial law.
- **Visual / Badge Motif:** Crossed bayonets over fortified ramparts.
- **Likely Cross-Plan Consumers:** Border Checkpoints, Combat Trait Checks, Patrols.
