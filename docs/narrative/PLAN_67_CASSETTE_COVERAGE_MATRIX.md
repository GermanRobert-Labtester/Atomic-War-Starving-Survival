## Voice diversity (final 12)

Military officer ×1 (Kilo, existing), hospital doctor ×1 (Maren, existing), pirate broadcaster ×1 (existing), father ×2 (Martinez existing; Father's Tapes — different structure), nurse ×1, conductor ×1, station engineer ×1, agri-technician ×1, dam operator ×1, teacher ×1, public-health doctor ×1. No more than two of any profession.

## Temporal diversity

Existing sets all begin at/after the exchange. New sets: Greenhouse Tapes and Station 14 begin **before** the exchange (routine); Dam Keeper and Evacuation Train straddle it; Teacher's Recordings and Father's Tapes run days-to-weeks after; Field Hospital 7 and Quarantine Tapes begin in the final pre-exchange days.

## Ending diversity (8 new)

Departure with records left (Field Hospital 7), dispersal (Evacuation Train), operational continuity (Station 14), transfer of seed stock (Greenhouse), unresolved apology (Father's), deliberate shutdown (Dam Keeper), routine maintained (Teacher), criteria without means (Quarantine). Zero abrupt-cut endings; the existing four already use that mode.
