# Plan 112 balance report

The additions are deliberately inside the live runtime's existing ranges.
They add acute water and air pressure, one slow low-infectivity blood threat,
and a moderate spore threat without increasing the catalog's maximum values.

The zero-infectivity radiation and prion rows remain explicit non-communicable
concepts inside the disease-shaped runtime for compatibility. The four new
rows all have positive infectivity and authored contagious phases.

## Treatment pressure

The new rows use existing antibiotics, medical kits, clean water, antiseptic,
herbal tea, and the existing camp protocols. No new stockpile or item economy
was introduced. The two curative windows are early, the hepatitis row is
supportive-only, and the spore row is suppressive/supportive, preserving
different medical decisions rather than making every disease an antibiotics
check.
