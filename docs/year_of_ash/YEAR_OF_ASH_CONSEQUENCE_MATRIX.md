# Year of Ash Consequence Matrix

The seven additions use existing numeric and reference fields to express political trade-offs:

| Questline | Primary faction | Secondary pressure | Existing hook types |
|---|---|---|---|
| Amnesty | Garrison | Rebuilders / Ash Sign | standing, morale, guilt, garrison encounters, dossier |
| Pilgrimage | Ash Sign | Rebuilders / Garrison | standing, morale, guilt, pilgrimage encounter |
| Irrigation | Rebuilders | Hydro Barons | standing, morale, guilt, water writ, hydro encounter |
| Water Tax | Hydro Barons | Rebuilders / Garrison | standing, morale, guilt, water-debt encounter |
| Blackmail | Black Ops | Garrison | standing, morale, guilt, archive copy, asset encounter |
| Mutiny | Garrison | Black Ops / Rebuilders | standing, morale, guilt, defection encounter |
| Seed Failure | Rebuilders | Garrison / Black Ops | standing, morale, guilt, seed packet, seed-tin encounter |

These are authored choice consequences, not a new political state model. The host applies standing
through the existing faction-war authority and items through canonical inventory. Secondary pressure
is represented by later choices in the graph where the live schema permits it; no unsupported
multi-faction field was added.
