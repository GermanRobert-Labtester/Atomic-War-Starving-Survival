## 1. Authored Dignity Wishes (3 Wishes)

Three wishes provide non-procedural end-of-life dignity, honoring Constraint 2.10 (strictly non-graphic, non-instructional palliative comfort, privacy, and customary rituals):

| Wish # | Archetype | Title | Dignity Request | Mourning / Palliative Integration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **19** | `the_undertaker` | Apart from the Wards | Asks for a quiet partitioned storage alcove with clean canvas, away from crowded medical wards | Prepares clean resting alcove; funeral rite proceeds without crowded commotion |
| **20** | `the_pacifist` | The Last Office | Asks for a quiet herbal steam vigil (dried herbs steeped in hot water) and no noise | Herbal steam vigil held at cot; grants peaceful death state and communal solace |
| **21** | `the_watchmaker` | The Final Balance | Asks to arrange tools in order on the bench and wind the pendulum clock in silence | Clock kept wound; survivor passes peacefully at their workbench |

---

## 2. Safety & Content Boundary Compliance

None of the dignity wishes describe methods, tools, dosages, or actions of self-harm or euthanasia. All requests focus entirely on privacy, comfort, silence, and customary personal rites.
