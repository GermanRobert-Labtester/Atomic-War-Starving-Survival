# Research Balance Matrix

> **Document Status:** Authoritative Balance Specifications
> **Project:** ASHFALL (Godot 4.7+ / .NET 8 / C# Core)
> **Date:** September 2026

## 1. Research Pacing & Day Budgets

The research pacing follows a three-tier day cost curve designed to integrate with the 30-day survival loop and long-term holdfast campaigns:

| Tier | Typical Day Cost | Prerequisites Count | Example Node | Strategic Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Tier 1 (Foundational)** | 5 – 8 Days | 0 | `knowledge_water_basics`, `knowledge_solar_basics` | Immediate survival stabilization during early weeks. |
| **Tier 2 (Applied)** | 9 – 13 Days | 1 | `knowledge_water_advanced`, `knowledge_air_filtration` | Medium-term infrastructure upgrades and risk reduction. |
| **Tier 3 (Mastery / Cross-Discipline)** | 14 – 18 Days | 2+ | `knowledge_geothermal_tap`, `knowledge_atmospheric_cloud_seeding` | Late-game game-changers and campaign resolution tools. |
