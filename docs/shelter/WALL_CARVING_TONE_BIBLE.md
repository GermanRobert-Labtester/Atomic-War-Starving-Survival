# Wall Carving Tone Bible — Plan 68

Applies to every entry in `wall_carving_templates.json`. Contract tests in `Plan68WallCarvingTests.cs` mechanically enforce parts of this.

## What a wall carving is

A physical trace applied to a shelter surface by someone with limited time and poor tools: scratched words, tallies, charcoal lines, nail-carved names, grease-pencil counts, children's drawings, arrows, crossed-out rosters, crude maps, height marks, prayers, jokes, warnings.

The catalog voice is **third-person description of the mark** (the established convention), with short quoted inscriptions where the mark itself is text.

## Band grammar

| Band | Reads as | Contains | Never contains |
|---|---|---|---|
| high 60–100 | modest, stubborn continuity | tallies that keep going, children's drawings, names of the living, future dates/planting, repair pride, house rules, dry jokes | triumphalism, sunshine optimism, slogans |
| medium 30–59 | documentary routine | ration grids, filter/battery/tool counts, duty rosters, corrected lines, draft/leak notes, dry complaints, unfinished schedules | despair, overt hope, emotional confessions |
| low 0–29 | grief, fear, exhaustion — restrained | stopped tallies, worn prayers, gouged counts, unexplained warnings, apologies, minimal ambiguous marks | graphic death detail, theatrical horror, suicide-adjacent imagery, 20 variants of names-of-the-dead |

## Line rules

- One short sentence, two very short sentences, or a phrase/mark description. ≤140 characters.
- Evidence, not interpretation. The wall carries the mark; the catalog never explains what it means about the world.
- At most one or two deliberate imperfections per entry; no cute misspelling.
- Em dashes and straight apostrophes match existing catalog typography; no fancy Unicode.
- Carved inscriptions may be uppercase (wall-register): `FILTER 2 — CHANGE AGAIN`, `TOMATOES — SPRING`, `DON'T SLEEP BY THE EAST DOOR`.

## Prohibited

- Literary clichés ("last hope", "darkness swallowed", "ashes of the old world", …) — test-gated.
- Lore exposition (war cause, factions, system explanations) — walls assume local knowledge.
- Modern meme/internet phrasing; dry survival humor only.
- Room-locked lines (no runtime room filter exists): a mark may *mention* a surface feature (pipe, door frame, vent) but must not depend on being in one room.
- Real-world calendar dates; day counts and month names only (e.g. "47 days", "APRIL").
- Invented gods, factions, or named NPCs; initials and unnamed hands only.
- Verbatim reuse of grave-epitaph or folklore catalog strings.

## Motif ceilings

No core motif above ~20–25% of a band (tally/mark-based ≤4–5 per band; low-band name motifs ≤5). Vary openings; avoid "Someone carved…" as a repeated construction.
