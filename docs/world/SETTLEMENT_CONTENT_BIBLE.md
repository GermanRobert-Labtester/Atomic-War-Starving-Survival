# Settlement Content Bible: 12 Living Communities

This document records the lore, survival mechanics, and environmental storytelling for each of the 12 canonical living wasteland settlements in ASHFALL.

---

### 1. Tinker's Notch (`settlement_tinkers_notch`)
- **Archetype:** Trade Post
- **Region:** `dead_suburbs` | **Population:** 140 | **Allegiance:** `faction_scavenger_guild`
- **Location Link:** `loc_settlement_tinkers_notch`
- **Survival Model:** Electrified perimeter fence powered by alternator banks and wind turbines; container market stalls.
- **Lore & Fiction:** A bustling swap meet in the dead suburbs. Factor stalls barter copper wire, batteries, and salvaged electronics under neutral market rules.

---

### 2. Ferry Point Exchange (`settlement_ferry_crossing`)
- **Archetype:** Trade Post
- **Region:** `the_drown` | **Population:** 95 | **Allegiance:** `faction_undertow`
- **Location Link:** `loc_settlement_ferry_crossing`
- **Survival Model:** River ferry landing and floating barge exchange controlling the western estuary crossings.
- **Lore & Fiction:** Trappers and river haulers trade clean water and cooked river fish for scrap metal and mechanical parts.

---

### 3. Nine Rails Junction (`settlement_nine_rails`)
- **Archetype:** Trade Post
- **Region:** `industrial_belt` | **Population:** 120 | **Allegiance:** `faction_the_office`
- **Location Link:** `loc_settlement_nine_rails`
- **Survival Model:** Sheltered rail concourse under floodlamps where four industrial spurs meet.
- **Lore & Fiction:** Allocation clerks verify freight manifests while merchants trade machine parts and scrap under timetable rules.

---

### 4. Iron Siding Redoubt (`settlement_iron_siding`)
- **Archetype:** Faction Stronghold
- **Region:** `industrial_belt` | **Population:** 110 | **Allegiance:** `faction_deserter_coalition`
- **Location Link:** `loc_settlement_iron_siding`
- **Survival Model:** Modular armored rail-car fortress buried under railroad ballast earthworks.
- **Lore & Fiction:** Deserter smiths and armorers forge structural steel plates and heavy hardware from freight car hulls.

---

### 5. Fort Karkov Marshalling Yard (`settlement_fort_karkov`)
- **Archetype:** Faction Stronghold
- **Region:** `high_scarp` | **Population:** 160 | **Allegiance:** `faction_deserter_coalition`
- **Location Link:** `loc_settlement_fort_karkov`
- **Survival Model:** Sandbagged diesel locomotives form defensive bastions around machine tooling workshops.
- **Lore & Fiction:** Militarized railhead bastion guarded by perimeter gun towers; sentries demand transit chits and disarm outsiders.

---

### 6. Lock Seven Hydraulic Bastion (`settlement_lock_seven`)
- **Archetype:** Faction Stronghold
- **Region:** `the_toll` | **Population:** 85 | **Allegiance:** `faction_the_tally`
- **Location Link:** `loc_settlement_lock_seven`
- **Survival Model:** Reinforced concrete sluice gate fortress controlling waterway transit.
- **Lore & Fiction:** Tally enforcers operate the hydraulic winches, charging tariffs in fuel and machine components for opening the floodgates.

---

### 7. Brine-Pan Hollow (`settlement_brine_pans`)
- **Archetype:** Refugee Camp
- **Region:** `the_toll` | **Population:** 65 | **Allegiance:** `faction_the_cutters`
- **Location Link:** `loc_settlement_brine_pans`
- **Survival Model:** Tidal evaporation basin and coal-fired evaporator pans extracting sodium chloride and mineral salts.
- **Lore & Fiction:** Salt freeholders boil estuarine brine in salvaged boiler pans to produce the region's essential food curing salt.

---

### 8. New Ceres Silo Collective (`settlement_silo_burrow`)
- **Archetype:** Refugee Camp
- **Region:** `the_verge` | **Population:** 150 | **Allegiance:** `faction_grain_exchange`
- **Location Link:** `loc_settlement_silo_burrow`
- **Survival Model:** Three massive concrete grain silos linked by underground trenches and earthen berms.
- **Lore & Fiction:** Agrarian refugee commune cultivating hard winter rye and trading preserved rations under constant raider pressure.

---

### 9. Slate Hollow Enclave (`settlement_slate_hollow`)
- **Archetype:** Refugee Camp
- **Region:** `high_scarp` | **Population:** 85 | **Allegiance:** `faction_cold_count`
- **Location Link:** `loc_settlement_slate_hollow`
- **Survival Model:** Subterranean quarry redoubt cut into impermeable slate strata in the High Scarp.
- **Lore & Fiction:** Quarry pit crews extract building stone and abrasive hones immune to surface fallout and acidic blizzards.

---

### 10. The Pilgrim's Hearth (`settlement_pilgrim_hearth`)
- **Archetype:** Religious / Ideological Community
- **Region:** `high_scarp` | **Population:** 50 | **Allegiance:** `faction_long_walk`
- **Location Link:** `loc_settlement_pilgrim_hearth`
- **Survival Model:** Mountain priory warmed by natural geothermal steam radiators at Switchback Pass.
- **Lore & Fiction:** Monks provide hot herbal broths, peace-bound lodging, and manuscript preservation to freezing pilgrims.

---

### 11. Cape Beacon Commune (`settlement_cape_beacon`)
- **Archetype:** Religious / Ideological Community
- **Region:** `coastal_shelf` | **Population:** 45 | **Allegiance:** `faction_black_flotilla`
- **Location Link:** `loc_settlement_cape_beacon`
- **Survival Model:** Coastal lighthouse bluff with rainwater cisterns, kelp beds, and prism optics.
- **Lore & Fiction:** Keepers maintain navigational lights, condense pure water from ocean fog, and guard optical lenses against sea raiders.

---

### 12. St. Nicholas Spring Sanctuary (`settlement_st_nicholas`)
- **Archetype:** Religious / Ideological Community
- **Region:** `the_cluster` | **Population:** 40 | **Allegiance:** `faction_quiet_house`
- **Location Link:** `loc_settlement_st_nicholas`
- **Survival Model:** Subterranean artesian spring beneath ancient monastery crypts.
- **Lore & Fiction:** Silent caretakers distribute clean drinking water and sterile burn salves to wounded travelers without demanding tribute.
