## 1. High-Impact Weather Preparation & Payoff

| Weather Kind | Warning Lead | Consequence if Ignored | Actionable Preparation | Mitigation Payoff |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Fallout Storm** | 1–3 Days | High survivor rad dosage (+150 rad/hr), intake scrubber damage | Stock carbon filters, seal louvres, cancel overland sorties | Intake scrubbers preserved, survivor dose capped to indoor baseline |
| **Black Rain** | 1–3 Days | Acid water contamination, hazmat suit degradation (5x rate) | Seal cistern intakes, prepare neutralizing lime wash | Prevents contaminated drinking water supply |
| **Blizzard** | 1–3 Days | Sub-zero freeze (-15 °C), greenhouse frost damage, pipe bursts | Stoke central furnace, allocate emergency fuel, brace frames | Greenhouse crops saved, hypothermia risk avoided |
| **Ashfall** | 1–2 Days | Air intake louvre clogs, visibility reduction (40%) | Pre-clean air filters, schedule indoor work shifts | Avoids emergency ventilator shutdowns |
| **Black Snow** | 1–2 Days | Structural roof load, perimeter radioactive accumulation | Stage de-icing salt, shovel roof cells | Prevents ceiling slab fatigue |
| **Clear / Overcast** | 1–7 Days | Missed travel opportunity | Dispatch long-range caravans and trade convoys | Maximum scavenging yield and travel speed |
