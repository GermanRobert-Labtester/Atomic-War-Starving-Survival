## 2. Survival Counterplay

- **Cold Phases (Deep Freeze, High Cold):** Stockpile diesel fuel and coal before Day 60 and Day 240.
- **Thaw Phase:** Secure sump drainage and complete ice-road haulage before Day 120.
- **Bloom Phase:** Prepare medicine and antifungal fungicides before Day 180.
- **Turning Phase:** Exploit clear skies and migratory game herds to replenish winter deficits.
