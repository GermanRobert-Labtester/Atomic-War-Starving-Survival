# Dose Register NPC Continuity & Political Profile

This document defines the personalities, administrative responsibilities, ethical dilemmas, and voice constraints for the four Dose Register figures.

---

## 1. Dr. Irina Vel (`npc_dr_irina_vel`)
- **Title / Role:** Radiation Registrar.
- **Assigned Register:** `register_ledger` (The Dose Ledger).
- **Core Philosophy:** *The number is the number.* Holds a red pencil and refuses to round down or soften figures. A reading is a historical fact; erasing it does not save the body.
- **Voice Guidelines:** Clinical, austere, uncompromising, tired, yet fundamentally protecting the truth against bureaucratic panic.
- **Key Dilemmas:** Confronting forged records, resisting political pressure from shelter leadership to falsify worker clearances, auditing missing ledger leaves.

---

## 2. Sister Wyn Omah (`npc_wyn_omah`)
- **Title / Role:** Sick-Room Nurse & Palliative Steward.
- **Assigned Register:** `register_sick` (The Sick List).
- **Core Philosophy:** *Care is a schedule, not a judgment.* Manages scarce beds and morphine trays. She will never shuffle bed orders to favor one survivor's comfort at the expense of another's survival.
- **Voice Guidelines:** Gentle, pragmatic, unhurried, quietly observant, unflinching in the face of terminal sickness.
- **Key Dilemmas:** Deciding between palliative relief and curative triage, managing crowded clean rooms, deciding who receives the last morphine ampoule.

---

## 3. Piet Abar (`npc_piet_abar`)
- **Title / Role:** The Clockmaker & Instrument Calibrator.
- **Assigned Register:** `register_ledger` (Instrument Calibration & Tagging).
- **Core Philosophy:** *Every figure has a drift, and the drift is normal.* Piet refuses to pretend measurement tools are infallible. Calibration is constant maintenance, not a one-time miracle.
- **Voice Guidelines:** Dry, meticulous, mechanical, honest about error margins and tolerances, distrustful of absolute certainty.
- **Key Dilemmas:** Detecting cracked reference sources, discovering systematic instrument drift that invalidates a month of readings, weighing the cost of costly retesting vs. working on imperfect numbers.

---

## 4. Saria Voss (`npc_saria_voss`)
- **Title / Role:** The Midwife & Cohort Registrar.
- **Assigned Register:** `register_cohort` (The Cohort Board).
- **Core Philosophy:** *A guess is not a truth.* Keeps the children's baseline board in erasable chalk so a child is not branded or caged by an uncertain number.
- **Voice Guidelines:** Protective, maternal yet unsentimental, guarded, fiercely protective of the next generation's dignity.
- **Key Dilemmas:** Deciding whether to write a low/kind baseline for newborns or an honest/grim one, defending adolescents from premature high-exposure labor rosters.
