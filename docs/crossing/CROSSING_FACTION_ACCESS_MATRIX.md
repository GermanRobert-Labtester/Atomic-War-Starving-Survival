# Crossing Faction Access Matrix

## 1. Access Rules & Enforcement Semantics

Each faction's `access_rule` details the exact social and institutional conditions required to maintain good standing and the specific transgressions that result in restriction or expulsion:

| Faction | Access Maintenance Condition | Primary Transgression Leading to Access Loss | Consequence of Restriction |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Scale** | Submit all goods to honest, calibrated weigh-in; pay market tolls. | Contesting a verified true reading without cause or using rigged weights. | Name placed on public slate; market remains open but trade surcharge levied. |
| **The Underwrite** | Settle risk premiums and honor contract forfeits to the letter. | Attempting to conceal cargo hazards, breach contract terms, or default on lien. | Underwrite protection voided; debt collection escalated to property seizure. |
| **The Compact** | Sign the public draft; abide by assembly arbitration outcomes. | Violence against council registrars; attempting to overturn votes by armed force. | Expulsion from charter assembly; loss of legal standing in property hearings. |
| **The Lamplighters** | Contribute fuel oil and clean glass to the central maintenance pool. | Tampering with street lanterns, breaking reflector glass, or siphoning oil. | Sector lamps extinguished; night-watch escort withdrawn from your quarter. |
| **The Granary Wardens** | Comply with minuted ration allotments; return dry sacks intact. | Unauthorized withdrawals, stealing seed stock, or hiding grain dampness/rot. | Granary draw revoked; barred from emergency winter caloric distribution. |
| **The Water Committee** | Submit containers for sediment inspection; contribute filter charcoal/salts. | Drawing from closed filtration beds during sanitation cycles; dumping waste. | Communal tap privileges terminated; wellhead access restricted to paid chits. |
| **The Quarantine Post** | Comply with 48-hour gate quarantine and declare respiratory symptoms. | Forging clearance stamps, bribing wicket guards, or violating cordon lines. | Immediate expulsion from the viaduct gate; detention in outer isolation ditch. |
| **The Smugglers' Court** | Settle off-ledger cargo cuts privately; maintain strict route secrecy. | Guiding official gate wardens to a culvert cache or reporting contacts. | Discreet culvert routes sealed; black-market intermediaries refuse all contact. |
