# Crossing & Standing Record Faction Overlap Reconciliation

## 1. Context & Architectural Principles

Both Nobody's Charter (Crossing) and Standing Record (Plan 98 / `standing_record_factions.json`) represent institutional survival systems born in the ashes of the war. Rather than creating disjointed or contradictory factions, several political institutions operate across both theaters while reflecting their local responsibilities.

---

## 2. Canonical Shared Factions

| Faction ID | Display Name | Role in Crossing | Role in Standing Record | Canonical Consistency |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `faction_the_scale` | The Scale | Calibrates trade weights at Stallrow and taxes bulk goods crossing the viaduct. | Enforces metered water conduits, cistern quotas, and sluice transit clearance in the industrial belt. | Universal institution of measurement, volumetric accounting, and anti-fraud verification. |

## 3. Thematic Seams & Inter-Expansion Synergies

1. **The Lamplighters (`faction_the_lamplighters`):**
   - In Crossing: Administers public street lighting and night route visibility across viaduct sectors.
   - In Standing Record: The Overlay (`faction_the_overlay`) requires `lamp_oil` and maintains survey lanterns on boundary posts. The Lamplighters serve as the supply and maintenance base for these regional beacon networks.

2. **The Granary Wardens (`faction_the_granary_wardens`):**
   - In Crossing: Administers the concrete grain silos and emergency caloric draws.
   - In Standing Record: The Rebuilders (`faction_the_rebuilders`) operate agricultural seed rotations and communal silos across the ash flats. The Granary Wardens represent the urban receiving and storage counterpart to the Rebuilders' rural agrarian production.
