# Crossing Faction Trade Matrix

## 1. Trade Dynamics & Player Motivations

| Faction | Desired Goods (`wants`) | Granted Services (`offers`) | Scarcity Profile | Why the Player Interacts |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **The Scale** | `trade_goods` | `stallrow_trade_access`, `verification` | High general demand for trade commodities. | To obtain authorized merchant status at Stallrow and verify salvage authenticity. |
| **The Underwrite** | `pledged_goods` | `seed_stock`, `covered_loss`, `favour_bank` | Demands valuable collateral and formal debt notes. | To secure seed stock and emergency replacement of lost expedition caravans. |
| **The Compact** | `signatories` | `charter_draft`, `ratification` | Needs citizen political backing and signed petitions. | To shape settlement legislation, pass arbitration motions, and establish charter laws. |
| **The Lamplighters** | `fuel_stores`, `reflector_glass` | `street_lighting`, `night_watch_escort`, `route_visibility` | Competes with engines and heating for liquid fuels. | To maintain night road safety, reduce nocturnal ambush threat, and navigate darkness. |
| **The Granary Wardens** | `staple_grain`, `burlap_sacks` | `ration_distribution`, `emergency_grain_draw`, `spoilage_inspection` | High caloric value; vulnerable to dampness and mold. | To secure grain draws during famine crises and prevent catastrophic food spoilage. |
| **The Water Committee** | `filter_media`, `sanitation_salts` | `clean_water_rights`, `well_head_access`, `quality_certification` | Constant consumption of charcoal and filtration consumables. | To guarantee safe, uncontaminated drinking water draws for the shelter and travelers. |
| **The Quarantine Post** | `medical_tinctures`, `respirator_masks` | `gate_health_screening`, `quarantine_clearance`, `outbreak_warning` | Scarcity of pharmaceuticals, antiseptics, and PPE. | To clear sick refugees and expedition crews through the gate during epidemics. |
| **The Smugglers' Court** | `unchartered_salvage`, `route_intelligence` | `off_ledger_trade`, `culvert_transit`, `discreet_arbitration` | Hidden items, uninspected crates, unmapped maps. | To bypass tariffs, move embargoed goods, and trade without bureaucratic inspection. |
