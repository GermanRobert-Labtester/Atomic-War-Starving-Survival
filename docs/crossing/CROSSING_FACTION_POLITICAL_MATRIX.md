# Crossing Faction Political Matrix

## 1. Political Leverage & Institutional Posture

| Faction | Core Institutional Leverage | Debt Stance | Arbitration Stance | Public Good vs Monopoly Stance | Main Political Rival |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **The Scale** | Brass weighbridge, metric verification, Stallrow stall permits. | Neutral: debts must be weighed in standard units, not speculative estimates. | Technical: relies on demonstrable physical weight and measure. | Rejects monopolies, insists all goods cross an open, audited scale. | The Smugglers' Court |
| **The Underwrite** | Emergency credit, insurance against loss, forfeit liens. | Absolute: debt is binding regardless of catastrophe; forfeits are non-negotiable. | Legalistic: enforces literal text of signed contracts without sentiment. | Commercial: believes risk must carry a premium; rejects unearned charity. | The Compact |
| **The Compact** | Charter draft, voting assembly, collective quorum, petition hall. | Reluctant: argues extreme emergency debt should be reviewed by assembly. | Democratic: disputes should be settled by open debate and counted votes. | Collective: survival depends on shared charter rights over private enforcement. | The Underwrite |
| **The Lamplighters** | Oil cisterns, iron street lanterns, night visibility, darkness. | Transactional: utility dues must be paid; arrears result in unlit sectors. | Utilitarian: visibility is a condition of safety; light follows maintenance. | Civic: illumination is a common benefit, but maintenance requires fuel dues. | The Underwrite / Smugglers |
| **The Granary Wardens** | Concrete grain silos, moisture monitoring, famine reserves. | Conservative: protected food reserves cannot be seized to settle debts. | Protective: grain is life; releases must be minuted against starvation. | Fiduciary: communal reserves belong to future winters, not current markets. | The Scale / The Underwrite |
| **The Water Committee** | Deep wellhead, sand-charcoal filtration beds, draw schedules. | Strict: water cannot be pledged as collateral; contamination is irreversible. | Technical / Sanitary: water safety is non-negotiable and outside politics. | Essential Public Trust: uncontaminated water is a shared necessity. | The Granary Wardens / Smugglers |
| **The Quarantine Post** | Viaduct gate wicket, isolation ward, medical screening. | Disinterested: pathogen status overrides contracts and debts. | Procedural / Triage: isolation protocols cannot be softened for status. | Gatekeeping: the settlement survives only if infection is held at the ditch. | The Compact / Smugglers |
| **The Smugglers' Court** | Drainage culverts, off-book transit, unrecorded route caches. | Informal: debt is settled by silence and favors, not legal forfeits. | Pragmatic: resolves disputes discreetly to avoid drawing official attention. | Anti-Bureaucratic: believes rules starve people; profit thrives in shadows. | The Quarantine Post / The Scale |
