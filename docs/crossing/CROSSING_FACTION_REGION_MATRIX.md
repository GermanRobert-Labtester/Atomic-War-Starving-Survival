# Crossing Faction Region Matrix

## 1. Regional Jurisdiction & Presence

All eight Crossing factions hold their primary headquarters and institutional seats within `region_crossing` (the Nobody's Charter viaduct settlement). However, their operational presence spans distinct sectors within the settlement geography:

| Faction | Home Region ID | Physical Headquarters | Sector Presence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Scale** | `region_crossing` | The Scalehouse (`loc_crossing_scalehouse`) | Stallrow bazaar, Deck Scale weighbridge (`loc_crossing_weighbridge`). |
| **The Underwrite** | `region_crossing` | Underwrite Hall (`loc_crossing_underwrite_hall`) | The Lockup (`loc_crossing_the_lockup`), credit repository. |
| **The Compact** | `region_crossing` | Petition Tent (`loc_crossing_petition_tent`) | Assembly hall, Founders' Marker (`loc_crossing_founders_marker`). |
| **The Lamplighters** | `region_crossing` | Nightfire Watch (`loc_crossing_nightfire`) | Watchtower (`loc_crossing_watchtower`), viaduct perimeter lanterns. |
| **The Granary Wardens** | `region_crossing` | Granary Pledge (`loc_crossing_granary_pledge`) | Subterranean concrete grain silos, drying racks. |
| **The Water Committee** | `region_crossing` | The Annex (`loc_crossing_the_annex`) | Aquifer pump wellhead, sand filtration beds below viaduct arches. |
| **The Quarantine Post** | `region_crossing` | Viaduct Gate (`loc_crossing_viaduct_gate`) | Outer approach road, sanitary wicket, isolation ditch. |
| **The Smugglers' Court** | `region_crossing` | Lower Viaduct Culverts | Unmapped drainage tunnels, riverbank piers, dark alleys behind Stallrow. |
