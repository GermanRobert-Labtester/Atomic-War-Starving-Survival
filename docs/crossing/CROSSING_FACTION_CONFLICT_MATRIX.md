# Crossing Faction Conflict & Arbitration Matrix

## 1. Institutional Conflict Triangles

The expansion to eight factions creates interlocking arbitration triangles where no single party holds a monopoly on moral correctness or practical authority:

```
                    [The Compact]
                    (Civil Assembly)
                      /        \
                     /          \
                    /            \
        [The Water Committee] ─── [The Granary Wardens]
        (Filtration / Purity)     (Food Reserves / Rations)
```
*Triangle 1: The Survival Allocation Dispute.* Water Committee demands a temporary shutdown of high-volume water draws for bed cleaning; Granary Wardens require water to process emergency mash before damp grain spoils; The Compact must assemble voters to rule on priority.

```
                  [The Quarantine Post]
                  (Gate Sanitary Cordon)
                      /        \
                     /          \
                    /            \
          [The Scale] ─────────── [The Smugglers' Court]
          (Stallrow Inspection)   (Off-Ledger Viaduct Culverts)
```
*Triangle 2: The Contraband vs Containment Conflict.* The Quarantine Post closes the viaduct gate due to suspected lung-rot; The Scale complains trade revenue is collapsing; The Smugglers' Court moves goods through drainage culverts, threatening both market taxation and containment protocols.

```
                    [The Underwrite]
                    (Credit & Forfeit Liens)
                      /        \
                     /          \
                    /            \
        [The Lamplighters] ─────── [The Compact]
        (Night Utility Dues)       (Citizen Debt Relief)
```
*Triangle 3: The Utility Arrears Dispute.* The Lamplighters threaten to extinguish quarter street lamps if fuel arrears are not settled; The Underwrite holds liens on the residents' homes and demands repayment first; The Compact attempts to pass a temporary debt and utility moratorium.

## 2. Bilateral Institutional Stances

| Faction Pair | Stance | Core Bone of Contention |
|---|---|---|
| Water Committee vs Granary Wardens | Competitive | Water allocation during drought vs grain processing volume. |
| Quarantine Post vs Smugglers' Court | Antagonistic | Cordon enforcement vs uninspected transit culverts. |
| The Scale vs Smugglers' Court | Antagonistic | Official weighbridge tax vs off-book black-market transit. |
| The Underwrite vs The Compact | Antagonistic | Enforceability of brutal forfeit contracts vs assembly debt relief. |
| The Lamplighters vs The Underwrite | Competitive | Priority of public utility maintenance dues vs private credit debts. |
| The Lamplighters vs Smugglers' Court | Antagonistic | Well-lit perimeter roads vs darkness required for contraband runs. |
| The Quarantine Post vs The Compact | Situational | Emergency public health executive orders vs citizen democratic rights. |
| Water Committee vs The Scale | Cooperative | Verifying potable water purity and metric volume allocation. |
