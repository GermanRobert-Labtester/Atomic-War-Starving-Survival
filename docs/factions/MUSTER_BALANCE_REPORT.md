## 2. Dominant Strategy Check

- **No Universal Dominance:**
  - High-payoff military/rebel paths inflict substantial casualty risk and heavy war tension escalation, making them unsustainable for prolonged campaigns.
  - Independent paths offer safety and wealth at the cost of failing to resolve major regional geopolitical objectives.
  - Low-manpower tactics (Partisan Ambush) conserve labor but expose survivors to catastrophic loss if cornered.
- **Resource Gating:**
  - Lack of 5.56mm ammunition blocks military offensive operations, forcing the player toward partisan or defensive postures.
  - Lack of medical reserves increases fatal infection probability among wounded combatants, disincentivizing repeated deployments.
