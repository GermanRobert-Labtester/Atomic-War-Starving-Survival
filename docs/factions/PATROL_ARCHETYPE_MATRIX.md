## Archetype Definitions

### Checkpoint
- **Trigger**: entering controlled territory
- **Choices**: pay toll, show pass, negotiate, avoid
- **Consequences**: safe passage, item loss, standing shift

### Caravan Escort
- **Trigger**: encountering faction convoy
- **Choices**: join escort, trade, avoid
- **Consequences**: standing gain, trade opportunity

### Raid Party
- **Trigger**: contested territory, high danger
- **Choices**: comply, bribe, flee, fight
- **Consequences**: item loss, standing loss, combat

### Press Gang
- **Trigger**: contested territory
- **Choices**: intervene, negotiate, ignore, side with
- **Consequences**: moral cost, standing shift, refugee state

### Refugee Eviction
- **Trigger**: controlled territory edge
- **Choices**: mediate, help refugees, supply, support faction
- **Consequences**: standing shift, moral cost

### Supply Run
- **Trigger**: faction logistics route
- **Choices**: trade, info exchange, avoid
- **Consequences**: trade opportunity, standing gain

### Reconnaissance
- **Trigger**: contested territory
- **Choices**: observe, share data, avoid
- **Consequences**: intel, standing shift

### Border Patrol
- **Trigger**: territory edge
- **Choices**: comply, negotiate, bribe, turn back, flee, fight
- **Consequences**: access, standing shift, combat
