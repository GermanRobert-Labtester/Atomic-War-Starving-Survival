# Verdict Existing Four-Site Forensic Audit (Arc 1: The Tempest Array)

> **Focus:** Deep stylistic, physical, and investigative audit of the four baseline sites that establish the artistic benchmark for the Verdict expansion.

---

## 1. The Four Original Sites

### Site 1: The First Geophone Pit (`loc_geophone_pit_1`)
- **Physical Orientation:** Concrete collar sunk like a wellhead, lid propped on a brick. Bedrock-bolted seismometer dinner-plate humming low.
- **Evidence:** Bedrock seismometer array still reading ground vibrations; cable runs east into treeline under ridgeline.
- **Contradiction:** No one has logged anything in four years except the machine itself; hand-painted warning overwritten twice, ending in a non-warning invitation.
- **Outward Clue:** Cable path points directly east toward the ridge and the twelve-gauge array.
- **Prose Texture:** Restrained, mechanical, uncanny without being supernatural.

### Site 2: The Twelve-Gauge Array (`loc_twelve_gauge_array`)
- **Physical Orientation:** Twelve shot-firing sounding posts along an isolated ridge; steel posts with flaking yellow stencils.
- **Evidence:** Stenciled firing orders, grease-stained plates listing charge weights and borehole depths.
- **Contradiction:** Ordnance is long gone, yet someone has maintained the plates legible despite the nearest settlement being nine hours away.
- **Outward Clue:** The cable running under the treeline is the Tempest's own private line, leading toward the fuse bunker.
- **Prose Texture:** Military-industrial discipline persisting after abandonment.

### Site 3: The Fuse World (`loc_network_fuse_bunker`)
- **Physical Orientation:** Dry shielded subterranean service gallery between facilities; bank-vault entrance with free-turning wheel.
- **Evidence:** Glass-fronted cabinets housing three years of paper readouts and linen-coded shift charters. Solenoid clicks on tape schedule.
- **Contradiction:** Deep dust everywhere except a clean, single-person-width swept path leading straight to the tape-silo door.
- **Outward Clue:** Tape-silo door with scheduled automated latch mechanism.
- **Prose Texture:** Bureaucratic ritual functioning automatically in an empty installation.

### Site 4: The Archive Tape-Silo (`loc_archive_tape_silo`)
- **Physical Orientation:** Chapel-sized vault lined with 21 racks of magnetic tape reels tagged by year.
- **Evidence:** Four years per rack, Department of the Interior tags; central aisle reading lectern with fist-sized speaker.
- **Contradiction:** Rack labeled "CURRENT YEAR" holds reels dated five years prior; lectern dust bears a single small child-sized handprint despite no recorded child on staff.
- **Outward Clue:** Recontextualizes the entire program from geological survey to pre-war population/civil-defense recording.
- **Prose Texture:** Haunting, physical, quiet climax of Arc 1.

## 2. Style Rules Derived for New Sites

1. **Specific Mechanical Equipment:** Stilling wells, barographs, anemometer cups, quartz periscopes, zinc core trays, thermoelectric generators, microwave feedhorns.
2. **Administrative Trace:** Department of the Interior stamps, linen shift charters, yellow tags, greaseboard targets, convoy manifests.
3. **Physical Contradiction:** Dates stamped after official closure, swept paths through dust, unlisted channels on recorder racks, missing pages in recurring windows.
4. **Outward Vectors:** Cables disappearing inland, bearings marked on plotting boards, requisition receipts citing other facilities.
