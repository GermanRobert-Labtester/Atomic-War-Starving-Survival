## 2. Cliché Screening & Elimination

- **"Mysterious Energy / Radiation" Trope:** 0 occurrences. Radiation is consistently grounded in specific particulate dust, radon gas, or isotope uptake.
- **"Secret Underground Lab" Trope:** 0 occurrences. Facilities are explicitly designated civil or industrial facilities (tide gauge, agronomy station, forestry ranger post).
- **"Cut Cables Everywhere" Trope:** Varied. Cables are either actively transmitting, rewired with modern glass fuses, or encased in vulcanized neoprene.
- **"Missing Logbooks" Trope:** Rather than generic missing books, logs feature specific omissions (e.g. unlisted military launch swaps, razor-scraped district plates, or wartime shifts).
