# Verdict Radio Pattern & Escalation Matrix

> **Design Objective:** Establish a rhythm where routine mechanical traffic dominates, deviations emerge subtly through numbers, and the late-game emergency stands out because the schedule finally breaks.

---

## 1. Five-Phase Progression Arc

```text
Phase 1: Baseline Machine Routine (D210–D260)
         Pilot tone, meter readings, fuse service, clock drift, geophone cadence, convening call.
            ↓
Phase 2: Local Measurement Discrepancies (D268–D288)
         Barometric mismatch (32 hPa), stilling well delta (+6 cm), core density shift (1.48 g/cm³).
            ↓
Phase 3: Cross-Site Synchronization & Upkeep (D295–D318)
         Decommissioned battery cycle, unscheduled 88.5 MHz burst, unrecorded river surge, census count +7.
            ↓
Phase 4: Procedural Clashes & Telemetry Anomalies (D326–D352)
         Repeater origin deviation, spectrometer calibration offset retained, quota margin down to 1, arrival shock phase inversion.
            ↓
Phase 5: Automated Emergency State (D360+)
         Carrier override engaged. Routine schedule suspended. Census window closed.
```

---

## 2. Silence Gaps & Pacing Analysis
- **D218 to D240:** 22-day silence gap while player explores initial geophone pit clues.
- **D260 to D268:** 8-day silence gap between early convening calls and coastal survey telemetry.
- **D288 to D295:** 7-day silence gap between highland core readings and border relay battery tests.
- **D304 to D312:** 8-day silence gap before core vault desiccant purge.
- **D344 to D352:** 8-day silence gap preceding seismic arrival telemetry inversion.
- **Result:** Average gap of 4–8 days prevents notification fatigue while sustaining atmospheric tension.
