## 2. Detailed Arc Breakdown

### Arc 1: The Tempest Array (Seismic & Archive Core — 4 Sites)
- **Question:** What was the deep subterranean sounding array measuring, and who built the automated tape vault?
- **Sites:**
  1. `loc_geophone_pit_1` (The First Geophone Pit)
  2. `loc_twelve_gauge_array` (The Twelve-Gauge Array)
  3. `loc_network_fuse_bunker` (The Fuse World)
  4. `loc_archive_tape_silo` (The Archive Tape-Silo)
- **Local Resolution:** The network was designed to record ground shock, collapse events, and human census tallies without human operator intervention.

### Arc 2: The Coastal Survey (Hydrology & Maritime Observation — 4 Sites)
- **Question:** Why did civil harbor and weather monitoring stations duplicate readings to classified inland destinations?
- **Sites:**
  5. `loc_abandoned_tide_gauge` (Greywater Tide Gauge Station)
  6. `loc_coastal_meteorological_station` (Cape Wrath Meteorological Station)
  7. `loc_clifftop_observation_bunker` (North Cliff Observation Bunker)
  8. `loc_sealed_marine_laboratory` (St. Jude Marine Laboratory)
- **Local Resolution:** Coastal stations established pre-exchange water and air baselines, continuing to record long after formal civilian abandonment.

### Arc 3: The Interior Caches (Terrestrial Ecology & Hydrology — 4 Sites)
- **Question:** Why were mundane forestry, mining, and river infrastructure storing synchronized isotope samples?
- **Sites:**
  9. `loc_forestry_survey_post` (Blackwood Forestry Survey Post)
  10. `loc_geological_core_vault` (Highland Core-Sample Repository)
  11. `loc_river_gauging_station` (Karsk River Gauging Station)
  12. `loc_abandoned_agricultural_station` (Valley Experimental Agronomy Station)
- **Local Resolution:** Interior agencies were conducting wartime soil, water, and crop survivability trials under Department of the Interior directives.

### Arc 4: The Border Wire (Demarcation & Telemetry Routing — 3 Sites)
- **Question:** Why did decommissioned border security relays remain powered and what were they transmitting?
- **Sites:**
  13. `loc_decommissioned_signal_relay` (Pass 4 Signal Relay Mast)
  14. `loc_border_checkpoint_ruins` (Gate Seven Border Checkpoint)
  15. `loc_minefield_observation_tower` (Pylon 19 Observation Post)
- **Local Resolution:** The border network tracked nocturnal transport of magnetic tapes into the mountain vaults and relayed telemetry carrier tones across the region.
