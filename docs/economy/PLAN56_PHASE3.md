## 1. `RegionalSupplyRouter` (Core/Economy, pure)

| Member | Function |
|---|---|
| `TagsForOrigin(originRegion)` | Normalizes both origin vocabularies — caravan route regions (`deep_coast` → flotilla+coastal, `industrial_belt` → foundry, `ash_flats` → greenhouse) and the legacy specialty regions (`flotilla`/`foundry`/`greenhouse`/`traplines`); `general` always included |
| `ProducesGood(catalog, origin, goodId)` | True when the good's annotation is in the origin's tag pool |
| `SpecialtyCargoForOrigin(catalog, origin, maxLots, rng?)` | Provenance-annotated goods (excluding the universal staples clean_water/canned_food/antibiotics); quantity = `clamp(stackSize/2, 1, 8)`; `priceRations = max(1, round(basePrice / rationAnchor))` where the anchor is **clean water's base price** ("every faction prices its offers against the litre"); seeded shuffle, cap 4 |
| `ShortageDemandScale(regionalSupply, origin)` | Locally produced → **0.5×**, general → **1.0×**, pure imports → **1.5×** on any scarcity demand delta |
| `FilterStockForShortage(stockIds, catalog, origin)` | Resupply filter: local + general + item-space stock kept, pure imports lapse during shortage |

Deterministic by construction: seeded shuffle when a stream is supplied,
stable ordinal rotation otherwise.

## 2. Wiring

| Consumer | Behavior |
|---|---|
| `TravelingCaravanSystem.SpawnCaravan` | When `Catalog` is bound (host `Create(dataDir)` loads it), regional specialty stock is generated by the router — the hand-coded per-region switch is retired to demo/compat duty. Unbound systems (headless demo) keep legacy stock exactly |
| `WaystationNetworkSystem` (7-day resupply) | Optional `BindShortagePolicy(catalog, isSuppliesShort)`: during a market shortage the resupply keeps locally produced + general stock and import-only stock lapses; unbound hosts resupply exactly as before |

## 3. Verified behavior (tests, 10 — `Plan56Phase3Tests`)

- Tag normalization across both vocabularies; `general` universal.
- Flotilla-origin cargo binds only flotilla/coastal/general-annotated goods;
  base staples never appear as specialty lots; `priceRations` derives from
  the ration anchor.
- Shortage scale: produced 0.5 / general 1.0 / imported 1.5.
- Waystation shortage resupply keeps local + general + item-space stock and
  drops pure imports; normal market resupplies full stock.
- Unbound caravan keeps the legacy greenhouse table (`herbal_tea` present) —
  demo compatibility pinned.
- Determinism: same seed → identical cargo; catalog-composition changes no
  longer stall or flip smoke checks (the batch-1 lesson applied here too:
  the caravan smoke asserts mechanics, not tuned-to-a-catalog literals).
