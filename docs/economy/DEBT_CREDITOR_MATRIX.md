## Creditor Personalities

### Supply Corps
- **Terms**: Low rates (10-20%), moderate terms (15-25 days)
- **Tolerance**: Forgiving — notes the default but doesn't escalate quickly
- **Doctrine**: "Dead debtors pay nothing"

### Hydro Barons
- **Terms**: High rates (20-30%), short-moderate terms (18-30 days)
- **Tolerance**: Low — water is leverage, embargo is the first response
- **Doctrine**: "Water is power"

### Railway Guild
- **Terms**: Moderate rates (15-35%), longer terms (28-45 days)
- **Tolerance**: Moderate — fair but firm, escalation is measured
- **Doctrine**: "The tracks must run"

### Ordnance Foundry
- **Terms**: Moderate rates (15-25%), short-moderate terms (14-30 days)
- **Tolerance**: Low — they have enforcers, bounty is standard
- **Doctrine**: "Production demands return"

### Scavengers
- **Terms**: High rates (25-35%), short terms (10-20 days)
- **Tolerance**: Very low — collateral up front, seizure on default
- **Doctrine**: "Pay or we take"
