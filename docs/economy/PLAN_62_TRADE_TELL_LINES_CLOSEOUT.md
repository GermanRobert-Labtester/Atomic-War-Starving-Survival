## 1. Executive Overview

Plan 62 delivers **60 terse trader-tell lines (15 per trust band)** into [`Assets/StreamingAssets/Data/trade_tell_lines.json`](../../Assets/StreamingAssets/Data/trade_tell_lines.json).

These lines provide the human observation layer for the negotiation table across the four trust bands:
- **Hostile**: The trader expects exploitation, violence, deceit, or wasted time (15 tells).
- **Wary**: The trader is willing to remain in the conversation but is actively measuring risk (15 tells).
- **Neutral**: The trader treats the exchange as business and waits for the offer to justify itself (15 tells).
- **Warm**: The trader trusts the player enough to relax some defensive habits, without becoming cheerful or sentimental (15 tells).

In accordance with Plan 62's hard constraints, this is a **pure data and narrative-authoring pass**: zero C# Core code changes, zero RNG changes, zero save changes, and 100% preservation of all existing contracts.

### Hostile (15 Posture Tells)
1. *They keep their goods close and leave little space between you.*
2. *One hand stays beneath the table while they study the offer.*
3. *Their attention keeps drifting from your hands to the doorway.*

13. *Their gaze settles on your pack longer than on the offer.*
14. *They wait through the silence without giving you anything back.*

5. *They separate the better goods from the rest without explanation.*
6. *The trader studies your face after checking every marked price.*
7. *They lean closer to inspect, then return to the same distance.*
8. *Their hands hover over the goods without claiming them yet.*

13. *They keep one elbow on the table and both feet ready.*
14. *Their gaze softens briefly, then returns to the scale.*
15. *They wait for you to move first after each adjustment.*

3. *The trader checks the scale once and leaves it alone.*
4. *Their expression barely changes as the piles shift between you.*
5. *They keep an even distance from both sides of the offer.*
6. *A steady gaze follows the numbers without lingering on your face.*
7. *They straighten one bundle while waiting for your next move.*
8. *Nothing in their posture hurries the exchange or delays it.*
9. *They listen without interrupting, then look back to the goods.*
10. *The trader rests their hands beside the scale, palms down.*
11. *They make room for another item without encouraging you to add it.*
12. *Their attention stays on the trade rather than the room around it.*
13. *They acknowledge the adjustment with a short, unreadable nod.*
14. *The goods are handled carefully, but without any visible attachment.*
15. *They wait in practiced silence for the offer to settle.*

2. *They leave the better goods within easy reach of your side.*

13. *Their attention stays with you even when someone passes behind them.*
14. *They inspect your goods quickly, trusting the familiar parts.*

## Architecture close and promotion handoff

The integration architecture for **Trade Tell** is therefore: authored JSON `trade_tell_lines.json` → `TradeTellEngine as data selector; existing trade presenter as consumer` → `TradeScreenGodotPanel / current presenter adapter, subject to call-site audit` → existing save owner `none for tell text; trade state remains with its existing owner` → a read-only Godot projection. This is the entire intended direction of dependency. A consumer may ask the owner for a fact; it may not mutate the owner's private copy or introduce a parallel clock, resource, outcome, or ledger. The phase-0 audit must replace the illustrative framework with exact current signatures and record any contrary source evidence.

**Promotion packet:** one chosen card ID; desired player outcome and explicit non-goals; current file:line premise; exact claimed paths; authoritative command and state owner; catalog row IDs; save migration classification; day order; panel route; 3–6 observable acceptance criteria; focused verification command. A foreman or named integrator may promote that packet. This document alone does not promote it.

**Polishing gate:** read every new player-facing line aloud for the restrained ASHFALL register, remove verdicts on the player's morality, remove unsupported reward language, align naming with live IDs, and reject any card that merely repeats a neighboring card without a distinct state or failure mode. Preserve historical closeout claims as dated claims, not as evidence of today's runtime.

## Editorial pass: twelve table scenes for a future trade tell tranche

These are prose direction samples, not new catalog IDs or implemented branches. They show how a posture tell can stay observational while a negotiation changes around it. Each must be checked against the live stance and trust ranges before authoring a catalog row. None asserts an inventory adjustment, bargain, or standing gain.

### 1. Hostile opening at the scale

The trader sets the empty pan down first. The scale is level before either side places a thing on it. One finger remains on the box latch, well away from the goods. The line belongs to a hostile trust band because it describes guarded behavior, not because it calls the trader a villain. If the player raises an offer, the line should not say the scale tipped toward agreement; that would promise a negotiation result the tell does not own. It can say that the finger leaves the latch for a moment. The price view must continue to display the actual current quote from the price owner.

### 2. Hostile walk-away

There is no threat in the departure. A sheet of paper is folded twice and returned to an inside pocket. The trader counts the visible crates before leaving and does not touch them. This is colder than a shouted insult and leaves the player a clear picture of the table. The text should render only after the trade screen's actual refusal or exit path, never as a prediction during an editable offer. A reopen on the same saved trade state must not imply a previous physical exchange. If the catalog has no exit-condition field, keep this as editorial reserve rather than smuggling it into an opening pool.

### 3. Wary inspection of a water vessel

The trader asks for the stopper to be removed, then waits until the glass has stopped vibrating in the player's hand. The gesture says the commodity needs inspection; it does not certify the water. The line must not state that contamination was found unless the water owner supplies an actual result. A wary tell can hold an unanswered question without using the word “suspicious.” The panel should show the known condition, unknown condition, or missing test as separate facts. If the trade route cannot display the condition, the line should stay with the glance and avoid a technical claim.

### 4. Wary count beside an invoice

One crate, then the next. The trader touches each number on the invoice with a short nail and stops at an unfilled line. The line gives a reason to look at the item count without adding a hidden fee or accusing the player of theft. Its associated trust band should come from the present trade model; no prose file may keep a private trust score. On a repeated recalculation, a different line may be selected only through the same seeded tell-selection contract. If the selection replays after restore, the observed line should be traceable to the pool and deterministic input used for it.

### 5. Neutral offer received

The trader turns the offer card so both sides can read it. A corner of the card is softened from earlier hands. The posture is businesslike and ordinary. It avoids the false warmth of “they smile” and the false hostility of “they sneer.” The line should not say that the offer was accepted until `TryConfirmTrade` has actually succeeded. A neutral opening can be shown during quote inspection; a neutral confirmation belongs only after settlement. This distinction is worth preserving even when both lines use the same location and speaker, because the player's control over the transaction changes between those moments.

### 6. Neutral stock shortage

There are fewer sealed cases than the board lists. The trader turns the board over and rewrites the count where the player can see it. The observation could accompany a real stock update, but it cannot create one. The inventory or merchant restock owner must publish the quantity first. The tell can then describe the human response to that fact. If the quantity is unchanged, this candidate is rejected for that context. The writer should never use scarcity language as a universal filler, because it would train the player to distrust a truthful stock display.

### 7. Warm return visit

The trader brings out the same chair without asking where it belongs. This is enough familiarity for a warm band; it does not need a declaration of friendship. If the player has never traded with this person, the line is anachronistic even when an aggregate trust value is high for some other reason. The current data model may not expose a per-trader visit history. In that case the line must be deferred or rewritten as a gesture that can happen on a first meeting. A trust band alone does not authorize invented shared history.

### 8. Warm repair of a damaged package

The trader offers a length of string and holds the torn wrapping while the player closes it. This act is courteous but does not make damaged goods whole. The quote and item condition remain exactly what their owners report. The line should not say “free repair” unless a real transaction path grants it. A good tell locates trust in a small action the screen can show without inventing a buff. The panel's accessible text should convey the action plainly; it should not rely on a tiny hand animation or color alone to carry the meaning.

### 9. Counteroffer under a failing lamp

The lamp flickers while both sides read the revised number. The trader waits for it to steady before taking the card back. This is an environmental detail, not an excuse for a random price change. The trade quote must already have updated through its price authority before the line is eligible. If the same stance and trust band can occur under full power, the lamp detail needs a power-state condition or must be removed. Generic tell pools cannot truthfully describe a blackout that the shelter has not experienced. A restrained sentence can be exact without being universal.

### 10. Caravan arrival with wet straps

Water has darkened the convoy straps, and the trader lays one along the table to keep the load from sliding. Arrival is visible, but the line must not imply that a specific weather event happened unless the caravan or weather owner can prove it. A trade tell is a reading of posture at the table, not a replacement travel log. If the player meets the trader inside a dry bunker after many days, a damp strap may still be plausible but loses the causal meaning of a recent storm. Author the context narrowly, or keep the physical action and discard the weather inference.

### 11. Debt reminder without menace

The trader opens a ledger to a page already marked. The amount remains in the ledger; the tell line need not repeat a number that can change. It should not imply immediate seizure, interest, or a collection deadline unless the debt owner has published those facts. A quiet page turn can carry pressure without turning the trader into a stock antagonist. If the player has no debt in the canonical ledger, this scene is unavailable. It must never be selected solely because the trust band is hostile. Debt is a separate condition and a separate authority.

### 12. Final unit on the table

The last item is placed at the edge of the cloth, away from the other goods. Both sides can see that there is only one. The word “last” is earned only if the stock owner reports exactly one available unit at the moment of display. If another trade consumes it before confirmation, the panel must refresh the quote and the tell; neither can authorize a sale from a stale preview. If it remains available, the selected prose should be quiet enough that the ordinary transaction still matters more than the line. It describes attention, not a new scarcity multiplier.

The editorial gate for all twelve scenes is the same: verify the state predicate, locate the correct stance/band pool, ensure one physical detail makes the line recognizable, and compare every noun that implies a mechanic with the real trade, stock, debt, and weather owners. When a predicate is unavailable, rewrite the line as an unconditional observation or defer it. A future tranche should first improve the actual screen consumer and catalog validation, then add only lines whose contexts the selector can support.
