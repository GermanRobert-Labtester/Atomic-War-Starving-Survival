# Folklore & Children's Culture Voice Bible

---

## 1. Voice Principles

1. **Child Logic, Not Adult Exposition:** Children interpret complex mechanical or radiologic realities using familiar physical objects (pipes, spoons, boots, zinc plates, filters, valves).
2. **Concrete Bunker Objects:** Every rhyme, chant, or tale roots itself in concrete details: dosimeter ticks, yellow decontamination lines, rubber gaskets, battery emergency lights, canned lard, chalk stubs.
3. **Oral Transmission Rhythm:** Simple meter, rhyming couplets, repetition suitable for skipping, jump-rope, or dormitory bedtime.
4. **Restraint Over Melodrama:** Dark realities are accepted matter-of-factly by children who have never seen open sun or rain.
5. **No Real-World Religion or Contemporary Slang:** No liturgical lifts, memes, or modern anachronisms.

---

## 2. Operational Truth vs. Child Myth

Children's folklore serves both emotional and survival functions:
- *Operational Truth Examples:*
  - `folklore_children_dosimeter_counting_rhyme`: "Click one, click two... click ten: drop the bucket and run down below" (teaches radiation escape threshold).
  - `folklore_children_three_mask_rule_song`: valve, strap, canister seal check (teaches respirator drill).
  - `folklore_children_ash_footprint_taboo`: yellow decon line avoidance (teaches isotope transfer containment).
- *Imaginative Coping Examples:*
  - `folklore_children_the_missing_subfloor`: mythical floor with clean orange juice.
  - `folklore_children_the_last_window_glass`: sky as a giant glass ceiling where birds never bump their heads.
