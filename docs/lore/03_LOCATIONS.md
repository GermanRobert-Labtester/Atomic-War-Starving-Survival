# New Locations

> Target file: `locations.json` (canonical schema — `id`, `displayName`,
> `description`, `dangerLevel` **1–10**, `travelHours`, `baseRadsPerHour`
> **10–85**)
>
> `description` is player-facing and written in the house voice.
> *Lore* is the deeper history behind it, feeding a `world_history`
> `knowledge_key` where marked.

## The Verge — *Agricultural Belt 4-South* · danger 3–5

### `loc_grange_hall`
**The Grange Hall** · d3 · 1.0h · 14 rads
> Oil lamps, a long table, and a hand-lettered sign asking visitors to leave
> weapons in the porch. Attendance is recorded in a ledger going back to the
> first week.

*Lore:* Where the Ash Militia votes. The ledger's first page has 47 names and
12 rifles noted in the margin. Twenty-two of those names have a small cross
beside them now, added later, in a different hand.
`knowledge_key: lore_verge_first_council`

### `loc_apiary_rows`
**The Apiary Rows** · d3 · 1.5h · 18 rads
> Forty white hive boxes in a field, thirty-eight of them silent. Someone has
> kept the grass cut around all forty.

*Lore:* Two colonies survived the first winter. The Militia treats them as
strategic infrastructure and posts a guard, which from a distance looks
absurd, and is not. `knowledge_key: lore_verge_two_hives`

### `loc_seed_library_annex`
**Seed Library Annex** · d4 · 1.5h · 16 rads
> A branch library where the fiction shelves have been cleared and refilled
> with labelled paper envelopes. The card catalogue has been repurposed and is
> meticulously current.

*Lore:* A librarian converted it in the first month without instruction from
anyone. She is dead; the catalogue is still current, so somebody continued.

### `loc_veterinary_surgery`
**Large-Animal Surgery** · d4 · 2.0h · 22 rads
> Livestock stocks, a hoist, and a drug cabinet with the lock cut out. The
> dosage charts on the wall are for animals weighing 400–900 kg.

*Lore:* The most-used medical facility in the Verge. Every human dose
administered here has been arithmetic performed under pressure by somebody who
was not a doctor.

### `loc_school_gymnasium`
**School Gymnasium** · d3 · 1.5h · 20 rads
> Basketball court markings under a grid of cot-shaped stains. A hand-painted
> banner near the ceiling still reads WELCOME BACK.

*Lore:* Evacuation centre, then overflow morgue, then — since year three —
a school again, for nine children. The banner has never been taken down and
has been true three times for three different reasons.

### `loc_cider_press`
**The Cider Press** · d4 · 2.0h · 24 rads
> A stone barn with a working screw press. The output is undrinkable and was
> never intended to be drunk.

*Lore:* Sector 4's only reliable ethanol. Fuel, disinfectant, and the nearest
thing to anaesthetic outside Garrison stores — which is why the Verge posts two
guards here and one at the grain silo.

### `loc_terrace_pumphouse`
**Terrace Pumphouse** · d5 · 2.5h · 26 rads
> The irrigation head for the whole south slope. The control room has a rifle
> loophole cut through the brick, added recently and neatly.

*Lore:* Whoever holds this holds the Verge's harvest. The loophole was cut by
the Militia the week after a Warlord surveyor came through "measuring."

## The Grid — *Sector 4 Central* · danger 4–7

### `loc_ration_queue_plaza`
**Ration Plaza** · d4 · 1.5h · 28 rads
> A civic square with painted queue lines, repainted often. The line forms at
> 0600 whether or not anyone has announced a distribution.

*Lore:* The Garrison did not order the queue. It formed, and then the Garrison
formalised it. It is the clearest demonstration in Sector 4 that people will
organise themselves and then hand the organisation to whoever is armed.

### `loc_conscription_office`
**District Conscription Office** · d5 · 1.5h · 26 rads
> Formerly a driving licence bureau. The ticket machine still works and people
> still take a number.

*Lore:* Voss's second order — *deserters will be shot* — is posted at the desk
in the same typeface as the pre-war opening hours.
`knowledge_key: lore_grid_take_a_number`

### `loc_municipal_archive`
**Municipal Archive** · d5 · 2.0h · 30 rads
> Rolling stacks, most collapsed. The fire suppression discharged at some point
> and everything below waist height is a solid grey brick of paper.

*Lore:* Sector 4's civil records. The Garrison has searched it twice for the
Continuity Allocation Schedule and not found it, because the Schedule was never
municipal. **Spine layer 2/3.** `knowledge_key: lore_grid_wrong_archive`

### `loc_dentists_row`
**Dentists' Row** · d5 · 2.0h · 32 rads
> Four dental practices on one street, three stripped to the walls. The fourth
> is missing only its chair.

*Lore:* Directly answers the dental chair in the player's shelter. Allocation
shelters were fitted from local requisition, in a hurry, from the nearest
supplier. Somebody drove a chair out to a hole in the ground and left it for a
dentist who scored 58.4. **Spine layer 1 payoff.**
`knowledge_key: lore_grid_the_missing_chair`

### `loc_transit_authority_hq`
**Transit Authority** · d6 · 2.5h · 34 rads
> Wall-sized route maps under glass, annotated in grease pencil with times that
> do not correspond to any published timetable.

*Lore:* The Quiet Evacuation was routed from this room. The grease pencil marks
are convoy departure slots. Convoy 12's slot is circled twice, and beside it,
in the same hand: **HELD — DOB QUERY**. **Spine.**
`knowledge_key: lore_grid_convoy_slots`

### `loc_printworks`
**The Printworks** · d5 · 2.0h · 30 rads
> Web presses seized solid. Pallets of undelivered leaflets, still banded,
> still square.

*Lore:* The Final Broadcasts in paper form: *REMAIN IN YOUR HOMES. AWAIT
FURTHER INSTRUCTION.* Eleven tonnes of it, printed the week before, never
distributed. Excellent tinder, which everyone knows and most people dislike
using.

### `loc_department_store`
**Vansen's Department Store** · d6 · 2.5h · 36 rads
> Six floors, comprehensively looted. The window mannequins are still dressed
> for a season that did not arrive.

*Lore:* Nothing of value remains and everyone goes anyway. It is the only
building in the Grid where the pre-war world is still staged rather than
ruined.

### `loc_public_swimming_baths`
**Municipal Baths** · d6 · 2.0h · 38 rads
> Drained deep end floored with mattresses in rows. Tiled walls hold heat well,
> which is why people slept here, and sound badly, which is why they stopped.

*Lore:* The Grid's largest informal shelter for two winters. Abandoned after a
respiratory outbreak the Garrison declined to attend.

### `loc_st_brigids_almshouse`
**St Brigid's Almshouse** · d7 · 3.0h · 40 rads
> A hospice. Beds made, charts filled in to a date and then not after it.

*Lore:* The staff stayed. All charts stop on the same day, which was the day the
oxygen ran out, and the final entries are in four different handwritings
because by then they were writing for each other.

## The Toll — *Interstate Corridor 4-East* · danger 5–7

### `loc_weighbridge`
**The Weighbridge** · d5 · 2.5h · 28 rads
> A truck scale with a working mechanical readout. The Tollman's first office,
> and still where prices are set.

*Lore:* Everything is priced by mass here, including favours, which the
Warlords consider a joke and repeat often enough that it has stopped being one.

### `loc_motel_verity`
**The Verity Motel** · d5 · 3.0h · 26 rads
> Twelve units around a dry pool. Neutral ground, enforced by the Warlords, who
> charge for the enforcement.

*Lore:* Where the Garrison and the Militia meet when they have to. Three
ceasefires have been signed in unit 7. The Tollman has never attended one and
has been paid for all of them.

### `loc_bridge_seven`
**Bridge Seven** · d6 · 3.5h · 30 rads
> Four lanes over the river gorge, intact, with charges still visibly taped to
> the underside of the span.

*Lore:* The reason the Warlords exist. Nobody has verified in five years that
the detonator works, and the Tollman's entire authority rests on nobody wanting
to be the one who checks. `knowledge_key: lore_toll_the_codes`

### `loc_recovery_yard`
**Recovery Yard** · d6 · 3.0h · 34 rads
> Wreckers and flatbeds nose-in around a crane. Half the fleet has been
> cannibalised to keep the other half running.

*Lore:* Sector 4's only heavy-lift capability. Ties directly to
`VehicleSystem.cs` — the single site where a vehicle can be recovered rather
than scrapped.

### `loc_ordnance_shoulder`
**The Ordnance Shoulder** · d7 · 4.0h · 44 rads
> Two kilometres of hard shoulder marked with paint sticks. The paint is
> Garrison-issue and the marking stopped partway.

*Lore:* Cluster submunitions from a strike that missed the interchange. The
survey team marked 340 and then stopped, and the reason they stopped is
documented nowhere and is obvious.

### `loc_bus_reversal_loop`
**Bus Reversal Loop** · d5 · 3.0h · 32 rads
> A turning circle at the edge of the evacuation route. Forty-one buses, nose
> to tail, all pointing back toward the city.

*Lore:* They were turned around, not abandoned. The order to return was given
and obeyed. **Spine-adjacent** — the evacuation of the unlisted was performed,
carefully, in the wrong direction.
`knowledge_key: lore_toll_sent_back`

### `loc_diesel_tank_farm`
**Tank Farm 4-East** · d6 · 3.5h · 36 rads
> Eight bulk tanks. Three still ring solid when struck, which is a test people
> perform from a distance.

*Lore:* The Warlords' actual treasury. Not guarded heavily — guarded
*precisely*, which is more frightening.

### `loc_radio_relay_mast`
**Relay Mast 12** · d6 · 4.0h · 38 rads
> A guyed lattice mast with a powered equipment hut at the base. Something in
> the hut is drawing current.

*Lore:* Warlord comms, and the reason their toll pricing is coordinated across
four checkpoints. Hooks `SignalHijackSystem.cs`.

## The Spine — *Highland Survey District* · danger 5–8

### `loc_ash_sign_shrine`
**The Ash Sign Shrine** · d5 · 4.5h · 48 rads
> A survey cairn built up with concrete and glass slag into something taller. A
> dosimeter is mounted at the top, working, at eye height.

*Lore:* The Cult's principal site. The dosimeter is not decorative and is not
ironic — it is read aloud daily, accurately, as liturgy.
`knowledge_key: lore_spine_the_reading`

### `loc_pilgrim_switchbacks`
**The Switchbacks** · d5 · 5.0h · 52 rads
> Eleven hairpins up the south face, the verges lined with boots. Pairs, placed
> neatly, thousands.

*Lore:* Ascending pilgrims leave footwear at the point where they commit to
continuing. The Cult does not clear them. Some pairs are very small.

### `loc_snowline_station`
**Snowline Patrol Station** · d6 · 5.0h · 42 rads
> A Garrison forward post above the treeline. Abandoned in good order —
> stove banked, log completed, door closed.

*Lore:* The last entry records dosimeter readings the duty officer describes as
"instrument fault, requesting replacement." Three consecutive shifts logged the
same fault. It was not a fault.

### `loc_low_background_lab`
**Low-Background Laboratory** · d6 · 5.5h · 30 rads
> Deep in the salt, behind two airlocks. Shielded with steel salvaged from
> pre-atomic shipwrecks, which is why the counters in here still mean
> something.

*Lore:* Real and rare — the only place in Sector 4 that can measure accurately
enough to distinguish fallout isotopes by origin. Whoever controls it can prove
where a bomb came from. Nobody in Sector 4 wants that proved.
`knowledge_key: lore_spine_provenance`

### `loc_ice_core_store`
**Ice Core Store** · d6 · 6.0h · 40 rads
> Racked cylinders in a freezer running on geothermal bleed. Labels give depth
> in metres and year before present.

*Lore:* Ninety thousand years of climate, intact, maintained by accident. The
newest core has a visible grey band 4 cm from the top.

### `loc_avalanche_gallery`
**Avalanche Gallery** · d7 · 5.5h · 46 rads
> A concrete snow shed over the pass road, half filled. The uphill side is
> load-bearing and the downhill side is not, any more.

*Lore:* The only wheeled route to the summit sites. Passable, once, per season,
by consensus about when.

### `loc_summit_relay`
**Summit Relay** · d7 · 7.0h · 56 rads
> Above everything. Line of sight to all five sub-regions on a clear day, and
> there are perhaps six clear days a year.

*Lore:* Whoever powers this can talk to all of Sector 4 at once. It has not
been powered since Hour Zero. Endgame-adjacent.

### `loc_the_vessels_cell`
**The Vessel's Cell** · d8 · 6.5h · 68 rads
> A reactor outbuilding, four metres square, door wedged from the inside. Water
> jugs stacked against one wall, all empty, all opened.

*Lore:* Where the Cult's prophet is said to have emerged unscathed. The room is
shielded on three sides by accident of construction, and there are eleven weeks
of tally marks on the wall. Both the miracle and the mundane explanation are
fully supported by the evidence, and the game never picks.
`knowledge_key: lore_spine_the_cell`

## The Drown — *Reclamation Zone 4-West* · danger 7–10

### `loc_lock_gate_four`
**Lock Gate Four** · d7 · 5.0h · 44 rads
> The gate that failed. Still open, exactly as far as it opened.

*Lore:* Lost power mid-cycle at Exchange+3W. The Drown is not a bomb crater —
it is a maintenance failure, which is why it keeps getting deeper.
`knowledge_key: lore_drown_the_failure`

### `loc_pump_station_nine`
**Pump Station Nine** · d7 · 5.5h · 48 rads
> Six drainage pumps under three metres of the water they were built to move.

*Lore:* Hooks `System_BilgePumps.cs` and the confirmed-missing
`ShelterModule_SubBay`. Restoring even one pump lowers the Drown by a
measurable amount and opens sublevels elsewhere. The single best long-project
target in the region.

### `loc_alloc_12b`
**Allocation 12-B** · d8 · 6.0h · 54 rads
> A subway maintenance level with a stencilled designation and no provisioning
> whatsoever. Fourteen chalk marks on the wall by the stair, then a gap, then
> six.

*Lore:* Where Convoy 12 sheltered after the roads closed. Never stocked —
12-B was a *fallback designation on a form*, not a prepared shelter. Halvard
Renn kept the water potable here for two years using equipment he improvised,
and the improvised equipment is still in place and still working.
**Spine — critical.** `knowledge_key: lore_af_renn_dies`

### `loc_records_annex`
**Records Annex** · d7 · 6.5h · 40 rads
> Reached by boat through a second-storey window. Dry above the waterline,
> heated, and someone has been dusting.

*Lore:* The Archivists' outpost. First contact with `faction_archivists`.
Nine people, no weapons displayed, and an immediate polite request for the
player's full name and the names of their dead. **Spine layer 4.**

### `loc_drowned_cinema`
**The Odeon** · d7 · 6.0h · 46 rads
> Water to the level of row F. The screen is intact. The seats below the line
> are still folded down, because somebody sat in them.

*Lore:* Used as a shelter in the first month; the flood came later and slowly.
Nobody drowned here. The folded seats are just seats. It reads as a mass grave
and is not one, and finding that out is the point.

### `loc_cold_store_atlantic`
**Atlantic Cold Store** · d8 · 7.0h · 50 rads
> Twelve thousand cubic metres of freezer, four metres deep in water that has
> frozen over the top. Contents unknown and, in principle, intact.

*Lore:* The largest untouched calorie cache in Sector 4, and unreachable
without either the pumps at Station Nine or a decision nobody has been willing
to make about cutting through the ice. Both factions know it is there. Neither
has moved. That stalemate is a questline.

### `loc_bathymetric_boat`
**Survey Launch *Kittiwake*** · d8 · 7.5h · 52 rads
> A river survey boat aground on a submerged roof. Sonar rig intact. The
> logbook continues eleven days past the Exchange.

*Lore:* The last eleven entries map the flooding as it happened, in metres, with
timestamps — the only accurate chart of the Drown that exists, and the reason
any of it can be navigated at all.

### `loc_the_shallows_market`
**The Shallows** · d7 · 6.5h · 42 rads
> Nine boats tied into a raft over what used to be a retail park. Trade happens
> at gunwale height and nobody boards anybody else's hull.

*Lore:* Neutral by necessity — the only market where all four factions trade
simultaneously, because the water makes ambush arithmetic unfavourable for
everyone. The etiquette is absolute and enforced by people who will not explain
it twice.
