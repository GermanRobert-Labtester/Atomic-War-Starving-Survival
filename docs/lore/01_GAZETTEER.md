# Sector 4 — Gazetteer

> Gives the 68 existing locations a map. New locations in `03_LOCATIONS.md`
> slot into these regions.

## Why the region needs a name

`warlords_sector_4` is canon, so **Sector 4** is already the administrative
designation of the place the game happens in. Nobody named it. It was a line
item in a civil-defense allocation table, and it outlived the government that
drew it.

The five sub-regions below each have two names: what the pre-war state called
it, and what the people who stayed call it now. That gap is a lore engine on
its own — a survivor who says "Reclamation Zone 4-West" instead of "the Drown"
is telling you where they worked before, and how long they have been alone.

Geography, assembled from what canon already implies: a river basin running
north to a cold sea (`location_frozen_river_barge`,
`location_crashed_icebreaker_convoy`, `location_abandoned_desalination`), a
mountain ridge inland (`location_abandoned_ski_resort`,
`location_collapsed_salt_mine`, `location_silent_observatory`), a drowned
industrial flat where the locks failed, farmland on the south terraces, and the
interstate that ties them together.

## 1. The Grid — *Sector 4 Central*

**Held by:** `iron_garrison` · **Danger band:** 4–7

The pre-war city. Named for the street plan and, later, for the thing that
stopped. Colonel Voss holds it because it is the only sub-region with intact
multi-storey buildings, and multi-storey buildings are where you put snipers.

Garrison rule here is genuinely functional and genuinely awful: the water is
clean, the streets are swept, and the labour that does both is conscripted.
Ration queues form at 0600 without being told to. People obey out of a habit
that predates the Garrison and would probably survive it.

**What you find:** pharmaceuticals, records, intact structure, tools.
**What kills you:** conscription patrols, collapse in fire-gutted blocks, and
the fact that everything worth taking has an owner who is armed.

**Existing locations:** `abandoned_hospital`, `loc_regional_hospital`,
`location_hospital_psych_wing`, `suburban_house`, `loc_suburban_district`,
`loc_urban_pharmacy`, `loc_police_precinct`, `loc_evacuation_bus_depot`,
`loc_basement_vault`, `loc_civil_defense_bunker`, `government_bunker`,
`location_ministry_of_truth_bunker`, `location_arcology_sector_4`,
`loc_comm_array`, `location_mirror_factory`

## 2. The Verge — *Agricultural Belt 4-South*

**Held by:** `ash_militia` · **Danger band:** 3–5

Terraced farmland on the south slope, the only ground in Sector 4 that still
produces calories. The Militia's Grange Hall council meets here weekly; the
whole sub-region is run by a show of hands.

The Verge is the safest place in the game and the most quietly frightening,
because its safety is conditional on a vote that could go the other way. It is
also the only region where a stranger is asked their name before they are asked
their business.

**What you find:** seed, food, livestock, medicine that was made rather than
looted, honest trade.
**What kills you:** very little, directly — but the Verge is where you learn
what your reputation costs, because these people talk to each other.

**Existing locations:** `loc_agricultural_coop`, `loc_grain_silo`,
`loc_botanical_nursery`, `loc_ash_woodland`, `rural_gas_station`,
`location_subterranean_seed_vault`, `location_seed_vault_antechamber`,
`location_bio_remediation_lab`, `location_ash_dune_cemetery`

## 3. The Spine — *Highland Survey District*

**Held by:** `cult_of_ash_sign` · **Danger band:** 5–8

The ridge. Held by the Cult for the reason the Cult says out loud — it is
closer to the sky — and for the reason it does not: it is high, dry, defensible,
and the military stopped patrolling above the snowline when the dosimeters
started reading wrong.

The Spine is where pre-war science lived: observatories, boreholes, a radio
telescope, a salt mine repurposed for low-background measurement. The Cult
inherited a landscape of instruments and reads them as scripture. They are not
stupid. Several of them can operate the instruments correctly. That is worse.

**What you find:** instruments, rad-resistant remedies, deep-core samples,
altitude, and the only people in Sector 4 who will walk into a hot zone for you.
**What kills you:** exposure, the boreholes, and devotion.

**Existing locations:** `location_abandoned_ski_resort`,
`location_silent_observatory`, `location_observatory_dome`,
`location_collapsed_salt_mine`, `loc_coal_mine`,
`location_geothermal_borehole_site`, `location_geothermal_vent_shaft`,
`location_geo_thermal_plant_ruins`, `location_deep_core_borehole`,
`location_radio_telescope_array`, `location_radar_array_spire`,
`location_magnetic_anomaly_crater`, `location_acoustic_testing_facility`

## 4. The Toll — *Interstate Corridor 4-East*

**Held by:** `warlords_sector_4` · **Danger band:** 5–7

Road. The Tollman holds the bridges because he held the demolition codes, and
he has never once had to prove he still does. Everything that moves between
regions moves through here and pays.

The Toll is the most honest sub-region in Sector 4. The price is stated up
front, the receipt is real, and nobody pretends the arrangement is anything
other than what it is. Survivors who have dealt with both will tell you they
would rather be robbed by the Warlords than helped by the Garrison.

**What you find:** fuel, ammunition, vehicles, contraband, passage.
**What kills you:** unexploded ordnance, the pileups, and arriving without
anything to trade.

**Existing locations:** `highway_pileup`, `location_uxo_highway_choke`,
`loc_highway_checkpoint`, `loc_toll_house`, `loc_fuel_depot`, `loc_train_yard`,
`location_abandoned_convoy_yard`, `location_concrete_batching_plant`,
`loc_substation_yard`, `location_substation_omega`,
`location_scrap_neuromancer_camp`

## 5. The Drown — *Reclamation Zone 4-West*

**Held by:** nobody · **Danger band:** 7–10

The industrial flats. When the lock gates lost power the river took back the
land it had been kept off for ninety years, and it took it back with everything
still standing. Streets became canals. Basements became depth.

No faction claims the Drown. The Garrison surveyed it once and did not go back.
The Cult calls it consecrated. The Warlords will sell you a boat and will not
crew it.

This is the endgame region — the flooded, silent, structurally lethal heart of
Sector 4, and the place where the deepest pre-war records survived precisely
because nobody could reach them.

**What you find:** everything nobody else could get to. Server rooms. Sealed
archives. Intact pre-war stock. The paperwork.
**What kills you:** water, cold, collapse, methane, and the absolute certainty
that nobody is coming to help.

**Existing locations:** `location_flooded_subway_depot`,
`location_sub_level_4_transit`, `loc_metro_tunnel`, `location_municipal_sewage`,
`location_submerged_data_center`, `location_submerged_arcology`,
`location_the_sump_cathedral`, `location_abandoned_desalination`,
`location_lethe_water_treatment`, `loc_water_treatment_plant`,
`location_frozen_river_barge`, `location_crashed_icebreaker_convoy`,
`location_ash_whale_carcass`, `location_the_memory_vault`

## Military installations (cross-regional)

Four sites do not belong to any faction's territory because all four predate
the factions and were never surrendered — they were simply left locked.

| Location | Region | Note |
|---|---|---|
| `loc_missile_silo` | The Spine | Garrison-flagged, Garrison-avoided |
| `location_drone_hive_silo` | The Toll | still has power |
| `location_automated_mortar_pit` | The Toll | still has orders |
| `location_the_dead_hand_core` | The Drown | still has authority |

The joke nobody in Sector 4 makes out loud: the only institution that survived
the Exchange with its chain of command fully intact is the one made of
machinery.

## Travel

`travelHours` in `locations.json` should read as distance from the player's
bunker, which sits on the boundary of **The Grid** and **The Verge** — near
enough to the city to have been worth building, far enough out to have been
worth hiding. This is not incidental. See `02_THE_LIST.md`.

Suggested banding for new entries:

| Region | travelHours |
|---|---|
| The Verge | 1.0 – 2.5 |
| The Grid | 1.5 – 3.0 |
| The Toll | 2.5 – 4.5 |
| The Spine | 4.0 – 7.0 |
| The Drown | 5.0 – 9.0 |
