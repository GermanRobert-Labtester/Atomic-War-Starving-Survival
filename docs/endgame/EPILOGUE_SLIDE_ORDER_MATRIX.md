## 1. Sequence Bands & Pacing Arc

The sequence of 20 slides progresses across 4 deliberate narrative bands:

```
[ ACT I: CATASTROPHE & REDOUBT ]  Orders 0 – 3   (Opening, Flash, Bunker, Winter)
              │
              ▼
[ ACT II: SUSTENANCE & SOCIETY ]  Orders 4 – 9   (Resources, Survivors, Losses, Factions, Roads, Radio)
              │
              ▼
[ ACT III: THE RECORD & VERDICT ] Orders 10 – 13 (Verdict, Witnesses, Relics, Choices)
              │
              ▼
[ ACT IV: RESOLUTION & TOMORROW ] Orders 14 – 19 (Muster, Resolution, Census, Ruins, Horizon, Close)
```
