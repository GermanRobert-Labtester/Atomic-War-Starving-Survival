## 2. Re-ordering Rationale

In the original 5-slide baseline:
- `What Remains` was placed *before* `Survivors`.
- `Final Word` occurred at order 4.

In the expanded 20-slide presentation:
- `Final Word` rightly concludes the entire 20-slide journey at index 19.
- `Survivors` pairs naturally with `Empty Bunks` (orders 5 and 6) to establish the living count versus remembered losses.
- `What Remains` serves as the architectural and environmental aftermath reflection at index 17 immediately preceding the generational forward outlook (`After Us`, order 18) and `Final Word` (order 19).
