## 3. Thematic Arc Progression

The 24 cases divide into clear narrative eras that trace the psychological and social evolution of the bunker:
1. **Initial Survival Crisis & Adjustment (Cases 1–4, Days 84–188):** High tension, small acts of human resistance (moonshine, chit forgery, music, a smuggled pet).
2. **Infrastructure Strain & Scarcity (Cases 5–9, Days 210–375):** Food equity, critical utility safeguarding, power and water rules.
3. **Culture, Science & Subversion (Cases 10–14, Days 420–640):** Medical ethics, preservation of pre-war knowledge, recreation, innovative micro-farming.
4. **Severe Recklessness vs. Human Compassion (Cases 15–18, Days 710–890):** Radiation falsification, stolen sweets for children, emotional surface retrieval of a flower, morale pranks.
5. **External Contact & Climax (Cases 19–23, Days 950–1210):** Trade fraud from outside, critical resource sabotage, external survivor radio acknowledgment.
6. **The Epilogue (Case 24, Day 3650):** 10-year anniversary, dissolution of emergency tribunals, universal amnesty, and birth of the post-ash republic.
