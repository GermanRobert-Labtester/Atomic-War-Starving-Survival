## Editorial and architecture polish

The second pass corrected the most consequential stale premises. Plan 142's older draft had treated nonexistent clothing host/catalog paths as current and used a wrong C# query name; it now distinguishes them as proposals and names `CalculateColdLossReduction`. Plans 178–180 had repeated earlier “no host/save section” claims after those paths appeared; those passages were marked as superseded or updated. Plan 146's copied draft had a relative master-document link for its former location; that link was corrected for the requested root path. Every revised plan starts with a current-evidence section so its older scenario inventory cannot silently override the live architecture.

The C# sections are API placement guides. They cite current method names and host call order, while requiring the builder to reconcile signatures and owner custody during Phase 0. They deliberately do not introduce a new registry, save section, mutable panel state, or fake completed route. The casebooks are review questions and acceptance design; they are not a mandate to implement every scenario in one package.
