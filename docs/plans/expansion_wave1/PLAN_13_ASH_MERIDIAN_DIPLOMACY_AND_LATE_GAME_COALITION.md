# Plan 13: Ash Meridian Diplomacy and Late-Game Coalition

**Status:** Starter expansion wave plus Continuation Wave 2; documentation-only, premise-gated, and not an implementation claim. **Wave target:** Plans 13–16 synchronized multi-edit, with distinct mechanics, story content, locations, shelter lore, character arcs, factions, and late-game material.

**Domain:** faction diplomacy, coalition politics, treaty pressure, and late-game alliance formation.

**Current-owner boundary:** Candidate work must route through DiplomaticSummitSystem, the canonical faction standing authority, relationship and radio owners, CampaignState, ShelterArchiveSystem, and existing quest/campaign routing. No second faction ledger, relationship store, map graph, shelter counter, ending resolver, legacy store, or parallel save section may be created.

## Scope and non-duplication contract

This plan treats a treaty shard under soot, a brass envoy seal, a lantern map with three routes, and a green-bound coalition ledger as authored anchors for a new playable layer. They are not assumed to exist in the shipped catalog. The current source audit must verify IDs, consumers, host construction, save registration, and live claims before any row becomes implementation-ready. The proposed signals — standing drift, envoy trust, treaty age, public rumor, route leverage, security tension, and coalition readiness — are read models or content prerequisites until an owner is named.

The proposed cast and political vocabulary are deliberately provisional: Ash Meridian Compact, Glass Orchard Syndicate, Quiet Foundry League, Long-Wake Assembly, and Sable Relay. The late-game proposal is a proposed late-game Meridian Assembly that can become a major regional power only if current faction IDs, standing sinks, and ending branches prove there is no existing equivalent. Existing diplomacy, standing, relationship, map, roster, archive, campaign, ending, and legacy behavior remains authoritative. Where an existing system already expresses a requested outcome, this plan supplies content, projection, UX, or a bridge rather than a replacement.
