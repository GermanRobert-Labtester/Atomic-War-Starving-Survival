# Plan 16: Endgame Horizons, Faction Convergence, and Legacy Frontiers

**Status:** Starter expansion wave plus Continuation Wave 2; documentation-only, premise-gated, and not an implementation claim. **Wave target:** Plans 13–16 synchronized multi-edit, with distinct mechanics, story content, locations, shelter lore, character arcs, factions, and late-game material.

**Domain:** late-game faction convergence, endgame decisions, frontier charters, legacy projections, and epilogue content.

**Current-owner boundary:** Candidate work must route through UnifiedEndingResolver, CampaignLegacySystem, campaign state, faction standing and diplomacy owners, expedition/colony owners, ShelterArchiveSystem, and existing scenario and ending routes. No second faction ledger, relationship store, map graph, shelter counter, ending resolver, legacy store, or parallel save section may be created.

## Scope and non-duplication contract

This plan treats a horizon chart with a black rim, a legacy token split in two, a final radio transcript, and a frontier charter sealed in ash as authored anchors for a new playable layer. They are not assumed to exist in the shipped catalog. The current source audit must verify IDs, consumers, host construction, save registration, and live claims before any row becomes implementation-ready. The proposed signals — ending readiness, faction convergence, legacy weight, expedition proof, charter legitimacy, public memory, and epilogue route — are read models or content prerequisites until an owner is named.

The proposed cast and political vocabulary are deliberately provisional: Horizon Directorate, Cinder Crown, Deep Archive, Last Tidelands, and the proposed frontier coalition called the Ashen Commons. The late-game proposal is late-game major factions are content proposals that must project into existing faction standing, diplomacy, campaign, ending, and legacy authorities; none is a second ending resolver or cross-run state store. Existing diplomacy, standing, relationship, map, roster, archive, campaign, ending, and legacy behavior remains authoritative. Where an existing system already expresses a requested outcome, this plan supplies content, projection, UX, or a bridge rather than a replacement.
