# Plan 15: Survivor Vocations, Personal Arcs, and Shelter Culture

**Status:** Starter expansion wave plus Continuation Wave 2; documentation-only, premise-gated, and not an implementation claim. **Wave target:** Plans 13–16 synchronized multi-edit, with distinct mechanics, story content, locations, shelter lore, character arcs, factions, and late-game material.

**Domain:** survivor vocations, personal story arcs, shelter culture, mentorship, and everyday belonging.

**Current-owner boundary:** Candidate work must route through survivor roster, RelationshipDecaySystem and existing relationship owner, skill progression, duty and needs systems, HobbySystem, SurvivorEducationSystem, ShelterArchiveSystem, and current journal routing. No second faction ledger, relationship store, map graph, shelter counter, ending resolver, legacy store, or parallel save section may be created.

## Scope and non-duplication contract

This plan treats a name card with a red stitch, a vocation badge, a shared meal bowl, and a shelter mural under dust as authored anchors for a new playable layer. They are not assumed to exist in the shipped catalog. The current source audit must verify IDs, consumers, host construction, save registration, and live claims before any row becomes implementation-ready. The proposed signals — confidence, affinity, fatigue, vocation fit, room belonging, mentorship continuity, and arc readiness — are read models or content prerequisites until an owner is named.

The proposed cast and political vocabulary are deliberately provisional: the Hearth Circle, Ledgerhands, Dust Choir, Lantern Apprentices, and the proposed late-game Common Table. The late-game proposal is a proposed late-game Common Table faction that represents a shelter culture coalition, with no new standing owner and no replacement for existing relationships, education, hobby, or archive systems. Existing diplomacy, standing, relationship, map, roster, archive, campaign, ending, and legacy behavior remains authoritative. Where an existing system already expresses a requested outcome, this plan supplies content, projection, UX, or a bridge rather than a replacement.
