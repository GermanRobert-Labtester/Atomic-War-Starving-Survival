# Plan 06 — Narrative Depth Trilogy: Last Letters, Echoes & the War Arc

## Goal

Deepen the narrative layer through letters that travel through social ties, recoverable echoes, and
a late-campaign faction-war arc. Final-wish catalog work is intentionally excluded.

## Scope boundary

- Plan 65 owns final_wishes.json and every final-wish definition.
- This plan owns delivery, interpretation, and consequence for existing letters; echo/cassette
  content; and the faction-war activation arc. It must not add final-wish rows or create a second
  end-of-life catalog.

## Task 6A — Last letters: delivery, kin, and response

1. Use existing letters and survivor relationships to create a delivery state: found, addressed,
   delivered, withheld, or unanswered.
2. Let delivery reveal a relationship fact, modest morale/grief effect, or narrative hook through
   the existing systems; do not grant a replacement final wish.
3. Persist the delivery state and test a letter that reaches its addressee after a save/load.

## Task 6B — Echo and cassette found-audio expansion

1. Expand recoverable echo/cassette material through the established narrative/audio formats.
2. Tie discoveries to real locations, people, and flags; keep audio references compatible with
   Plan 07's cue/voice pipeline.
3. Validate continuity, reachability, and replay after restoration.

## Task 6C — Faction-war arc activation

1. Author the Day 480–600+ war-state triggers, choices, and consequence flags using the existing
   narrative and faction paths.
2. Feed existing radio, standing, and epilogue consumers instead of creating a second war model.
3. Add deterministic headless coverage for escalation, choice, and resolution.

## Definition of Done

- Plan 65 remains the only final-wish catalog owner.
- Letters have persistent delivery/response consequences.
- Echoes and the war arc remain data-driven and continuity-checked.
