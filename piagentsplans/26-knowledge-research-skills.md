# Plan 26 — Latent Expertise & Adult Re-Specialization

## Goal

Make existing survivors' latent expertise visible, earned, and consequential through adult
awakening and re-specialization. This plan does not externalize research/skills or expand
specialty, manual, or autopsy catalogs.

## Scope boundary

- Plan 34 owns research-tree externalization.
- Plan 33 owns skill-catalog externalization.
- Plans 80, 105, and 79 own library manuals, trade specialties, and autopsy procedures.
- Plan 154 owns child education and generational teaching.
- This plan owns adult latent-expertise discovery and recovery from lost/changed roles. It must not
  add research nodes, skill definitions, specialty rows, manuals, or autopsy procedures.

## Task 26A — Latent-expertise awakening contract

1. Inventory existing latentExpertTrait values and map each to an existing skill/profession id.
2. Create data-backed awakening conditions: relevant work, a crisis, contact with familiar tools,
   or a witnessed need. Conditions reveal capability; they do not mint a new skill.
3. Record an awakening as a stable survivor fact with a clear journal/UI explanation.
4. Test deterministic triggering, duplicate prevention, and save/load.

## Task 26B — Adult re-specialization and loss

1. Let an adult survivor regain, redirect, or relinquish an expertise after injury, displacement,
   bereavement, or prolonged work in a new role.
2. Apply bounded effects through existing skill progression, duty, and relationship authorities;
   do not create a second education system.
3. Define recovery/support choices so specialization is a human trade-off, not a random bonus.
4. Test injury/recovery, role reassignment, and the no-eligible-survivor case.

## Task 26C — Visibility and downstream use

1. Surface known versus suspected expertise in roster, duty, and expedition preparation views.
2. Allow existing systems to consume an awakened trait through one explicit query interface.
3. Verify all referenced skill, profession, and trait ids resolve and no catalog is duplicated.

## Definition of Done

- Plans 33, 34, 79, 80, 105, and 154 retain their catalog/education ownership.
- Latent expertise is persistent, explainable, and bounded by existing skill authorities.
- No new research tree, manual, specialty, or autopsy catalog is introduced.
