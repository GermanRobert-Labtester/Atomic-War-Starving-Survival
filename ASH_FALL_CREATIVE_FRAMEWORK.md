# ASHFALL: ATOMIC WAR - COMPREHENSIVE CREATIVE WRITING FRAMEWORK

## 📋 PROJECT OVERVIEW & WORKFLOW

This framework provides a complete system for creating all diegetic writing, lore, quests, environmental fiction, and systemic text for ASHFALL: Atomic War.

### Core Writing Principles

**Tone & Style Guidelines:**
- **Cold, exhausted, human** - No glorification, no magic, no fantasy
- **Grounded in reality** - Every piece should feel like it could exist in our world
- **Systemic integration** - All text connects to gameplay systems
- **Progressive decay** - Language degrades over time to reflect societal collapse
- **Multiple voices** - Each survivor has distinct speech patterns and concerns
- **Diegetic only** - All text exists in-world, not as UI/meta text

**Formats & Delivery:**
- All text is discoverable through environmental exploration
- All text has gameplay consequences or context
- All text can be found in multiple states (pristine, damaged, contaminated)
- All text supports multiple playthroughs with different discoveries

**Modular Design:**
- Templates for every text type
- Examples you can copy and adapt
- Guidelines for maintaining consistency
- Integration points with game systems
- Scalable from single entries to full questlines

## 📚 SECTION 1: LORE & WORLD-BUILDING SYSTEM

### 1.1 Pre-Blast Municipal Collapse (Days 1-57)

**Document Types Available:**
- Curfew orders and emergency proclamations
- Ration stamp rules and distribution schedules
- Evacuation cancellation notices
- Utility shutoff warnings (water, power, gas)
- Municipal service bulletins
- News clippings and editorials
- Internal memos and employee handbooks
- School notices and community announcements
- Business closure notices
- Traffic and transportation updates

**Template Structure:**
```
[Document Type] - [Department/Location] - [Date Range]

[Official Header/Footer with stamps, logos, signatures]

[Main Content - progressively more desperate as days advance]

[Handwritten additions, coffee stains, post-it notes]

[System Integration Notes: Which game systems this affects]
```

**Example: Curfew Order #2024-03-17-B**
```
CITY OF NEW HAVEN
EMERGENCY MANAGEMENT AGENCY

CURFEW ORDER #2024-03-17-B
EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY

TO: All City Residents
FROM: Director Eleanor Voss, Civil Defense Authority

Effective 8:00 PM tonight, a 24-hour curfew is in effect for all residents of New Haven.
Violators will be subject to immediate arrest and quarantine protocols.

RATION DISTRIBUTION SCHEDULE:
- Zone A (Downtown Core): 06:00-08:00 AM
- Zone B (Residential Districts): 08:00-10:00 AM
- Zone C (Industrial/Commercial): 10:00 AM-12:00 PM
- Zone D (Outlying Areas): 12:00-02:00 PM

ALL PUBLIC TRANSPORTATION SUSPENDED
ALL NON-ESSENTIAL BUSINESSES CLOSED
SCHOOLS AND CHILDCARE FACILITIES SUSPENDED

"Stay indoors. Stay safe. The government is working around the clock to resolve this situation."

STAMP: EMERGENCY MANAGEMENT AGENCY
SIGNATURE: ________________
DATE: March 17, 2024

[Handwritten in blue ink in margin: "Heard on scanner - bridges rigged. Don't trust the buses. Also, the water tastes funny today."]
[Coffee stain on bottom right corner]
```

**System Integration:**
- Affects NPC schedules and patrol patterns
- Determines which zones are accessible during curfew
- Influences ration distribution mechanics
- Creates tension between "official" narrative and survivor rumors
- Triggers event: Increased police/military presence
- Affects: Morale (fear of authority), Stealth (curfew violators)

**Variations by Day:**
- **Day 1-7:** Normal bureaucratic language, some concern but not panic
- **Day 8-14:** More urgent, mentions "escalating tensions"
- **Day 15-21:** Directives become orders, mentions "mandatory compliance"
- **Day 22-28:** Desperate language, mentions "resource constraints"
- **Day 29-35:** Panicked tone, mentions "civil unrest"
- **Day 36-42:** Collapse language, mentions "temporary measures"
- **Day 43-49:** Futile tone, mentions "final warnings"
- **Day 50-57:** Complete breakdown, mentions "abandon all hope"

### 1.2 The Detonation Event Log (Days 58-63)

**Document Types Available:**
- Emergency broadcast transcripts
- Military communications and orders
- Civil defense system recordings
- Survivor first-person accounts
- Automated system logs
- News network transcripts
- Traffic camera stills with timestamps
- Weather service alerts
- Seismic monitoring data

**Template Structure:**
```
[Time Stamp] - [Source/Location] - [Event Type]

[Official Transcript]
[Survivor Account]
[System Alert]
[Post-Event Additions]

[Background noise, static, or distortion level]
```

**Example: Military Communication - NW-2024-058-001**
```
[03:47:12] NEW HAVEN MILITARY COMMAND CENTER - VOICE LOG
Classification: TOP SECRET // EYES ONLY

"This is General Hastings. We have multiple high-yield warheads inbound.
Repeat: multiple high-yield warheads. Civil defense systems activating.
All personnel report to nearest shelter immediately.

[Static - 47 seconds - distant screams in background]

"Civilian infrastructure compromised. Power grid down. Communications degraded.
Emergency broadcast system activating. All stations switch to Protocol ALPHA.

[Static - 12 seconds - mechanical beeping starts]

"This is NOT a drill. I repeat, this is NOT a drill. Impact in T-minus 15 minutes.
All personnel, this is your final warning. Shelter protocols engaged.

[End transmission - signal lost]

[Handwritten on back in pencil: "Saw the flash. Heard nothing. Then the heat hit like a goddamn oven door. The sky was white. Not gray. White. Like the sun decided to come down and touch the earth."]
```

**System Integration:**
- Determines initial shelter state and damage
- Sets radiation levels in different zones
- Creates specific structural damage patterns
- Influences which NPCs survive or die
- Triggers: Radiation storm events, structural collapse timers
- Affects: Player starting location, initial inventory, NPC availability

**Key Details to Include:**
- **Flash:** Description of the light (white, blue-white, duration)
- **Heat Pulse:** How far it traveled, what it did to surfaces
- **Blast Wave:** Direction, force, structural damage
- **EMP:** Electronics failure, communication blackout
- **Fallout:** Immediate radiation levels, wind direction
- **Casualties:** Estimated numbers, specific locations mentioned
- **Aftermath:** Fires, flooding, structural collapse

### 1.3 Post-Detonation Diegetic Radio Logs (Days 64+)

**Document Types Available:**
- Automated beacon loops
- Military distress calls
- Civilian broadcast attempts
- Emergency frequency scans
- Jammed transmissions
- Weather reports
- Time/date stamps
- Automated health and welfare checks
- Repeating distress signals

**Template Structure:**
```
[Frequency] - [Time Stamp] - [Signal Quality %]

[Automated Message]
[Human Transmission]
[Background Noise Profile]
[Interference Pattern]

[Loop repetition interval]
```

**Example: Automated Beacon - Frequency 156.800 MHz**
```
FREQUENCY 156.800 MHz - 08:47:12 - SIGNAL: WEAK (78%)
Loop interval: 15 minutes

"This is Emergency Broadcast System Station NW-9, New Haven Civil Defense.
Automated health and welfare check. Please respond if you can copy.

[Static - 3 seconds - distant voices murmuring]

All personnel report to designated shelters. Radiation levels elevated but not immediately fatal.
Shelter locations remain valid. Water and food supplies adequate for 30 days.

[Static - 8 seconds - mechanical whirring]

Repeat: This is not a test. Civil authorities are coordinating relief efforts.
Aid convoys en route from western regions. Estimated arrival: 7-10 days.

[End of transmission]

[Handwritten on back in red pen: "They're lying. The convoys never came. I've been listening for weeks. Only static and this damn repeating message."]
```

**System Integration:**
- Provides world state updates
- Can trigger random events (raider attacks, radiation storms)
- Gives hints about safe routes and locations
- Creates atmosphere through static and interference
- Affects: Player knowledge, NPC behavior, quest availability

**Transmission Types:**
1. **Automated Loops** (repeating, no human): Beacons, health checks, warnings
2. **Human Transmissions** (desperate, emotional): Survivor calls, pleas for help
3. **Military Traffic** (coded, urgent): Orders, situation reports, encrypted
4. **Civilian Broadcasts** (chaotic, uncoordinated): News, rumors, warnings
5. **Jammed Signals** (unintelligible): Interference, enemy jamming, equipment failure
6. **Distress Signals** (repeating patterns): SOS, automated SOS, repeating coordinates

### 1.4 Scavenger Graffiti & Marker Codes

**Types of Graffiti:**
- **Danger Warnings:** Radiation, raiders, structural hazards
- **Resource Markers:** Water, food, medical supplies, safe routes
- **Territory Claims:** Gang markings, faction boundaries
- **Navigation Aids:** Arrows, distances, warnings
- **Personal Messages:** \"Jake was here\", \"Don't trust the well\"
- **Counting Systems:** How many people passed, days since last raid
- **Codes & Ciphers:** Simple substitution, number codes, symbol systems

**Template Structure:**
```
[Location] - [Type of Graffiti] - [Condition]

[Visual Description]
[Interpretation]
[Possible Meaning]
[System Integration]
```

**Example: Radiation Warning - Main Street Alley**
```
NEW HAVEN MAIN STREET ALLEY - RADIATION WARNING - FADED BUT LEGIBLE

Visual Description:
A large yellow radiation symbol (circle with three blades) spray-painted on the brick wall.
Underneath, in smaller letters: "HOT ZONE - 50 FEET IN"
Scrawled underneath in pencil: "Unless you like your insides cooked"

Interpretation:
The symbol is standard radiation warning, but the context suggests it's been there since the blast.
The fading indicates it's been there for weeks.
The pencil addition is likely from a survivor who encountered the hazard.

Possible Meaning:
- Actual radiation hotspot 50 feet into the alley
- Warning from someone who tested the area
- Deliberate trap or warning from raiders
- Misleading - the radiation is actually elsewhere

System Integration:
- If player enters the alley: Radiation exposure event
- Can trigger: \"Radiation Poisoning\" affliction
- Requires: Geiger counter or dosimeter to detect
- Alternative: Can be ignored at player's risk
- Lore: Adds to player's understanding of the city's layout
```

**Common Marker Codes:**

**Danger Markers:**
- ⚠️ + skull = Radiation hazard
- ⚠️ + knife = Raider territory
- ⚠️ + X = Trap or ambush
- ⚠️ + ⚡ = Electrical hazard
- ⚠️ + 💀 = Dead body nearby

**Resource Markers:**
- 💧 = Water source
- 🍎 = Food cache
- ⚕️ = Medical supplies
- 🔋 = Power source/battery
- 📦 = Lootable container
- 🚪 = Safe exit/entrance

**Navigation Markers:**
- → 500 = 500 feet that direction
- ⏰ 3d = 3 days ago
- 👥 4 = 4 people passed this way
- 🔥 = Fire hazard, avoid
- 🏠 = Home base direction

**Faction Markers:**
- 🌀 = Raiders (spiral)
- ⚔️ = Militia (sword)
- 🕊️ = Peacekeepers (dove)
- 💀 = Death Cult (skull)
- 📿 = Knowledge Keepers (book)

```

Emotional Impact:
- For Daniel: Confirms Jake's fate, survivor's guilt
- For Player: Humanizes victims, makes disaster personal
- For World: Shows relationships mattered even in end
- For Game: Creates emotional investment

Gameplay Integration:
- NPC Fate: Confirms Jake died on way from store
- Cat Mechanic: Can rescue or ignore the cat
- Loot: Supplies mentioned are available in store
- Dialogue: Daniel references this note
- Moral Choice: Honor Jake's memory or ignore it

### 2.2 Example Location: New Haven Public Library - Children's Section

**Location ID:** loc_childrens_library
**Spawn Conditions:** Day 15-120 (always accessible after Day 15)
**Contamination Status:** Moderate radiation (2.3 mSv/hr) - safe for short visits
**Accessibility:** Clear entrance, but second floor collapsed
**NPC Presence:** None (last survivor left 3 weeks ago)
**Structural Integrity:** Partially collapsed second floor, ground floor intact

---

### BASE DESCRIPTION (Pre-Blast)

The New Haven Public Library's Children's Section was a vibrant, colorful space designed to inspire young minds. Shelves of picture books lined the walls, interspersed with cozy reading nooks featuring bean bag chairs and soft lighting. A large mural of storybook characters adorned the far wall, and a fish tank bubbled gently in the corner. Storytime sessions were held every Saturday morning, drawing families from across the neighborhood.

**Layout:** 40' x 60' open space with 8 bookshelves, 3 reading nooks, circulation desk, storytime stage
**Purpose:** Children's education and entertainment
**Occupants:** Librarian Ms. Henderson, 12-25 children per storytime, parents waiting
**Notable Features:** Mural wall, fish tank (goldfish named Bubbles), storytime stage with puppet theater

---

### POST-BLAST DESCRIPTION (Immediate Aftermath)

The blast shattered windows and sent books flying like shrapnel. The fish tank exploded, coating everything in water and glass shards. The mural wall was pockmarked with radiation burns. The storytime stage collapsed inward. Most books were incinerated in the initial heat pulse, but some survived in protected corners.

**Damage Assessment:**
- North wall: Windows blown out, books scattered
- East wall: Mural partially burned away
- South wall: Circulation desk overturned
- West wall: Reading nooks collapsed
- Ceiling: Partial collapse on second floor
- Fish tank: Exploded, water damage everywhere

**Survivor Response:**
- Day 1: Librarian Ms. Henderson barricaded herself in the storage room
- Day 3: She attempted to salvage books, but radiation sickness forced her to leave
- Day 7: A group of looters took what remained of the non-fiction section
- Day 14: Radiation levels spiked, making the area too dangerous

**Looting Evidence:**
- Fiction section: Completely stripped
- Non-fiction: Partially looted, books with "HISTORY" or "SCIENCE" stamps remain
- Children's books: Some survived in plastic bins
- Administrative files: Water-damaged but legible

**Hazard Introduction:**
- Broken glass everywhere (cuts risk)
- Water + electricity = electrocution risk in some areas
- Radiation hotspot near the exploded fish tank
- Collapsing ceiling hazards on second floor

---

### CURRENT STATE (Discovered by Player)

The Children's Section is eerily quiet, the silence broken only by the occasional creak of settling debris. The air smells of wet paper and something metallic underneath. The mural on the east wall is still visible in patches, the colors faded but the shapes recognizable. The fish tank area is a glistening mess of broken glass and dried water stains.

**Structural Integrity:** Partially collapsed second floor, ground floor mostly intact
**Environmental Hazards:**
- Radiation: 2.3 mSv/hr near fish tank (safe for short visits)
- Broken glass: Cuts risk on floor
- Water damage: Some electrical hazards remain
- Ceiling: Weak spots over storytime stage

**Loot Status:**
- Fiction books: Gone
- Children's books: Some remain in plastic bins (mostly damaged)
- Non-fiction: 12 books intact, 8 water-damaged
- Administrative files: 3 folders legible
- Librarian's personal items: In storage room

**NPC Activity:** None - last survivor left 3 weeks ago, taking only a few books and a flashlight

**Sensory Details:**
- **Visual:** Faded mural characters watching you, water stains forming patterns, books scattered like fallen leaves
- **Auditory:** Distant creaking of building, your own breathing, occasional drip of water
- **Olfactory:** Wet paper, old dust, faint metallic tang from radiation
- **Tactile:** Dusty surfaces, sticky spots where water once was, sharp glass underfoot in some areas
- **Gustatory:** Dust coating your tongue, metallic taste from radiation exposure

**Gameplay Consequences:**
- Can search bookshelves for non-fiction books (provides knowledge/skills)
- Can salvage plastic bins for waterproofing material
- Can find librarian's personal journal in storage room
- Radiation exposure risk if staying too long
- Can barricade entrance for shelter use

---
