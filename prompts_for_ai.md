# AI Art Prompts for ASHFALL (Working Title)

This document contains detailed prompts for generating game assets for a 2D atomic-survival game. The desired art style is a blend of gritty realism and stylized 2D art, inspired by games like *This War of Mine* and *Darkest Dungeon*, but with a unique, cold, and desaturated color palette. The tone is exhausted, human, and restrained.

---

### General Art Style Guidelines

-   **Palette:** Muted, desaturated colors. Grays, cold blues, dusty browns, and occasional stark reds for emphasis (e.g., warnings, blood).
-   **Lighting:** High contrast, dramatic lighting (chiaroscuro). Use of strong shadows to create a sense of dread and confinement.
-   **Texture:** Gritty, textured brushes. Everything should look worn, dirty, and decaying.
-   **Perspective:** 2.5D perspective for environments and props. Profile or 3/4 view for characters.

### 1. Adobe Firefly Prompts (Flux 2 Pro)

*Firefly excels at photorealistic and detailed painterly styles. Use it for high-fidelity concept art, key art, and detailed environment pieces.*

#### **Environment Concept Art:**

-   "Concept art of a dilapidated fallout shelter interior, 2D painterly style. A single bare lightbulb illuminates a room with makeshift beds, a cluttered workbench with old electronics, and water stains on concrete walls. The mood is claustrophobic and somber. Desaturated, cold color palette. High detail, gritty textures."
-   "Digital painting of a ruined city street during a nuclear winter. Snow and ash fall from a grey sky, covering abandoned cars and crumbling buildings. A lone figure in a heavy coat and gas mask walks down the empty street. Style of a graphic novel, muted colors, high contrast lighting."
-   "Key art for a survival game. A survivor's face in profile, illuminated by the faint green glow of a Geiger counter. Their expression is grim and determined. The background is a dark, indistinct bunker interior. Photorealistic detail, dramatic chiaroscuro lighting, shallow depth of field."

#### **UI Elements & Icons:**

-   "A set of 12 flat, minimalist UI icons for a survival game, on a dark background. Icons for: hunger (empty bowl), thirst (cracked glass), radiation (atomic trefoil), health (heart with a crack), morale (half-frowning face), warmth (fading flame). Clean lines, single color (off-white), slightly distressed texture."
-   "UI design for a vintage radio tuner interface in a post-apocalyptic game. A circular tuning dial with frequency numbers, a signal strength meter (a needle gauge), and a grimy, scratched metal texture. The interface is lit by a faint, flickering green light. Photorealistic, high detail."
