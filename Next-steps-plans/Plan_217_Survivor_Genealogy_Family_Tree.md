### Deterministic day and event order

Use `_simDay` or the campaign day coordinator argument, never a literal UI day or wall-clock clock. ### Player surface and accessibility

Show current state, legal action, cost and consequence, and precise blocker in the same view. Preserve keyboard/controller back and focus after an action or refresh.
